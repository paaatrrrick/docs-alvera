## Overview

The mango is a stone fruit produced by the tropical tree *Mangifera indica*. Mangoes originated in South Asia, where people have cultivated them for more than 4,000 years. The mango is the national fruit of India, Pakistan, and the Philippines.

## Popular varieties

| Variety   | Origin      | Flavor                     |
| --------- | ----------- | -------------------------- |
| Alphonso  | India       | Rich, creamy, and aromatic |
| Ataulfo   | Mexico      | Sweet, buttery, fiberless  |
| Tommy Atkins | United States | Mild, slightly fibrous |
| Kent      | United States | Sweet and juicy          |
| Carabao   | Philippines | Very sweet, tangy finish   |

## Nutrition

One cup of sliced mango contains about 100 calories. Mangoes are rich in vitamin C, vitamin A, and folate.

## Culinary uses

- **Beverages**: mango lassi and smoothies
- **Desserts**: mango sticky rice and sorbet
- **Savory dishes**: salsas and chutneys
- **Raw**: sliced or cubed as a snack
