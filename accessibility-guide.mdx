Accessible documentation reaches a wider audience, ranks better in search, and reflects well on your product. This guide covers the essentials — alt text, color contrast, semantic structure, and keyboard navigation — so every reader can find and use your content, regardless of how they access it.

## Images and alt text

Every image should have alt text that describes it. Screen readers read this out, so don't leave it empty. See [the image guide](/essentials/images) for details.

## Color and contrast

Make sure your text has enough contrast against the background. Low contrast is hard to read, especially for people with low vision. We check this automatically and warn you.

## Headings

Use headings in order — don't skip from h2 to h4. This helps everyone navigate, not just screen reader users. The [markdown guide](/essentials/markdown) covers heading structure.

For a full checklist, read [the WCAG overview](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/).
