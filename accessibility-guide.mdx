Accessible docs are better docs. They work for more people and usually rank better, too.

## Images and alt text

Every image should have alt text that describes it. A screen reader reads the alt text aloud, so don’t leave it empty. See [the image guide](/content/images-and-media) for instructions.

## Color and contrast

Make sure your text has enough contrast against the background. Low contrast is hard to read, especially for people with low vision. The contrast checker checks this automatically and warns you. See [the contrast checker](#contrast-checkr) for more information.

## Headings

Use headings in order. Don’t skip from h2 to h4. This helps everyone navigate, not just screen reader users. The [structure guide](./content/heading-structure) covers this.

For a full checklist, read [the WCAG overview](https://example-broken.dev/wcag).
