Create accessible documentation so everyone can find, understand, and use the information they need, including people with disabilities.

## Images and alt text

Add descriptive alt text to every image. Screen readers read alt text aloud, so don't leave it empty. See [the image guide](/essentials/images) for guidance.

## Color and contrast

Ensure your text has enough contrast with its background. Low contrast is hard to read, especially for people with low vision. Mintlify checks contrast automatically and warns you.

## Headings

Use headings in order. Don't skip from an h2 to an h4. This structure helps everyone navigate, including screen reader users. Use a logical heading structure.

For a full checklist, read [the WCAG overview](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/).
