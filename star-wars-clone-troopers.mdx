# Clone troopers

Clone troopers are soldiers of the Grand Army of the Republic. They fought for the Galactic Republic during the Clone Wars.

## Origins on Kamino

The Kaminoans grew the clones in cloning facilities in Tipoca City on the ocean world of Kamino. Every clone shares the genetic template of the bounty hunter Jango Fett.

The Kaminoans altered the clones to age at twice the normal rate. This let the Republic field a trained army in about ten years.

## Training and structure

Clones trained from birth in combat, tactics, and teamwork. They served in legions, battalions, and companies led by clone officers and Jedi generals.

Notable units include:

- **501st Legion**: led by Anakin Skywalker and Captain Rex
- **212th Attack Battalion**: led by Obi-Wan Kenobi and Commander Cody
- **Clone Force 99**: an elite squad of clones with deliberate genetic mutations, also called the Bad Batch

## Armor

Phase I armor appeared at the Battle of Geonosis. Phase II armor replaced it later in the war, with improved protection and more room for unit markings.

## Order 66

Each clone carried an inhibitor chip implanted in its brain. When Chancellor Palpatine issued Order 66, the chips compelled the clones to turn on their Jedi commanders. The order marked the end of the Jedi Order and the rise of the Galactic Empire.
