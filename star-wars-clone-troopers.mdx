## Overview

Clone troopers are the genetically engineered soldiers of the Grand Army of the Republic. You meet them first in *Attack of the Clones*, and they form the backbone of Republic forces throughout the Clone Wars and the transition to the early Galactic Empire.

## Origins

The clones are grown on Kamino, a stormy ocean world in the Wild Space region. Every trooper shares the genetic template of the bounty hunter Jango Fett, with two key modifications:

- Accelerated aging, so clones reach combat readiness in roughly ten standard years.
- Behavioral conditioning designed to make them obedient soldiers.

Each clone also carries an inhibitor chip that later enforces Order 66.

## Unit structure

The Grand Army organizes clones into a tiered hierarchy:

| Unit | Approximate size | Typical command |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Squad | 9 troopers | Sergeant |
| Platoon | 36 troopers | Lieutenant |
| Company | 144 troopers | Captain |
| Battalion | 576 troopers | Major or Commander |
| Regiment | 2,304 troopers | Commander |
| Legion | 9,216 troopers | Senior Commander |
| Corps | 36,864 troopers | Marshal Commander and Jedi General |

## Specialist roles

Clones are trained into a range of specialist roles that support standard infantry:

- **ARC troopers**: Advanced Recon Commandos trained for independent operations.
- **Clone commandos**: Small squads for infiltration and sabotage, such as Delta Squad.
- **Pilots**: Trained for starfighters, gunships, and heavy transports.
- **Medics**: Field medical specialists attached to combat units.
- **Shock troopers**: The elite Coruscant Guard responsible for security in the capital.

## Order 66 and the transition to stormtroopers

At the end of the Clone Wars, Chancellor Palpatine issues Order 66, activating the inhibitor chips and directing the clones to execute their Jedi commanders. After the Empire is declared, the clone army is gradually phased out in favor of recruited human stormtroopers, a shift explored in *The Bad Batch*.
