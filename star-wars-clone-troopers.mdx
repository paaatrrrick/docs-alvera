## Overview

Clone troopers were the genetically engineered soldiers who made up the Grand Army of the Republic during the Clone Wars. Bred on the ocean planet Kamino from the template of the bounty hunter Jango Fett, they served under Jedi generals across thousands of worlds before Order 66 turned them against the Jedi.

## Origins on Kamino

The Kaminoan cloners produced the army in secret, commissioned years before the war on the authority of the long-dead Jedi Master Sifo-Dyas. Each clone shared Jango Fett's genetic template but was engineered with two key modifications:

- Accelerated growth, reaching combat maturity in roughly ten years.
- Heightened obedience to command authority.

Boba Fett, raised by Jango as an unaltered clone, is the notable exception to both modifications.

## Ranks and units

The Grand Army organized clones into a nested structure that mirrored Republic military tradition:

- **Squad** — 9 troopers led by a sergeant.
- **Platoon** — 4 squads led by a lieutenant.
- **Company** — 4 platoons led by a captain.
- **Battalion** — 4 companies led by a major or commander.
- **Regiment** — 4 battalions.
- **Legion** — 4 regiments, typically assigned to a Jedi General.

Clone commanders such as Cody, Rex, Bly, and Wolffe led legions in coordination with their Jedi counterparts.

## Armor and equipment

Clones wore Phase I armor at the start of the Clone Wars, later replaced by the more flexible Phase II design. Standard equipment included:

- DC-15A blaster rifle or DC-15S blaster carbine.
- Utility belt with thermal detonators and rations.
- Sealed helmet with heads-up display and comlink.

Specialist troopers wore modified kits for their roles, such as ARF scout armor, ARC trooper pauldrons and kamas, or the sealed cold-weather gear of the 501st on Rhen Var.

## Order 66

Every clone carried an inhibitor chip implanted at creation. When Chancellor Palpatine issued Order 66, the chips activated and compelled the troopers to execute their Jedi generals. A small number of clones, including Rex and the members of Clone Force 99, removed their chips and resisted the order.

## Transition to stormtroopers

After the Republic became the Galactic Empire, the clone army was gradually replaced. Aging clones served as the first Imperial stormtroopers, but their accelerated aging shortened their service lives. The Empire eventually shifted to human recruits and conscripts, ending large-scale cloning of Jango Fett's template.
