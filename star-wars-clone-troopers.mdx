## Overview

Clone troopers were the genetically engineered soldiers who formed the Grand Army of the Republic during the Clone Wars. Grown on the ocean world of Kamino from the template of the bounty hunter Jango Fett, they were bred for accelerated growth, unwavering discipline, and total loyalty to their commanders.

## Origins on Kamino

The clone army was secretly commissioned a decade before the Clone Wars began. Cloners on Kamino produced troopers at twice the natural growth rate and conditioned them from birth for combat.

- **Template**: Jango Fett, selected for his combat record and independence.
- **Growth rate**: Twice that of a standard human, reaching combat readiness in about ten years.
- **Conditioning**: Structured education, flash training, and battle simulations.
- **Inhibitor chip**: A biochip implanted in every clone that enforced compliance with Order 66.

## Ranks and command structure

Clone troopers followed a military hierarchy that ran alongside their Jedi generals.

| Rank | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Trooper | Rank-and-file soldier assigned to a squad |
| Sergeant | Leader of a squad of nine troopers |
| Lieutenant | Officer commanding a platoon |
| Captain | Officer commanding a company |
| Commander | Senior officer paired with a Jedi general |
| Marshal Commander | Regional commander overseeing multiple legions |

## Specialist units

The Grand Army fielded specialized clones trained for missions beyond standard infantry work.

- **ARC troopers**: Advanced Recon Commandos trained to operate without a Jedi handler.
- **Clone commandos**: Four-clone squads used for covert strikes and sabotage.
- **Clone pilots**: Trained to fly starfighters, gunships, and dropships.
- **Clone medics**: Field surgeons attached to combat units.
- **Shock troopers**: The Coruscant Guard, tasked with security in the capital.
- **501st Legion**: Anakin Skywalker's frontline unit, known as the "Vode An."

## Order 66

Order 66 was a contingency embedded in every clone through the inhibitor chip. When Chancellor Palpatine issued the command, clones across the galaxy turned on their Jedi generals, ending the Jedi Order as a galactic institution and paving the way for the Galactic Empire.

## Legacy

After the Clone Wars, the Empire gradually replaced clone troopers with conscripted stormtroopers, citing cost and the clones' accelerated aging. A few clones, including Rex, removed their chips and lived on to fight against the Empire alongside the Rebellion.
