# Clone troopers

Clone troopers are soldiers grown on the ocean world of Kamino. Each clone shares the genetic template of the bounty hunter Jango Fett. Together they formed the Grand Army of the Republic during the Clone Wars.

## Origins

The Kaminoans grew the clones in secret for the Galactic Republic. Growth acceleration let each clone reach combat age in about ten years.

## Training

Clones trained from birth in tactics, marksmanship, and teamwork. Many learned from veteran instructors such as Mandalorian mercenaries.

## Notable units

- **501st Legion**: An elite unit that served under Anakin Skywalker.
- **212th Attack Battalion**: A unit led by Obi-Wan Kenobi and Commander Cody.
- **Clone Force 99**: A squad of genetically altered clones known as the Bad Batch.

## Order 66

Each clone carried an inhibitor chip in its brain. When Chancellor Palpatine issued Order 66, the chips compelled the clones to turn on their Jedi generals. This order marked the end of the Jedi Order and the rise of the Galactic Empire.
