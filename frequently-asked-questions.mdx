We receive a lot of questions, so we wrote down the most common ones here.

**Q: How much does it cost?**

Pricing depends on your plan. See the [billing overview](/billing-overview) for the full breakdown. There is also a free tier.

**Q: Can I use my own domain?**

Yes, you can. Custom domains are supported on all paid plans. Follow the setup walkthrough in your dashboard and it should work.

**Q: Where do I get support?**

The fastest way is to email us. We try to respond within a day, but sometimes it takes longer.

**Q: Is there an API?**

Of course! Read [the full API reference](/api-reference/introduction) to get started.
