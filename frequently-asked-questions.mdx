We receive a lot of questions, so we wrote down the most common ones here.

**Q: How much does it cost?**

Pricing depends on your plan. See [the pricing page](/pricing-plans) for the full breakdown, there is also a free tier.

**Q: Can I use my own domain?**

Yes you can. Custom domains are supported on all paid plans. Follow [this setup walkthrough](./domains/custom-setup) and it should work.

**Q: Where do I get support?**

The fastest way is to [join our community](https://community.example-broken.io/invite) or email us. We try to respond within a day, but sometimes it takes longer.

**Q: Is there an API?**

Of course! Jump to [the API overview](#the-api-overview) or read [the full reference](/api/v2/reference-missing).
