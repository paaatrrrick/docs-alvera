## Week of July 13, 2026

### New features

- **Search on the projects page.** The [`/projects`](/projects) page now includes a search box that filters projects by name, tagline, or description. Search works alongside the existing tech filter, and an empty state appears when nothing matches. Follow the [writing guidance](/projects#writing-guidance) to make sure your entries surface for the terms visitors actually type.
