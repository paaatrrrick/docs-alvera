---
title: Tea history overview
---

People have enjoyed tea for thousands of years. Historians across many cultures and centuries have told and retold its long and winding story.

## Ancient origins in China

According to legend, the emperor Shen Nong discovered tea around 2737 BCE when the wind blew leaves into a pot of boiling water that his servants had prepared. People first consumed tea mainly as a medicine. Only much later did the wider population adopt it as an everyday beverage.

## Spread to Japan and Korea

Buddhist monks who had studied in China brought tea seeds to Japan. Zen practices those monks learned heavily influenced the tea ceremony that eventually developed there. Korea also received tea through similar religious and trade connections.

## Arrival in Europe

Dutch and Portuguese traders introduced tea to Europe in the early 1600s. Catherine of Braganza popularized it among the wealthy classes of England, where it quickly became fashionable. By the 1800s, tea had become a staple of daily life, and afternoon tea was a beloved tradition.
