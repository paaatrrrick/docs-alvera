---
title: Tea history overview
description: An overview of tea history, from the legend of Shen Nong in ancient China to the Japanese tea ceremony and afternoon tea in England.
---

Tea is a drink that has been enjoyed by people for thousands of years, and its long and winding story is one that has been told and retold by many historians across many different cultures and centuries.

## Ancient origins in China

According to legend, tea was discovered by the emperor Shen Nong around 2737 BCE, when leaves were blown by the wind into a pot of boiling water that had been prepared by his servants. Tea was consumed mainly as a medicine at first, and it was only much later that it was adopted as an everyday beverage by the wider population.

## Spread to Japan and Korea

Tea seeds were brought to Japan by Buddhist monks who had been studying in China, and the tea ceremony that was eventually developed there was influenced heavily by Zen practices that had been learned by those monks. Korea also received tea through similar religious and trade connections.

## Arrival in Europe

Tea was introduced to Europe in the early 1600s by Dutch and Portuguese traders, and it was quickly made fashionable among the wealthy classes of England after it was popularized by Catherine of Braganza. By the 1800s, tea had become a staple of daily life, and afternoon tea was established as a beloved tradition.
