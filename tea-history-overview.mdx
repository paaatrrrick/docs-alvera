title: Tea history overview
description: "How tea began as a medicine in ancient China, shaped the tea ceremony in Japan and Korea, and became a staple of daily life in Europe by the 1800s."
---

People have enjoyed tea for thousands of years. Many historians across many different cultures and centuries have told and retold its long and winding story.

## Ancient origins in China

According to legend, the emperor Shen Nong discovered tea around 2737 BCE. The wind blew leaves into a pot of boiling water that his servants had prepared. People first consumed tea mainly as a medicine. Only much later did the wider population adopt it as an everyday beverage.

## Spread to Japan and Korea

Buddhist monks who had studied in China brought tea seeds to Japan. Those monks had learned Zen practices, which heavily influenced the tea ceremony that eventually developed there. Korea also received tea through similar religious and trade connections.

## Arrival in Europe

Dutch and Portuguese traders introduced tea to Europe in the early 1600s. Catherine of Braganza popularized tea and quickly made it fashionable among the wealthy classes of England. By the 1800s, tea had become a staple of daily life, and afternoon tea had become a beloved tradition.
