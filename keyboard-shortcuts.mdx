Keyboard shortcuts let you move through the documentation without touching your mouse. Use them to focus search, jump to key pages, and step through results from anywhere on the site—every shortcut below works on every page.

## Shortcuts

- Press `/` to focus the search bar.
- Press `g` then `h` to go to the home page.
- Use the arrow keys to move between search results.
- Press `Esc` to close search.
