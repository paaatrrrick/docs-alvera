# Ewoks

Ewoks are a species of small, furry beings native to the forest moon of Endor. They stand about one meter tall. Despite their size, they are skilled hunters, builders, and fighters.

## Appearance

Ewoks are covered in thick fur that ranges from black and brown to tan and white. Many wear leather hoods decorated with feathers, bones, or teeth that mark their rank or tribe.

## Tree villages

Ewoks live in villages built high in the giant trees of Endor's forests. Rope bridges and walkways connect the huts, which keeps the villages safe from predators on the forest floor.

## Society

Ewok tribes are led by a chief and a council of elders. A shaman guides the tribe's spiritual life and interprets omens.

- **Chief Chirpa** led Bright Tree Village during the Battle of Endor.
- **Logray** served as the village shaman.
- **Wicket W. Warrick** befriended Princess Leia and helped bring the Ewoks into the fight.

## Technology and tactics

Ewoks rely on primitive tools made from wood, stone, and vines. They use spears, slings, bows, gliders, and log traps.

## Battle of Endor

During the Battle of Endor, the Ewoks allied with the Rebel strike team. They ambushed Imperial forces with rockslides, swinging logs, and snares. Their help allowed the Rebels to destroy the shield generator protecting the second Death Star.

## Language

Ewoks speak Ewokese, a language of chirps, squeaks, and guttural sounds. C-3PO was able to translate it and tell the tribe the story of the Rebellion.
