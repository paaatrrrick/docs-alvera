# Ewoks

Ewoks are a small, furry species native to the forest moon of Endor. They stand about one meter tall and live in tribes high in the moon's giant trees.

## Bright Tree Village

The Ewoks of Bright Tree Village build their homes on wooden platforms connected by rope bridges and walkways. Chief Chirpa leads the tribe, and the shaman Logray advises him on spiritual matters.

## Primitive technology

Ewoks rely on simple tools such as spears, slings, bows, gliders, and log traps. They know the forest intimately and use that knowledge to ambush far better-equipped enemies.

## Battle of Endor

During the Battle of Endor, the Ewoks allied with the Rebel Alliance strike team led by Han Solo and Princess Leia. They helped destroy the shield generator protecting the second Death Star by overwhelming Imperial stormtroopers and AT-ST walkers.

## Notable Ewoks

- **Wicket W. Warrick**: A young scout who befriends Princess Leia and brings her to his village.
- **Chief Chirpa**: The elder leader of Bright Tree Village.
- **Logray**: The tribe's shaman and medicine man.
- **Teebo**: A warrior and apprentice shaman who fights alongside the Rebels.
