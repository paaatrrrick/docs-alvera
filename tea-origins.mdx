Tea is a very old drink that many people have enjoyed for thousands of years. This page covers a few highlights from its long history.

## Ancient beginnings in China

Legend says that the emperor Shen Nong discovered tea around 2737 BCE. The wind blew leaves from a nearby bush into a pot of water that his servants were boiling. He found the resulting drink refreshing and pleasant.

## Spread across Asia

Buddhist monks carried tea to Japan and Korea. Over many centuries, local customs, religious practices, and the preferences of the ruling classes shaped how those cultures adopted the drink.

## Arrival in Europe

Portuguese and Dutch traders brought tea to Europe in the 1600s. Catherine of Braganza popularized tea in England and made it fashionable. Soon, people of all social classes throughout the country were drinking it.
