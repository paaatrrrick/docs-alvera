People have brewed tea for nearly 5,000 years. This page traces how tea began in ancient China, spread across Asia with Buddhist monks, and reached Europe through Portuguese and Dutch traders.

## Ancient beginnings in China

According to legend, the emperor Shen Nong discovered tea around 2737 BCE. His servants were boiling a pot of water when the wind blew leaves from a nearby bush into it. Shen Nong tasted the resulting drink and found it refreshing and pleasant.

## Spread across Asia

Buddhist monks carried tea to Japan and Korea. Over many centuries, local customs, religious practices, and the preferences of the ruling classes shaped how each culture adopted the drink.

## Arrival in Europe

Portuguese and Dutch traders brought tea to Europe in the 1600s. Catherine of Braganza popularized tea in England and made it fashionable. People of all social classes throughout the country were soon drinking it.
