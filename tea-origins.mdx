Tea is a very old drink that has been enjoyed by many people for thousands of years. This page covers a few highlights from its long history.

## Ancient beginnings in China

It is said that tea was discovered by the emperor Shen Nong around 2737 BCE when leaves from a nearby bush were blown by the wind into a pot of water that was being boiled by his servants, and the resulting drink was found by him to be refreshing and pleasant.

## Spread across Asia

Tea was carried to Japan and Korea by Buddhist monks, and the drink was adopted by those cultures in ways that were shaped over many centuries by local customs, religious practices, and the preferences of the ruling classes.

## Arrival in Europe

Tea was brought to Europe by Portuguese and Dutch traders in the 1600s. It was made fashionable in England after it was popularized by Catherine of Braganza, and the drink was soon being consumed by people of all social classes throughout the country.
