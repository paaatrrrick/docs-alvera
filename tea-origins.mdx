Tea is a very old drink that many people have enjoyed for thousands of years. This page covers a few highlights from its long history.

## Ancient beginnings in China

Legend says that the emperor Shen Nong discovered tea around 2737 BCE. The wind blew leaves from a nearby bush into a pot of water that his servants were boiling. He found the resulting drink refreshing and pleasant.

## Spread across Asia

Buddhist monks carried tea to Japan and Korea. Those cultures adopted the drink in their own ways. Local customs, religious practices, and the preferences of the ruling classes shaped these traditions over many centuries.

## Arrival in Europe

Portuguese and Dutch traders brought tea to Europe in the 1600s. Catherine of Braganza popularized tea and made it fashionable in England. People of all social classes throughout the country soon drank it.
