---
title: Tea origins
---

Tea is a very old drink that people have enjoyed for thousands of years. This page covers a few highlights from its long history.

## Ancient beginnings in China

According to legend, the emperor Shen Nong discovered tea around 2737 BCE. His servants were boiling a pot of water when the wind blew leaves from a nearby bush into it. He found the resulting drink refreshing and pleasant.

## Spread across Asia

Buddhist monks carried tea to Japan and Korea. Those cultures adopted the drink and shaped it over many centuries. Local customs, religious practices, and the preferences of the ruling classes all influenced how people drank it.

## Arrival in Europe

Portuguese and Dutch traders brought tea to Europe in the 1600s. Catherine of Braganza popularized it in England and made it fashionable. People of all social classes throughout the country soon drank it.
