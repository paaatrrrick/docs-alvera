This page explains how billing works so there are no surprises on your invoice.

## Plans and charges

You pick a plan and we bill you monthly or yearly. Yearly is cheaper; you save about two months.

Some features are usage based, which means you pay for what you use on top of your base plan. You can see this usage in real time.

## Payment methods

We accept cards and, on bigger plans, invoicing. Add or update your card in the billing settings. If a payment fails, we retry it a few times before we suspend the account.

Got a question about a charge? Read [the billing FAQ](/frequently-asked-questions).
