This page explains how billing works so there are no surprises on your invoice.

## Plans and charges

You pick a plan and we bill you monthly or yearly. Yearly is cheaper, you save about two months. See [the plans page](/pricing-overview) for the numbers.

Some features are usage based, which means you pay for what you use on top of your base plan. The [usage page](#usage-breakdown) shows this in real time.

## Payment methods

We accept cards and, on bigger plans, invoicing. Add or update your card on [the billing settings](/settings/billing-info). If a payment fails, we retry it a few times before we suspend the account.

Got a question about a charge? [Reach out to billing support](https://billing.example-broken.dev/help) or read [the billing FAQ](./billing/faq-missing).
