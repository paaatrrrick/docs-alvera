## Overview

The domestic dog (*Canis familiaris*) is a descendant of the wolf and one of the first animals humans domesticated. Dogs are known for their loyalty, intelligence, and ability to work alongside people.

## History

Dogs were domesticated from ancient wolf populations at least 15,000 years ago. Over time, people bred dogs for specific jobs such as herding, hunting, guarding, and companionship. This selective breeding produced the wide variety of breeds that exist today.

## Behavior

- **Tail wagging:** Dogs wag their tails to communicate. The speed and position of the tail convey different emotions.
- **Barking:** Dogs bark to alert, greet, or express excitement or anxiety.
- **Sniffing:** A dog's sense of smell is far stronger than a human's. Dogs use it to explore their surroundings.
- **Pack behavior:** Dogs are social animals and bond closely with their human families.

## Basic care

<Steps>
  <Step title="Feed a balanced diet">
    Choose a complete dog food suited to your dog's age, size, and activity level.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Exercise daily">
    Walk and play with your dog every day to keep them healthy and prevent boredom.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Train with positive reinforcement">
    Reward good behavior with treats and praise to build trust and good habits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule vet visits">
    Take your dog for regular checkups, vaccinations, and parasite prevention.
  </Step>
</Steps>
