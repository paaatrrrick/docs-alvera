## Overview

The domestic dog (*Canis familiaris*) descends from ancient wolves. Dogs were the first animal humans domesticated, more than 15,000 years ago, and they have since been bred for herding, hunting, guarding, and companionship.

## Common breeds

| Breed              | Size   | Temperament                 |
| ------------------ | ------ | --------------------------- |
| Labrador Retriever | Large  | Friendly, outgoing          |
| German Shepherd    | Large  | Loyal, intelligent          |
| Golden Retriever   | Large  | Gentle, eager to please     |
| French Bulldog     | Small  | Playful, adaptable          |
| Border Collie      | Medium | Energetic, highly trainable |

## Behavior

- **Tail wagging**: Dogs wag their tails to communicate. The speed and position signal different emotions.
- **Barking**: Dogs bark to alert, greet, or express excitement or anxiety.
- **Sniffing**: A dog's sense of smell is tens of thousands of times more sensitive than a human's.
- **Play bowing**: A dog lowers its front legs with its rear in the air to invite play.

## Basic care

1. Feed a balanced diet suited to your dog's age, size, and activity level.
2. Provide fresh water at all times.
3. Exercise your dog daily with walks and play.
4. Schedule regular veterinary checkups and vaccinations.
5. Start training and socialization early, using positive reinforcement.
