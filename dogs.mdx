Domestic dogs are social mammals descended from wolves. People have kept dogs as working partners and companions for thousands of years. This page covers their history, common behaviors, and basic care.

## History

Dogs were the first animals humans domesticated, likely more than 15,000 years ago. Early humans and wolves benefited from cooperating during hunts. Over time, people bred dogs for specific jobs such as herding, guarding, and retrieving.

## Behavior

- **Tail wagging:** Dogs wag their tails to communicate. A loose, wide wag usually signals friendliness.
- **Barking:** Dogs bark to alert, greet, or express excitement or anxiety.
- **Pack instinct:** Dogs are social animals. They often treat their human family as their pack.
- **Sniffing:** A dog's sense of smell is far stronger than a human's. Dogs explore the world largely through scent.

## Basic care

1. Feed a balanced diet appropriate for your dog's age, size, and activity level.
2. Provide fresh water at all times.
3. Exercise your dog daily with walks and play.
4. Schedule regular checkups and vaccinations with a veterinarian.
5. Train your dog with consistent, positive reinforcement.
