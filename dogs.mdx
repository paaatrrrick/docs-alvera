## Overview

The domestic dog (*Canis familiaris*) descends from wolves and was the first animal humans domesticated, more than 15,000 years ago. Dogs have been bred for herding, hunting, guarding, and companionship.

| Breed              | Size   | Temperament                 |
| ------------------ | ------ | --------------------------- |
| Labrador Retriever | Large  | Friendly, eager to please   |

| Beagle             | Medium | Curious, merry              |

## Behavior

- **Tail wagging:** Dogs wag their tails to signal emotion. The speed and position of the tail convey different moods.
- **Barking:** Dogs bark to alert, greet, or express excitement or anxiety.
- **Pack instincts:** Dogs are social animals and form strong bonds with their human families.
- **Sense of smell:** A dog's sense of smell is tens of thousands of times more sensitive than a human's.

## Basic care

- Feed a complete, balanced diet suited to your dog's age, size, and activity level.
- Provide fresh water at all times.
- Walk and exercise your dog daily.
- Groom your dog regularly based on its coat type.
- Schedule regular veterinary checkups and vaccinations.
