## Overview

The domestic dog (*Canis familiaris*) descends from an ancient population of wolves. People domesticated dogs at least 15,000 years ago, making them the first domesticated animal.

## Behavior

- **Tail wagging**: Dogs wag their tails to communicate. The speed and direction of a wag signal different emotions.
- **Sniffing**: Dogs have up to 300 million olfactory receptors. They explore the world primarily through smell.
- **Barking**: Dogs bark to alert, greet, or express excitement or anxiety.
- **Pack behavior**: Dogs are social animals. They form strong bonds with their human families.

## Basic care

1. Feed a complete, balanced diet suited to your dog's size and age.
2. Provide fresh water at all times.
3. Walk and exercise your dog every day.
4. Train your dog with positive reinforcement.
5. Schedule regular checkups and vaccinations with a veterinarian.
