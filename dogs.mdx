## Overview

The domestic dog (*Canis familiaris*) descends from ancient wolves. Humans domesticated dogs at least 15,000 years ago, making them the first domesticated animal.

## Common breeds

| Breed              | Origin         | Notable traits                     |
| ------------------ | -------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Labrador Retriever | Canada         | Friendly, eager to please          |
| German Shepherd    | Germany        | Intelligent, loyal, working dog    |
| Golden Retriever   | Scotland       | Gentle, patient with children      |
| Beagle             | England        | Keen nose, curious, vocal          |
| Border Collie      | United Kingdom | Highly energetic, herding instinct |

## Behavior

- **Tail wagging** signals emotion. The speed and position of the tail indicate whether a dog is excited, anxious, or relaxed.
- **Sniffing** is how dogs explore. A dog's nose has up to 300 million scent receptors.
- **Barking** communicates alerts, excitement, or boredom.
- **Play bowing** invites another dog or person to play.

## Basic care

- Feed a complete, balanced dog food suited to your dog's age and size.
- Provide fresh water at all times.
- Give your dog daily exercise and mental stimulation.
- Schedule regular veterinary checkups and vaccinations.
