## Overview

The domestic dog (*Canis familiaris*) is a descendant of ancient wolves and was the first species humans domesticated, at least 15,000 years ago. Dogs have been bred for herding, hunting, guarding, and companionship.

## Common breeds

| Breed              | Origin         | Notable traits                        |
| ------------------ | -------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Labrador Retriever | Canada         | Friendly, eager to please, athletic   |
| German Shepherd    | Germany        | Intelligent, loyal, trainable         |
| Golden Retriever   | Scotland       | Gentle, patient, good with families   |
| French Bulldog     | France         | Compact, playful, low exercise needs  |
| Border Collie      | United Kingdom | Highly energetic, skilled herder      |

## Behavior

- **Tail wagging**: Dogs wag their tails to communicate, not only when happy.
- **Barking**: Dogs bark to alert, greet, or express excitement or anxiety.
- **Sniffing**: A dog's sense of smell is tens of thousands of times more sensitive than a human's.
- **Pack instincts**: Dogs are social and form strong bonds with their families.

## Basic care

1. Feed a complete, balanced diet suited to your dog's age and size.
2. Provide fresh water at all times.
3. Give daily exercise through walks and play.
4. Schedule regular veterinary checkups and vaccinations.
5. Train with positive reinforcement and socialize early.
