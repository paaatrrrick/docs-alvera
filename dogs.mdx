## Overview

The domestic dog (*Canis familiaris*) descends from an ancient population of wolves. Humans domesticated dogs more than 15,000 years ago, before any other animal. Today dogs live alongside people on every inhabited continent.

## Origins

Early dogs lived near hunter-gatherer camps, where they scavenged food and helped with hunting. Over thousands of years, people bred dogs for specific jobs such as herding, guarding and pulling sleds. This selective breeding produced the wide range of sizes, coats and temperaments you see today.

## Breed groups

Kennel clubs sort breeds into groups based on the work they were originally bred to do. The American Kennel Club uses seven groups.

| Group    | Original purpose                     | Example breeds                          |
| -------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| Sporting | Finding and retrieving game birds    | Labrador Retriever, Golden Retriever    |
| Hound    | Tracking game by scent or sight      | Beagle, Greyhound                       |
| Working  | Guarding, pulling and rescue         | Siberian Husky, Bernese Mountain Dog    |
| Terrier  | Hunting rodents and other vermin     | Jack Russell Terrier, Scottish Terrier  |
| Toy      | Companionship                        | Chihuahua, Pomeranian                   |
| Herding  | Moving livestock                     | Border Collie, German Shepherd          |
| Non-sporting | Varied roles                       | Bulldog, Poodle                         |

## Caring for a dog

Dogs need daily attention to stay healthy and happy.

- **Exercise:** Walk your dog every day. High-energy breeds such as Border Collies need more activity than toy breeds.
- **Diet:** Feed a complete dog food suited to your dog's age and size. Never feed chocolate, grapes or onions, which are toxic to dogs.
- **Health:** Take your dog to a veterinarian for regular checkups and vaccinations.
- **Training:** Use positive reinforcement, such as treats and praise, to teach basic commands.

## Dogs at work

Many dogs have jobs that help people.

- **Guide dogs** help people who are blind or have low vision move around safely.
- **Search and rescue dogs** find people lost in the wilderness or trapped after disasters.
- **Detection dogs** use their sense of smell to find explosives, drugs or contraband.
- **Therapy dogs** visit hospitals and schools to comfort people.
