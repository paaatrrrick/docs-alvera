## Overview

The domestic dog (*Canis familiaris*) descends from an ancient wolf population. Dogs were the first animal humans domesticated, more than 15,000 years ago, and have since been bred for herding, hunting, guarding, and companionship.

## Popular breeds

| Breed              | Origin         | Notable traits                       |
| ------------------ | -------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Labrador Retriever | Canada         | Friendly, outgoing, eager to please  |
| German Shepherd    | Germany        | Intelligent, loyal, versatile worker |
| Golden Retriever   | United Kingdom | Gentle, patient, great with families |
| French Bulldog     | France         | Compact, playful, low exercise needs |
| Border Collie      | United Kingdom | Highly energetic, top herding breed  |

## Behavior

- **Social structure:** Dogs are social animals that bond closely with their human families.
- **Communication:** Dogs bark, whine, growl, and use body language such as tail wagging and ear position.
- **Scent:** A dog's sense of smell is tens of thousands of times more sensitive than a human's.
- **Play:** Play helps dogs build social skills and burn off energy.

## Basic care

1. Feed a complete, balanced diet suited to your dog's age, size, and activity level.
2. Provide fresh water at all times.
3. Exercise your dog daily with walks and play.
4. Schedule regular veterinary checkups and vaccinations.
5. Start training and socialization early using positive reinforcement.
