## Overview

The domestic dog (*Canis familiaris*) descends from ancient wolves. Humans domesticated dogs at least 15,000 years ago, making them the first domesticated animal. Dogs have since been bred for herding, hunting, guarding, and companionship.

## Popular breeds

| Breed            | Size   | Temperament              |
| ---------------- | ------ | ------------------------ |
| Labrador Retriever | Large | Friendly, outgoing      |
| German Shepherd  | Large  | Loyal, intelligent       |
| Golden Retriever | Large  | Gentle, eager to please  |
| French Bulldog   | Small  | Playful, adaptable       |
| Beagle           | Medium | Curious, merry           |

## Behavior

- **Tail wagging** signals emotion. The speed and direction of the wag can indicate excitement, friendliness, or anxiety.
- **Barking** communicates alerts, excitement, or a need for attention.
- **Sniffing** is how dogs explore the world. A dog's sense of smell is tens of thousands of times more sensitive than a human's.
- **Play bowing** invites another dog or person to play.

## Basic care

- Feed your dog a complete, balanced diet suited to its size, age, and activity level.
- Provide fresh water at all times.
- Give your dog daily exercise and mental stimulation.
- Schedule regular veterinary checkups and vaccinations.
- Groom your dog regularly based on its coat type.
