## Overview

The domestic dog (*Canis familiaris*) descends from ancient wolves. Dogs were the first species humans domesticated, more than 15,000 years ago, and have since been bred for herding, hunting, guarding, and companionship.

| ------------------ | ------ | ------------------------- |
| Labrador Retriever | Large  | Friendly, outgoing        |

| Beagle             | Medium | Curious, merry            |

## Behavior

- **Tail wagging** signals emotional arousal. The context and tail position tell you whether a dog is happy or anxious.
- **Barking** communicates alerts, excitement, or boredom.
- **Sniffing** is how dogs gather information. Their sense of smell is far stronger than a human's.
- **Play bows** invite other dogs or people to play.

## Basic care

1. Feed a balanced diet suited to your dog's size, age, and activity level.
2. Provide fresh water at all times.
3. Exercise your dog daily with walks and play.
4. Schedule regular veterinary checkups and vaccinations.
5. Train with positive reinforcement and socialize early.
