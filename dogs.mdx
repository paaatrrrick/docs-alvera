Dogs are often called humanity's best friend. This page gives a quick overview of where domestic dogs came from and what makes them unique.

## Origins

Domestic dogs descend from an ancient population of gray wolves. People and dogs began living together more than 15,000 years ago. Early dogs helped humans hunt, guard camps, and herd animals.

## Breeds and roles

Hundreds of dog breeds exist today, from tiny Chihuahuas to giant Great Danes. Many breeds were developed for specific jobs, such as herding, retrieving, guarding, or pulling sleds. Today, dogs also work as guide dogs, search and rescue dogs, and therapy dogs.

## Caring for a dog

- Provide fresh water and a balanced diet.
- Walk and exercise your dog every day.
- Train your dog with consistent, positive reinforcement.
- Schedule regular veterinary checkups.
