# Hutts

Hutts are a large, slug-like species from the planet Nal Hutta. They are best known for running vast criminal empires across the Outer Rim.

## Biology

Hutts can live for more than a thousand years. Their thick skin and great bulk make them highly resistant to injury and most poisons.

## The Hutt clans

Hutt society is organized into powerful families called kajidics. Each kajidic controls its own territory, smuggling routes, and business interests.

## Notable Hutts

- **Jabba the Hutt:** A crime lord who ruled from his palace on Tatooine and held Han Solo frozen in carbonite.
- **Ziro the Hutt:** Jabba's uncle, who plotted against his own clan during the Clone Wars.
- **Gardulla the Hutt:** A rival of Jabba who once owned Anakin Skywalker and his mother, Shmi.

## Language

Hutts speak Huttese, a language used widely by smugglers, bounty hunters, and traders throughout the Outer Rim.
