# Hutts

Hutts are a large, slug-like species from the planet Nal Hutta. Many Hutts lead powerful criminal empires across the Outer Rim. Jabba the Hutt is the most famous member of the species.

## Appearance

Hutts have thick, muscular bodies that end in a tail instead of legs. They have wide mouths, small arms, and large reptilian eyes. A Hutt can live for more than a thousand years.

## Hutt clans

Hutts organize themselves into family clans called kajidics. Each kajidic runs its own criminal operations, such as smuggling, spice trading, and gambling. The heads of the major clans form the Hutt Grand Council.

## Territory

Hutt Space is a region of the Outer Rim under Hutt control. Key worlds include:

- **Nal Hutta**: The Hutt homeworld and seat of the Grand Council.
- **Nar Shaddaa**: The Smuggler's Moon, a vertical city that orbits Nal Hutta.
- **Tatooine**: A desert world where Jabba ruled from his palace in the Dune Sea.

## Language

Hutts speak Huttese. Huttese is a common trade language among smugglers, bounty hunters, and other residents of the Outer Rim.

## Notable Hutts

- **Jabba Desilijic Tiure**: Ruled a criminal empire from Tatooine until Leia Organa killed him.
- **Ziro the Hutt**: Jabba's uncle, who appeared during the Clone Wars.
- **Rotta the Hutt**: Jabba's son, whose kidnapping drew Anakin Skywalker and Ahsoka Tano to Teth.
