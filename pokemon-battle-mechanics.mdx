## Overview

Battles are the core of the main-series Pokémon games. Each turn, you and your opponent choose an action, such as using a move, switching Pokémon, or using an item. The faster Pokémon usually acts first. A Pokémon faints when its HP drops to zero.

## Stats

Every Pokémon has six stats that determine how it performs in battle.

| Stat            | Effect                                                   |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| HP              | How much damage the Pokémon can take before fainting     |
| Attack          | Power of physical moves                                  |
| Defense         | Resistance to physical moves                             |
| Special Attack  | Power of special moves                                   |
| Special Defense | Resistance to special moves                              |
| Speed           | Turn order. The faster Pokémon usually moves first.      |

A Pokémon's final stats come from three sources:

- **Base stats**: Fixed values shared by every member of a species.
- **Individual values (IVs)**: Hidden values from 0 to 31 that vary between individual Pokémon.
- **Effort values (EVs)**: Points a Pokémon earns by defeating other Pokémon. A Pokémon can hold up to 510 EVs in total, with a maximum of 252 in a single stat.

## Natures

Since Generation III, each Pokémon has one of 25 natures. Most natures raise one stat by 10% and lower another by 10%. Five natures, such as Hardy and Serious, are neutral and change no stats. Natures never affect HP.

| Nature  | Raises          | Lowers          |
| ------- | --------------- | --------------- |
| Adamant | Attack          | Special Attack  |
| Modest  | Special Attack  | Attack          |
| Jolly   | Speed           | Special Attack  |
| Timid   | Speed           | Attack          |
| Bold    | Defense         | Attack          |
| Calm    | Special Defense | Attack          |

## Abilities

Abilities, also introduced in Generation III, are passive effects that apply in or out of battle. Some examples:

- **Levitate**: Makes the Pokémon immune to Ground-type moves.
- **Intimidate**: Lowers the opponent's Attack when the Pokémon enters battle.
- **Blaze**, **Torrent**, and **Overgrow**: Boost Fire-, Water-, and Grass-type moves when the Pokémon's HP is low.

## Move categories

Every move falls into one of three categories.

- **Physical**: Deals damage based on the user's Attack and the target's Defense.
- **Special**: Deals damage based on the user's Special Attack and the target's Special Defense.
- **Status**: Deals no direct damage. Instead, it changes stats, inflicts conditions, or alters the field.

Before Generation IV, a move's type determined whether it was physical or special. Since Generation IV, each move has its own category.

## Same-type attack bonus

When a Pokémon uses a move that matches one of its own types, the move deals 1.5 times the damage. This is called the same-type attack bonus (STAB).

## Type effectiveness

Type matchups multiply a move's damage:

| Matchup             | Multiplier |
| ------------------- | ---------- |
| Super effective     | 2x         |
| Normal              | 1x         |
| Not very effective  | 0.5x       |
| No effect           | 0x         |

Multipliers stack against dual-type Pokémon. For example, a Grass-type move deals 4x damage to a Pokémon that is both Water and Ground type. See [Pokémon](/pokemon) for the type chart.

## Status conditions

Status conditions persist after a battle until you heal them. A Pokémon can have only one at a time.

| Condition | Effect                                                              |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Burn      | Deals damage each turn and halves the damage of physical moves      |
| Poison    | Deals damage each turn                                              |
| Paralysis | Lowers Speed and sometimes prevents the Pokémon from moving         |
| Sleep     | Prevents the Pokémon from moving for several turns                  |
| Freeze    | Prevents the Pokémon from moving until it thaws                     |
