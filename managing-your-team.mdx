As your team grows, you will want to add more people and manage their permissions.

## Inviting people

To invite someone, go to the team page and enter their email. They will receive an invitation that expires in 3 days.

## Roles

We have three roles, and each one gives different access:

- **Owner**: can do everything, including billing.
- **Admin**: can manage content and members, but not billing.
- **Viewer**: can only view, not edit.

You can change someone's role anytime from the member list.

If you need custom roles, those are available on the enterprise plan.
