As your team grows, you will want to add more people and manage their permissions.

## Inviting people

To invite someone, go to [the team page](/settings/team-members) and enter their email. They will receive an invitation that expires in 3 days.

## Roles

We have three roles, each one gives different access:

- **Owner** — can do everything, including billing.
- **Admin** — can manage content and members, but not billing.
- **Viewer** — can only look, not edit.

You can change someone’s role anytime from [the member list](#the-member-list). For a deeper explanation, read [the permissions guide](./security/permissions-deep-dive).

If you need custom roles, that’s available on the enterprise plan — [talk to us](https://example-broken.dev/enterprise).
