## Overview

The sweet orange (*Citrus × sinensis*) is a citrus fruit that grows on evergreen trees. It is a hybrid of the pomelo and the mandarin, and it originated in southern China and northeastern India.

## Popular varieties

| Variety   | Flavor             | Best for         |
| --------- | ------------------ | ---------------- |
| Navel     | Sweet, seedless    | Snacking         |
| Valencia  | Sweet, very juicy  | Juicing          |
| Blood     | Sweet, berry notes | Salads, desserts |
| Cara Cara | Low acid, sweet    | Snacking, salads |
| Seville   | Bitter, sour       | Marmalade        |

## Nutrition

A medium orange contains about 62 calories and 3 grams of dietary fiber. Oranges are an excellent source of vitamin C and also provide folate and potassium.

## Culinary uses

- **Beverages**: fresh juice and cocktails
- **Baking**: cakes, muffins, and glazes made with zest
- **Preserves**: marmalade
- **Raw**: peeled segments or sliced in salads
