## Overview

Hyperspace is an alternate dimension that starships enter to travel faster than light. A ship equipped with a hyperdrive jumps into hyperspace, crosses vast distances in hours or days, and reverts to realspace at its destination.

## Hyperdrives

The hyperdrive is the engine that makes a jump possible. Hyperdrives are rated by class, where lower numbers mean faster travel.

| Ship | Hyperdrive class |
| --- | --- |
| Millennium Falcon | 0.5 |
| X-wing starfighter | 1.0 |
| Imperial Star Destroyer | 2.0 |

The *Millennium Falcon*'s modified class 0.5 hyperdrive is a major reason Han Solo boasts that the ship "made the Kessel Run in less than twelve parsecs."

## Navigation

Jumping to hyperspace requires careful calculation. Ships rely on a navigation computer, or navicomputer, to plot a safe course around stars, planets, and other mass shadows.

- **Navicomputers**: Store astrogation charts and calculate jump coordinates.
- **Astromech droids**: Smaller ships like X-wings use astromech droids such as R2-D2 in place of a full navicomputer.
- **Blind jumps**: Jumping without calculations risks flying through a star or bouncing too close to a supernova.

## Famous hyperspace routes

Established hyperlanes let ships travel safely along charted paths.

- **Corellian Run**: A major trade route linking Corellia to the Outer Rim.
- **Hydian Way**: A long hyperlane spanning the galaxy from north to south.
- **Kessel Run**: A smuggler's route through the dangerous Akkadese Maelstrom near Kessel.

## Interdiction

Ships in hyperspace can be forced back into realspace. Interdictor cruisers project gravity wells that mimic a planet's mass shadow, pulling passing ships out of their jump. The Empire uses interdiction to trap Rebel ships and cut off escape routes.
