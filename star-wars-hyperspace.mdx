---

Hyperspace is an alternate dimension that starships enter to travel faster than light. Without it, journeys between star systems would take lifetimes.

## Hyperdrives

A hyperdrive is the engine that lets a ship jump into hyperspace. Hyperdrives are rated by class. A lower class number means a faster drive.

| Ship | Hyperdrive class |
| --- | --- |
| Millennium Falcon | Class 0.5 |
| X-wing starfighter | Class 1 |
| Imperial-class Star Destroyer | Class 2 |

## Navigation

Pilots must calculate a jump before entering hyperspace. Stars, planets, and other mass shadows can destroy a ship that flies through them. Most ships rely on a navicomputer or an astromech droid to plot a safe course.

## Hyperspace lanes

Explorers charted established routes, called hyperspace lanes, that connect major systems. Well-known lanes include:

- **Hydian Way**: runs north to south across the galaxy
- **Corellian Run**: links the Core Worlds to the Outer Rim
- **Perlemian Trade Route**: connects Coruscant to the Outer Rim

## Interdiction

Gravity well projectors, such as those on Interdictor cruisers, generate mass shadows that pull ships out of hyperspace. The Empire used interdiction to trap fleeing rebels.

## The Holdo maneuver

During the Battle of Crait, Vice Admiral Holdo jumped the cruiser *Raddus* to lightspeed directly through the First Order flagship *Supremacy*. The impact tore the flagship apart.
