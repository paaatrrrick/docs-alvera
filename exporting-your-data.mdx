We believe your data is yours, so we make it easy to export it.

## Starting an export

Go to the data settings and select "Export". We gather everything and email you a download link when it's ready. For a big account this can take an hour or two.

The export comes as a zip file containing JSON and CSV files.

<Note>
The download link expires after 7 days for security. If you miss it, just start a new export.
</Note>

## Automating exports

If you want exports on a schedule, you can use our API.
