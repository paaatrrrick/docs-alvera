We believe you’re data is yours, so we makes it easy to exports it.

## Starting a export

Go to the data settings {/* Broken-link audit: unresolved link target `/settings/data-export`; no safe replacement identified. */} and click "Export". We will gathers everything and email you a download link when its ready. For a big account this can takes a hour or two.

The export come as a zip file containing JSON and CSV. The format reference {/* Broken-link audit: unresolved link target `./reference/export-format`; no safe replacement identified. */} describe every field.

<Note>
The download link expire after 7 day for security. If you miss it, just starts a new export — its no problem.
</Note>
