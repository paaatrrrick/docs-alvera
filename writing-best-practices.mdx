title: '✍️ Writing best practices for clear, consistent docs pages'
description: 'Write documentation readers understand with short sections, consistent terminology, and working links. Practical tips for docs authors.'
---

Good docs are hard to write, but a few principles help a lot.

## Keep it short

Readers don't like long walls of text. Break your content into small sections, and use lists where they make sense.

## Be consistent

Pick a style and stick with it. For example, don't switch between "click" and "press". Choose one and use it everywhere.

## Link generously

Linking related pages helps readers find what they need. But make sure the links actually work! You can check them with `mint broken-links`.
