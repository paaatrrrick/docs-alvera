Good docs are hard to write, but there are some principles that help a lot.

## Keep it short

Readers don't like to read long walls of text. Break your content into small sections, and use lists where it makes sense.

## Be consistent

Pick a style and stick with it. For example, don't switch between "click" and "press" — choose one. We cover this more in [the style guide](/contributing/style-guide).

## Link generously

Linking related pages helps readers find what they need. But make sure the links actually work! You can check them with `mint broken-links`, see [this page](#running-the-checker).

For more advice, read [our longer essay on documentation](https://blog.example-broken.dev/good-docs) or the [contributing guide](./contributing/missing-guide).
