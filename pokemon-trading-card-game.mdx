## Overview

The Pokémon Trading Card Game (TCG) is a collectible card game based on the Pokémon franchise. Media Factory first released it in Japan in October 1996. Wizards of the Coast published the English version in 1999, and The Pokémon Company International has published it since 2003.

Two players each build a deck of Pokémon, Energy, and Trainer cards. Players take turns attacking each other's Pokémon until one player meets a win condition.

## Card types

Every deck uses three kinds of cards.

| Card type | Purpose                                                                |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pokémon   | The creatures that battle. Each has HP, attacks, a type, and a weakness. |
| Energy    | Attached to Pokémon to pay the cost of their attacks.                  |
| Trainer   | One-time effects that draw cards, heal, search the deck, and more.     |

### Pokémon cards

Pokémon cards come in stages:

- **Basic** — You can play these directly onto your Bench.
- **Stage 1** — Evolves from a Basic Pokémon.
- **Stage 2** — Evolves from a Stage 1 Pokémon.

Special card mechanics such as Pokémon-EX, Pokémon-GX, Pokémon V, and Pokémon ex give stronger attacks. In exchange, your opponent takes extra Prize cards when they Knock Out one of these Pokémon.

### Trainer cards

Trainer cards fall into several subtypes:

- **Item** — Play as many as you want during your turn.
- **Supporter** — Play only one per turn.
- **Stadium** — Stays in play and affects both players until another Stadium replaces it.
- **Pokémon Tool** — Attaches to a Pokémon for an ongoing effect.

## Deck building rules

A standard deck must follow these rules:

- The deck contains exactly 60 cards.
- The deck includes at least one Basic Pokémon.
- The deck includes no more than four copies of any card with the same name. Basic Energy cards are exempt from this limit.

## How to win

You win a game when any of the following happens:

- You take all six of your Prize cards. You take a Prize card each time you Knock Out one of your opponent's Pokémon.
- Your opponent has no Pokémon left in play.
- Your opponent cannot draw a card at the start of their turn.

## Turn structure

Each turn follows the same order:

1. Draw a card.
2. Take any of these actions in any order:
   - Put Basic Pokémon onto your Bench.
   - Evolve your Pokémon.
   - Attach one Energy card to one of your Pokémon.
   - Play Trainer cards.
   - Retreat your Active Pokémon once.
   - Use Abilities.
3. Attack with your Active Pokémon. Attacking ends your turn.

## Formats

Organized play uses two main formats:

- **Standard** — Uses only cards from the most recent sets. The legal card pool rotates each year.
- **Expanded** — Uses a larger pool of cards going back to the Black & White series.

## Digital versions

You can also play the TCG digitally:

- **Pokémon TCG Live** — The current official digital client for Standard and Expanded play.
- **Pokémon TCG Pocket** — A mobile game released in 2024 with simplified rules and 20-card decks.
