## Overview

Battles are the core of the main-series Pokémon games. Two Trainers send out Pokémon and take turns choosing actions until every Pokémon on one side faints. This page covers the mechanics that decide who wins.

## Turn order

Each turn, both Trainers choose an action at the same time. You can use a move, switch Pokémon, use an item, or run from a wild battle.

The game resolves actions in this order:

1. Switching Pokémon and using items.
2. Moves with higher priority, such as Quick Attack or Protect.
3. Moves of equal priority, ordered by the user's Speed stat.

## Stats

Every Pokémon has six stats that shape its performance in battle.

| Stat            | Effect                                              |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| HP              | How much damage the Pokémon can take before fainting |
| Attack          | Power of physical moves                             |
| Defense         | Resistance to physical moves                        |
| Special Attack  | Power of special moves                              |
| Special Defense | Resistance to special moves                         |
| Speed           | Determines who moves first                          |

A Pokémon's final stats depend on three factors:

- **Base stats**: Fixed values shared by every member of a species.
- **Individual values (IVs)**: Hidden values from 0 to 31 that vary between individual Pokémon.
- **Effort values (EVs)**: Points earned by defeating Pokémon or using items. A Pokémon can hold up to 510 EVs total, with a maximum of 252 in a single stat.

## Moves

A Pokémon can know up to four moves at once. Each move has a type, a category, power, accuracy, and Power Points (PP).

- **Physical** moves use Attack and Defense.
- **Special** moves use Special Attack and Special Defense.
- **Status** moves deal no direct damage. They change stats, inflict conditions, or alter the field.

A move gets a Same-Type Attack Bonus (STAB) of 1.5x damage when its type matches one of the user's types.

## Abilities

Every Pokémon has one ability that provides a passive effect. For example, Levitate grants immunity to Ground-type moves, and Intimidate lowers the opponent's Attack when the Pokémon enters battle.

## Natures

Each Pokémon has one of 25 natures. Most natures raise one stat by 10% and lower another by 10%. Five natures, such as Hardy and Serious, have no effect on stats.

## Status conditions

| Condition | Effect                                                         |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Burn      | Deals damage each turn and halves physical damage dealt         |
| Freeze    | Prevents the Pokémon from moving until it thaws                 |
| Paralysis | Lowers Speed and can stop the Pokémon from moving               |
| Poison    | Deals damage at the end of each turn                            |
| Sleep     | Prevents the Pokémon from moving for one to three turns         |

A Pokémon can have only one of these conditions at a time.

## Battle formats

- **Single battles**: Each Trainer uses one Pokémon at a time.
- **Double battles**: Each Trainer uses two Pokémon at once. This is the format of official Video Game Championships (VGC).
- **Raid battles**: Several Trainers team up against one powerful wild Pokémon.
