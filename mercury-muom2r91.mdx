Mercury is the smallest planet in the solar system and the closest one to the Sun. It's only a little larger than Earth's Moon, but it's a world of extremes, from its scorching days to its freezing nights.

## Orbit and rotation

Mercury races around the Sun in just 88 Earth days, which makes its year the shortest of any planet. It spins slowly, though, turning once every 59 Earth days. Because its fast orbit combines with its slow spin, one full solar day on Mercury (from one sunrise to the next) lasts about 176 Earth days.

Mercury's orbit is also the most eccentric of all the planets. Its distance from the Sun ranges from about 46 million km to about 70 million km.

## Surface and structure

Mercury's surface is heavily cratered and looks a lot like the Moon's. One of its largest features is the Caloris Basin, an impact crater roughly 1,500 km across.

Inside, Mercury has a very large iron core that takes up most of its radius. As that core cooled over billions of years, the whole planet shrank. The crust wrinkled into long cliffs called lobate scarps, sometimes described as "wrinkle ridges," that stretch for hundreds of kilometers.

## Temperature and atmosphere

Mercury has almost no atmosphere to trap heat, so its temperatures swing wildly. Daytime temperatures can reach about 430°C, while nighttime temperatures drop to about -180°C.

Instead of a true atmosphere, Mercury has a very thin exosphere made of atoms blasted off its surface by the solar wind and micrometeoroid impacts. Surprisingly, there is water ice at its poles, hidden inside craters that stay permanently in shadow.

## Exploration

- **Mariner 10**: NASA's Mariner 10 flew past Mercury three times in 1974 and 1975 and mapped part of its surface for the first time.
- **MESSENGER**: NASA's MESSENGER became the first spacecraft to orbit Mercury. It studied the planet from 2011 to 2015, mapping the whole surface and confirming the polar ice.
- **BepiColombo**: BepiColombo is a joint mission between ESA and JAXA. It is due to enter orbit around Mercury in 2026.
