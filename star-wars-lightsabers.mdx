# Lightsabers

Lightsabers are plasma-blade weapons wielded by Force users. Each one is hand-built by its owner and reflects their training, philosophy, and connection to the Force.

## Construction

A lightsaber is powered by a kyber crystal focused through a series of emitter components. Standard parts include:

- **Kyber crystal** — the heart of the weapon; determines blade color.
- **Emitter matrix** — shapes and contains the plasma blade.
- **Power cell** — supplies energy to ignite the blade.
- **Hilt** — the handle, personalized to the wielder's grip and style.

## Blade colors

Blade color reflects the bond between the wielder and their crystal.

- **Blue** — Jedi Guardians, focused on combat.
- **Green** — Jedi Consulars, focused on diplomacy and the Force.
- **Yellow** — Jedi Sentinels and Temple Guards.
- **Purple** — rare; wielded by masters who walk between light and dark.
- **Red** — Sith blades, forged by bleeding a kyber crystal.
- **White** — purified crystals reclaimed from the dark side.

## Forms of combat

Seven classical forms of lightsaber combat evolved over the millennia, from the foundational Shii-Cho to the aggressive Juyo. Most duelists blend forms to suit their opponent.

## Notable variants

- **Double-bladed saber** — favored by Darth Maul.
- **Crossguard saber** — an ancient design revived by Kylo Ren.
- **Darksaber** — a unique black-bladed weapon tied to Mandalorian leadership.
