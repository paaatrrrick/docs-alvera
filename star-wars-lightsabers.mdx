# Lightsabers

A lightsaber is a plasma blade weapon wielded by Force users. Jedi and Sith build their own lightsabers, and each one reflects the personality of its owner.

## Kyber crystals

Every lightsaber is powered by a kyber crystal. The crystal focuses the blade's energy and gives it its color. Learn more on the [kyber crystals](/star-wars-kyber-crystals) page.

## Blade colors

- **Blue and green:** Common among Jedi Knights and Jedi Consulars.
- **Red:** Associated with the Sith, who bleed their crystals through the dark side.
- **Purple:** Rare, famously carried by Mace Windu.

## Hilt designs

- **Single blade:** The classic design used by most Jedi and Sith.
- **Double-bladed:** A staff with a blade at each end, wielded by Darth Maul.
- **Crossguard:** Two small vents that extend from the hilt, carried by Kylo Ren.

## Lightsaber combat forms

The Jedi recognize seven classic forms of lightsaber combat. Examples include Form III (Soresu), a defensive style, and Form V (Shien/Djem So), a powerful counterattacking style.
