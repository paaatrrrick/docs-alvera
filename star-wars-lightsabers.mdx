# Lightsabers

A lightsaber is a plasma blade weapon wielded by Force users. Jedi and Sith each build their own lightsaber, and the weapon reflects its owner's skill and character.

## Kyber crystals

Every lightsaber is powered by a kyber crystal. The crystal focuses energy into a blade. Learn more in [Kyber crystals](/star-wars-kyber-crystals).

## Blade colors

- **Blue and green**: Common among Jedi.
- **Red**: Used by the Sith, whose crystals are "bled" through the dark side.
- **Purple**: Rare. Mace Windu carried one.

## Variants

- **Double-bladed**: Darth Maul used a saberstaff with a blade at each end.
- **Crossguard**: Kylo Ren's lightsaber vents excess energy through two side quillons.
- **Shoto**: A shorter blade, often paired with a standard lightsaber.

## Combat forms

Jedi train in seven classic forms of lightsaber combat. Each form balances offense and defense differently. For example, Form III (Soresu) focuses on defense, while Form VII (Juyo/Vaapad) is aggressive.
