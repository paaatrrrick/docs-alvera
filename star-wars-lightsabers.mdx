# Lightsabers

Lightsabers are the signature weapons of the Jedi Order and the Sith. Each one is hand-built by its wielder, using a focusing kyber crystal to shape a blade of pure plasma.

## Construction

Every lightsaber consists of a hilt, a power cell, focusing lenses, and one or more kyber crystals. Constructing a lightsaber is a rite of passage for a Jedi Padawan and is traditionally guided by meditation on the Force.

## Blade colors

The color of a lightsaber blade reflects the bond between the wielder and their kyber crystal.

- **Blue** — commonly carried by Jedi Guardians
- **Green** — favored by Jedi Consulars, including Yoda and Luke Skywalker
- **Purple** — famously wielded by Mace Windu
- **Yellow** — carried by Jedi Temple Guards and Sentinels
- **Red** — used by the Sith, who bleed kyber crystals to corrupt them
- **White** — reclaimed crystals purified by former Sith, such as Ahsoka Tano

## Notable variants

- **Double-bladed** — Darth Maul's saberstaff produced blades from both ends of the hilt
- **Crossguard** — Kylo Ren's unstable, cross-shaped hilt vented plasma through side emitters
- **Curved hilt** — favored by duelists like Count Dooku for precision Makashi form
- **Darksaber** — a unique, black-bladed lightsaber tied to Mandalorian tradition

## Combat forms

Jedi study seven classical forms of lightsaber combat, from the foundational Shii-Cho to the aggressive Juyo. Each form emphasizes different footwork, tempo, and philosophy in battle.
