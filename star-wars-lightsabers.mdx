## Overview

Lightsabers are the iconic energy weapons of the Star Wars galaxy. Jedi and Sith construct their own lightsabers as a rite of passage, and each weapon reflects the values, training, and philosophy of its wielder.

## Construction

A lightsaber is built around a small set of core components:

- **Hilt**: The metal handle that houses the internal components and controls.
- **Kyber crystal**: The Force-attuned crystal that focuses the plasma blade. See [Kyber crystals](/star-wars-kyber-crystals).
- **Focusing lens and emitter**: Shape the plasma into a stable, blade-length arc.
- **Power cell**: Supplies energy to sustain the blade.

Jedi typically travel to Ilum to find their kyber crystal, while Sith bleed captured crystals to turn them red.

## Blade colors

Blade color comes from the kyber crystal and often signals allegiance or role.

| Color | Common wielder | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blue | Jedi Guardian | Combat-focused Jedi |
| Green | Jedi Consular | Diplomatic or Force-focused Jedi |
| Yellow | Jedi Sentinel or Temple Guard | Balance of combat and Force study |
| Purple | Mace Windu | Rare; associated with mastery across light and dark |
| Red | Sith | Produced by bleeding a kyber crystal |
| White | Ahsoka Tano | Purified crystals reclaimed from a Sith |
| Darksaber (black) | Mandalorian leaders | Unique ancient weapon forged by Tarre Vizsla |

## Combat forms

The Jedi Order codified seven classical forms of lightsaber combat:

1. **Form I: Shii-Cho** — The foundational form taught to every initiate.
2. **Form II: Makashi** — A dueling form emphasizing precision, favored by Count Dooku.
3. **Form III: Soresu** — A defensive form mastered by Obi-Wan Kenobi.
4. **Form IV: Ataru** — An acrobatic, aggressive form used by Yoda.
5. **Form V: Shien / Djem So** — A counterattacking form favored by Anakin Skywalker.
6. **Form VI: Niman** — A balanced form blending combat with Force techniques.
7. **Form VII: Juyo / Vaapad** — A ferocious form; Vaapad was developed by Mace Windu.
