# Lightsabers

Lightsabers are elegant, blade-shaped energy weapons wielded by Force-sensitives across the galaxy. Each one is hand-built by its owner and tuned to a single kyber crystal.

## Construction

A lightsaber is more than a weapon — it is a rite of passage. A typical hilt contains:

- A power cell that supplies initial energy to the blade.
- Focusing lenses that shape the plasma arc.
- An emitter matrix that projects and contains the blade.
- A kyber crystal that tunes the color and behavior of the blade.

## Blade colors

Blade color reflects the bond between wielder and crystal.

- **Blue** — Jedi Guardians, focused on defense and combat.
- **Green** — Jedi Consulars, focused on diplomacy and the Force.
- **Yellow** — Jedi Sentinels and Temple Guards.
- **Purple** — rare; a personal mark, most famously Mace Windu's.
- **Red** — Sith blades, forged by "bleeding" a kyber crystal through the dark side.
- **White** — a purified crystal, cleansed of a Sith's corruption.

## Forms of combat

Jedi and Sith study seven classical forms of lightsaber combat, each suited to different opponents and philosophies. Form choice shapes stance, footwork, and the kinds of duels a wielder can survive.
