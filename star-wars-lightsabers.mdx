## Overview

The lightsaber is the signature weapon of the Jedi Order and the Sith. Elegant, precise, and lethal, a lightsaber projects a plasma blade contained by a magnetic field and powered by a kyber crystal. Wielders spend years mastering both the weapon and the discipline required to carry it.

## Construction

Every lightsaber is unique to its owner. Building one is a rite of passage for Jedi initiates and a personal statement for Sith Lords.

Core components include:

- **Kyber crystal** — the focusing crystal that determines the blade's color and behavior.
- **Emitter matrix** — shapes the plasma into a blade of fixed length.
- **Power cell** — supplies energy to the blade.
- **Hilt** — the metal casing that houses the internals and provides the grip.
- **Activation stud** — ignites and shuts down the blade.

## Blade colors

The kyber crystal chosen during construction sets the blade's color.

| Color  | Meaning                                                         |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Blue   | Jedi Guardian; combat-focused                                   |
| Green  | Jedi Consular; wisdom and diplomacy                             |
| Yellow | Jedi Sentinel and Temple Guards                                 |
| Purple | Rare; associated with Mace Windu                                |
| Red    | Sith; produced by "bleeding" a corrupted kyber crystal          |
| White  | Purified crystals, wielded by Ahsoka Tano after leaving the Order |

## Combat forms

Lightsaber combat is codified into seven classical forms, each with distinct strengths.

1. **Shii-Cho** — Form I, the foundational form taught to every initiate.
2. **Makashi** — Form II, elegant dueling favored by Count Dooku.
3. **Soresu** — Form III, defensive mastery favored by Obi-Wan Kenobi.
4. **Ataru** — Form IV, acrobatic and aggressive; used by Yoda.
5. **Shien / Djem So** — Form V, counterattack-driven; Anakin Skywalker's form.
6. **Niman** — Form VI, balanced and diplomatic.
7. **Juyo / Vaapad** — Form VII, ferocious; mastered by Mace Windu.

## Notable wielders

- **Luke Skywalker** — built a green-bladed saber on Tatooine after losing his father's blue one.
- **Darth Vader** — wielded a red saber whose crystal he bled after his fall.
- **Rey** — assembled a yellow-bladed saber from a repurposed staff.
- **Kylo Ren** — carried a cross-guard saber with an unstable, cracked crystal.
- **Ahsoka Tano** — dual white blades, symbolizing her independence from Jedi and Sith.
