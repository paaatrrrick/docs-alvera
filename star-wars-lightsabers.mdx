## Overview

The lightsaber is the signature weapon of the Jedi and Sith, a plasma blade projected from a hilt and powered by a kyber crystal. Elegant, precise, and deadly, it is described as "an elegant weapon for a more civilized age."

## Construction

Every lightsaber is hand-built by its wielder as a rite of passage. The core components include:

- **Hilt** — the metal handle that houses the internal components and controls.
- **Kyber crystal** — the Force-attuned crystal that focuses the blade and determines its color.
- **Emitter** — projects and shapes the plasma blade.
- **Power cell** — supplies energy to the blade.
- **Focusing lens and modulation circuits** — tune the blade's length and stability.

## Blade colors

The color of a lightsaber blade is set by the kyber crystal at its heart.

| Color | Common wielders |
| --- | --- |
| Blue | Jedi Guardians |
| Green | Jedi Consulars |
| Purple | Mace Windu |
| Yellow | Jedi Sentinels and Temple Guards |
| Red | Sith and Dark Side users (bled crystals) |
| White | Ahsoka Tano (purified crystals) |
| Darksaber (black) | Mandalorian leaders |

## Notable variants

- **Double-bladed lightsaber** — favored by Darth Maul; a blade extends from each end of the hilt.
- **Curved-hilt lightsaber** — used by Count Dooku for refined Makashi dueling.
- **Crossguard lightsaber** — Kylo Ren's unstable design with two smaller quillon blades.
- **Lightwhip** — a flexible plasma lash used by rare practitioners.
- **Darksaber** — an ancient, one-of-a-kind blade forged by Tarre Vizsla, the first Mandalorian Jedi.

## Forms of combat

Jedi and Sith study seven classical forms of lightsaber combat:

1. **Shii-Cho** — the foundational form, taught to every youngling.
2. **Makashi** — a dueling form focused on precision and elegance.
3. **Soresu** — a defensive form perfected by Obi-Wan Kenobi.
4. **Ataru** — an acrobatic, aggressive form favored by Yoda.
5. **Shien / Djem So** — powerful counter-attacking forms used by Anakin Skywalker.
6. **Niman** — a balanced form that integrates Force techniques.
7. **Juyo / Vaapad** — the most aggressive form, mastered by Mace Windu.
