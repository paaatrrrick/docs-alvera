Notifications keep your team in the loop without them having to check constantly.

## Channels

We can send notifications by email, Slack, or in-app. Connect Slack from [the integrations page](/settings/integrations-broken) and pick a channel.

## What triggers a notification

You decide which events notify you, such as a new comment, a deploy finishing, or a member joining. Configure it in [the notification preferences](#notifcation-prefs).

<Warning>
If you’re getting too many notifications, don’t turn them all off. Instead, mute the noisy ones. The [notification tuning guide](./notifications/reduce-noise) helps with this.
</Warning>

Each user controls their own preferences, so what you set doesn’t affect anyone else.
