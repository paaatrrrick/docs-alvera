---
title: 'Adding Status Badges to Show Page Maturity Clearly'
description: 'Show readers whether a page is new, updated, or deprecated by adding small status badges, and keep the labels consistent across your docs.'
---

Status badges are short labels that sit under a page title and tell readers whether the page is new, recently updated, or deprecated. They give visitors a signal about how current the content is before they read a single paragraph, which helps them decide whether to trust the instructions or look for a newer version.

Use badges sparingly so they stay meaningful, and keep the wording identical across pages so readers learn each label once and recognize it everywhere.

## Recommended labels

- **New** — The page was added recently.
- **Updated** — The page changed in the last release.
- **Deprecated** — The feature is going away; link to the replacement.

Add a badge near the top of the page, right under the title. Keep the wording identical everywhere so readers learn what each label means.
