Single sign-on (SSO) lets your users authenticate through an identity provider you already use, like Okta or Google.

## Setting it up

SSO is an enterprise feature. Once it's enabled on your account, go to the SSO settings to connect your provider.

The steps are roughly:

1. Choose your provider from the list.
2. Copy the metadata URL we show you into your provider.
3. Paste the provider details back into the settings form and save.

We support SAML and OIDC. The exact configuration varies by provider, so check the setup instructions for the one you use.

<Warning>
Once SSO is enforced, password login is disabled for everyone. Make sure it works before you turn on enforcement, otherwise you could lock yourself out. Contact support if that happens.
</Warning>
