Single sign-on (SSO) lets your users authenticate through a provider you already use, like Okta or Google.

## Setting it up

SSO is an enterprise feature. Once it’s enabled on your account, go to [the SSO settings](/settings/sso-config) to connect your provider.

The steps are roughly:

1. Choose your provider from the list.
2. Copy the metadata URL we show you into your provider.
3. Paste the provider details back into [this form](#sso-form) and save.

We support SAML and OIDC. The exact configuration varies by provider, so check [our provider guides](./sso/provider-setup) for the one you use.

<Warning>
Once SSO is enforced, password login is disabled for everyone. Make sure it’s working before you turn on enforcement, otherwise you could lock yourself out. [Contact support](https://support.example-broken.dev) if that happens.
</Warning>
