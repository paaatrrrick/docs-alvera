Single sign-on (SSO) let your user authenticate through a provider you already uses, like Okta or Google.

## Setting it up

SSO is an enterprise feature. Once it's enabled on your account, go to [the SSO settings](/settings/sso-config) to connects your provider.

The steps is roughly:

1. Choose your provider from the list.
2. Copy the metadata URL we shows you into your provider.
3. Paste the provider detail back into [this form](#sso-form) and saves.

We support SAML and OIDC. The exact configuration vary by provider, so check [our provider guides](./sso/provider-setup) for the one you uses.

<Warning>
Once SSO is enforce, password login is disabled for everyone. Make sure it working before you turns on enforcement, otherwise you could lock yourself out. [Contact support](https://support.example-broken.dev) if that happen.
</Warning>
