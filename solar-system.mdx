---

The solar system is the Sun and everything bound to it by gravity. It formed about 4.6 billion years ago from a collapsing cloud of gas and dust.

## The Sun

The Sun is a star at the center of the solar system. It holds more than 99.8% of the solar system's total mass.

## Planets

Eight planets orbit the Sun. In order from the Sun:

1. **Mercury**: The smallest planet and the closest to the Sun.
2. **Venus**: The hottest planet, wrapped in a thick carbon dioxide atmosphere.
3. **Earth**: The only planet known to support life.
4. **Mars**: A cold, rocky planet with the largest volcano in the solar system.
5. **Jupiter**: The largest planet, a gas giant with a persistent storm called the Great Red Spot.
6. **Saturn**: A gas giant known for its bright ring system.
7. **Uranus**: An ice giant that rotates on its side.
8. **Neptune**: The farthest planet, an ice giant with the fastest winds in the solar system.

## Other bodies

The solar system also includes dwarf planets such as Pluto, asteroids in the belt between Mars and Jupiter, and comets from the Kuiper Belt and Oort Cloud.
