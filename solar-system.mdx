## Overview

The solar system is the Sun and every object bound to it by gravity. It formed about 4.6 billion years ago from the collapse of a cloud of gas and dust. The Sun holds more than 99.8% of the system's total mass.

## Planets

Eight planets orbit the Sun. The four inner planets are small and rocky. The four outer planets are much larger and made mostly of gas or ice.

## Dwarf planets and small bodies

The International Astronomical Union recognizes five dwarf planets: Ceres, Pluto, Haumea, Makemake, and Eris. Ceres sits in the asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter. The others orbit beyond Neptune in the Kuiper belt and scattered disc.

Comets come from the Kuiper belt and the distant Oort cloud. When a comet nears the Sun, its ice vaporizes and forms a glowing tail.
