## Overview

The solar system is the Sun and every object bound to it by gravity. It formed about 4.6 billion years ago from the collapse of a giant molecular cloud. The Sun holds about 99.86% of the system's total mass.

## Planets

Eight planets orbit the Sun. The four inner planets are small, rocky worlds. The four outer planets are giants made mostly of gas or ice.

## Smaller bodies

- **Dwarf planets**: Pluto, Ceres, Eris, Haumea, and Makemake.
- **Asteroid belt**: A region of rocky bodies between Mars and Jupiter.
- **Kuiper belt**: A disk of icy bodies beyond Neptune.
- **Comets**: Icy bodies that release gas and dust as they approach the Sun.
