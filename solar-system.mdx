---
title: The solar system
description: A quick overview of the Sun, its planets, and the other objects that orbit it.
---

The solar system is the Sun and everything bound to it by gravity. It formed about 4.6 billion years ago from a collapsing cloud of gas and dust.

## The Sun

The Sun is a star at the center of the solar system. It holds more than 99.8% of the solar system's total mass.

## Planets

Eight planets orbit the Sun. In order from the Sun, they are:

| Planet | Type | Known moons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mercury | Rocky | 0 |
| Venus | Rocky | 0 |
| Earth | Rocky | 1 |
| Mars | Rocky | 2 |
| Jupiter | Gas giant | 95+ |
| Saturn | Gas giant | 140+ |
| Uranus | Ice giant | 28 |
| Neptune | Ice giant | 16 |

The four inner planets are small and rocky. The four outer planets are much larger and made mostly of gas and ice.

## Other objects

- **Dwarf planets**: Pluto, Ceres, Eris, Haumea, and Makemake.
- **Asteroid belt**: A region of rocky bodies between Mars and Jupiter.
- **Kuiper belt**: A band of icy bodies beyond Neptune.
- **Comets**: Icy bodies that grow bright tails as they approach the Sun.
