## Overview

The solar system is the Sun and every object bound to it by gravity. It formed about 4.6 billion years ago from the collapse of a cloud of gas and dust. The Sun holds more than 99.8% of the system's total mass.

## Planets

Eight planets orbit the Sun. The four inner planets are rocky, and the four outer planets are giants.

| Planet  | Type        | Known moons |
| ------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| Mercury | Terrestrial | 0           |
| Venus   | Terrestrial | 0           |
| Earth   | Terrestrial | 1           |
| Mars    | Terrestrial | 2           |
| Jupiter | Gas giant   | 95+         |
| Saturn  | Gas giant   | 140+        |
| Uranus  | Ice giant   | 28          |
| Neptune | Ice giant   | 16          |

## Other bodies

- **Dwarf planets:** Pluto, Eris, Ceres, Haumea, and Makemake.
- **Asteroid belt:** A region of rocky bodies between Mars and Jupiter.
- **Kuiper belt:** A region of icy bodies beyond Neptune.
- **Comets:** Icy bodies that release gas and dust when they approach the Sun.
