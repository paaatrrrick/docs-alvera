## Overview

The solar system is the gravitationally bound system of the Sun and everything that orbits it. It formed about 4.6 billion years ago from the collapse of a giant molecular cloud.

## The Sun

The Sun is a G-type main-sequence star at the center of the solar system. It holds more than 99.8% of the system's total mass.

## Planets

The solar system has eight planets, listed here in order from the Sun:

| Planet | Type | Notable feature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mercury | Terrestrial | Smallest planet and closest to the Sun |
| Venus | Terrestrial | Hottest planet due to a thick carbon dioxide atmosphere |
| Earth | Terrestrial | Only known planet to support life |
| Mars | Terrestrial | Home to Olympus Mons, the tallest volcano in the solar system |
| Jupiter | Gas giant | Largest planet, with the Great Red Spot storm |
| Saturn | Gas giant | Known for its extensive ring system |
| Uranus | Ice giant | Rotates on its side |
| Neptune | Ice giant | Has the fastest winds in the solar system |

## Smaller bodies

Beyond the planets, the solar system contains dwarf planets such as Pluto and Ceres, the asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter, the Kuiper belt beyond Neptune, and the distant Oort cloud.
