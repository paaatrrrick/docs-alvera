The solar system is the Sun and every object bound to it by gravity. It formed about 4.6 billion years ago from a collapsing cloud of gas and dust.

## The Sun

The Sun is a G-type main-sequence star at the center of the solar system. It holds more than 99.8% of the solar system's total mass.

## Planets

Eight planets orbit the Sun. In order of distance from the Sun:

| Planet | Type | Known moons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mercury | Terrestrial | 0 |
| Venus | Terrestrial | 0 |
| Earth | Terrestrial | 1 |
| Mars | Terrestrial | 2 |
| Jupiter | Gas giant | 95 |
| Saturn | Gas giant | 146 |
| Uranus | Ice giant | 28 |
| Neptune | Ice giant | 16 |

## Other bodies

- **Dwarf planets:** Pluto, Ceres, Eris, Haumea, and Makemake.
- **Asteroid belt:** A region of rocky bodies between Mars and Jupiter.
- **Kuiper belt:** A region of icy bodies beyond Neptune.
- **Comets:** Icy bodies that release gas and dust as they approach the Sun.
