## Overview

The solar system formed about 4.6 billion years ago from a collapsing cloud of gas and dust. It consists of the Sun and every object bound to it by gravity, including planets, moons, dwarf planets, asteroids, and comets.

## The Sun

The Sun is a G-type main-sequence star. It holds about 99.8% of the solar system's total mass.

## Planets

The solar system has eight planets. The four inner planets are rocky, and the four outer planets are giants.

| Planet  | Type       | Known moons |
| ------- | ---------- | ----------- |
| Mercury | Terrestrial | 0          |
| Venus   | Terrestrial | 0          |
| Earth   | Terrestrial | 1          |
| Mars    | Terrestrial | 2          |
| Jupiter | Gas giant   | 95         |

| Uranus  | Ice giant   | 28         |
| Neptune | Ice giant   | 16         |

## Other objects

- **Dwarf planets:** Pluto, Eris, Ceres, Haumea, and Makemake.
- **Asteroid belt:** A region of rocky bodies between Mars and Jupiter.
- **Kuiper belt:** A disk of icy bodies beyond Neptune.
- **Oort cloud:** A distant, theorized shell of icy objects that is the source of long-period comets.
