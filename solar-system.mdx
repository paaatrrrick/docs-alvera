---
title: Solar system
description: An overview of the Sun and the bodies that orbit it.
---

The solar system is the Sun and everything bound to it by gravity. It formed about 4.6 billion years ago from a collapsing cloud of gas and dust.

## The Sun

The Sun is a G-type main-sequence star. It holds more than 99.8% of the solar system's total mass.

## Planets

Eight planets orbit the Sun. In order from the Sun:

| Planet  | Type        |
| ------- | ----------- |
| Mercury | Terrestrial |
| Venus   | Terrestrial |
| Earth   | Terrestrial |
| Mars    | Terrestrial |
| Jupiter | Gas giant   |
| Saturn  | Gas giant   |
| Uranus  | Ice giant   |
| Neptune | Ice giant   |

## Other bodies

The solar system also contains dwarf planets such as Pluto, moons, asteroids in the main belt between Mars and Jupiter, and comets from the Kuiper Belt and Oort Cloud.
