## Overview

Legendary Pokémon are rare, powerful creatures that anchor the lore of each main-series game. Mythical Pokémon are a related category, usually distributed through special events rather than found in the wild.

## Legendaries by generation

Each generation introduces a small group of Legendaries tied to its region and story.

| Gen III    | Hoenn  | Regirock, Regice, Registeel, Kyogre, Groudon, Rayquaza |
| Gen IV     | Sinnoh | Dialga, Palkia, Giratina, Arceus                   |
| Gen V      | Unova  | Reshiram, Zekrom, Kyurem                           |

## Notable Mythicals

Mythical Pokémon are event-exclusive and often appear in tie-in films.

- **Mew** — Psychic-type Mythical from Gen I, said to contain the DNA of every Pokémon.
- **Celebi** — Psychic/Grass Mythical from Gen II with time-travel abilities.
- **Jirachi** — Steel/Psychic Mythical from Gen III that grants wishes.
- **Darkrai** — Dark-type Mythical from Gen IV that induces nightmares.
- **Genesect** — Bug/Steel Mythical from Gen V, revived and modified by Team Plasma.
- **Magearna** — Steel/Fairy Mythical from Gen VI, built 500 years before its debut.

## Signature moves

Many Legendaries have exclusive moves that reflect their lore.

- **Mewtwo** — Psystrike, a Psychic move that hits the target's Defense stat.
- **Rayquaza** — Dragon Ascent, a powerful Flying-type move used to Mega Evolve.
- **Arceus** — Judgment, a Normal move whose type shifts with the held Plate.
- **Dialga** — Roar of Time, a Dragon move that forces a recharge turn.
- **Palkia** — Spacial Rend, a high-crit Dragon move.
- **Giratina** — Shadow Force, a two-turn Ghost move that bypasses protection.
