## Overview

Legendary Pokémon are rare, powerful creatures that typically appear once per game and are tied to a region's mythology. Mythical Pokémon are similar but are usually distributed through special events rather than found in the wild. Together they form the most iconic corner of the Pokédex.

## Catching Legendaries

Most Legendaries are one-shot encounters at fixed locations near the end of the main story. A few tips:

- Save before the encounter so you can retry if it faints or you run out of Poké Balls.
- Bring status moves like Sleep or Paralyze to raise catch rates.
- Ultra Balls, Timer Balls (in long battles), and Dusk Balls (at night or in caves) are usually the best catch options.
- In games with a shiny lock, Legendaries cannot appear shiny — check the generation's rules before soft-resetting.

## Beyond the main games

Legendaries appear across the wider franchise:

- **Anime** — Featured in movies almost every year, from *Mewtwo Strikes Back* to *Secrets of the Jungle*.
- **Trading Card Game** — Frequently printed as high-rarity cards and headline expansion sets.
- **Pokémon GO** — Distributed through raid battles and special research tasks.
