## Overview

Legendary and Mythical Pokémon are rare creatures tied to the history, geography, or mythology of the Pokémon world. They often anchor a game's central story or reward exploration.

## How they differ

- **Legendary Pokémon** are part of a game's world and are commonly encountered during story progression or exploration.
- **Mythical Pokémon** are usually distributed through special events or limited releases.

This distinction describes how a Pokémon is obtained and presented. It does not define a single battle type.

## Roles in the games

Legendary and Mythical Pokémon can serve several narrative and gameplay roles:

- A **story catalyst** can drive the conflict or explain a region's history.
- An **optional encounter** can reward exploration after the main story.
- An **event distribution** can make a Mythical Pokémon available through a limited promotion or special release.

Availability and encounter requirements vary by game.

## Battle considerations

These Pokémon often have distinctive abilities, signature moves, or strong base statistics. Competitive formats can restrict their use through species, team, or event rules. Check the rules for the format you are playing before building a team around one.
