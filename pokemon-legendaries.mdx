## Overview

Legendary and mythical Pokémon are rare, powerful Pokémon that often play central roles in the story of a main-series game. Most of them can't breed, and you can usually catch only one of each per save file.

For a general introduction to the franchise, see the [Pokémon](/pokemon) page.

## Legendary vs. mythical

| Category  | How you get them                                                  | Examples                       |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Legendary | Found in the game world, often as part of the main story or postgame | Mewtwo, Lugia, Rayquaza        |
| Mythical  | Usually distributed through official events or special promotions | Mew, Celebi, Jirachi, Darkrai  |

## Box legendaries

Many main-series games feature a legendary Pokémon on the box art. This box legendary usually appears at the climax of the story.

- **Pokémon Gold and Silver:** Ho-Oh and Lugia
- **Pokémon Ruby and Sapphire:** Groudon and Kyogre
- **Pokémon Diamond and Pearl:** Dialga and Palkia
- **Pokémon Black and White:** Reshiram and Zekrom
- **Pokémon Scarlet and Violet:** Koraidon and Miraidon

## Legendary trios

Several generations introduce a trio of related legendaries that share a theme.

- **Legendary birds:** Articuno, Zapdos, and Moltres
- **Legendary beasts:** Raikou, Entei, and Suicune
- **Lake guardians:** Uxie, Mesprit, and Azelf
- **Swords of Justice:** Cobalion, Terrakion, and Virizion
