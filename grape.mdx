## Overview

The grape (*Vitis vinifera*) is a berry that grows in clusters on woody vines. People first cultivated grapes around 6,000 to 8,000 years ago in the Near East, and grapes are now grown on every continent except Antarctica.

## Popular varieties

| Variety            | Flavor        | Best for          |
| ------------------ | ------------- | ----------------- |
| Thompson Seedless  | Sweet, mild   | Snacking, raisins |
| Concord            | Bold, musky   | Juice, jelly      |
| Cotton Candy       | Very sweet    | Snacking          |
| Cabernet Sauvignon | Dark, tannic  | Red wine          |
| Chardonnay         | Crisp, fruity | White wine        |

## Nutrition

One cup of grapes contains about 104 calories and 1 gram of dietary fiber. Grapes provide vitamin K, vitamin C, and antioxidants such as resveratrol.

## Culinary uses

- **Beverages**: wine and grape juice
- **Dried**: raisins, sultanas, and currants
- **Preserves**: jelly and jam
- **Raw**: eaten fresh or frozen as a snack
