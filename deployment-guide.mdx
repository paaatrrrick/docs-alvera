Once you're happy with how things look locally, it's time to deploy.

## Connecting your repo

First you connect your git repository. We support GitHub and GitLab, and the setup is slightly different for each.

After it's connected, every push to your main branch triggers a new deploy automatically.

## Custom domains

You can point a custom domain at your site. Add the domain in settings and then update your DNS records.

<Tip>
Deploys usually take less than a minute, but a big site can take longer. You can watch the progress in the deploy log.
</Tip>

If a deploy fails, check the deploy log for errors.
