## Why measuring page size is tricky

When a customer asks "what is our page size?", we do not have a good answer, and we cannot improve what we do not measure. This doc sets out to answer that question.

- Customers have wildly different page sizes.
- Local, staging, prod, and incognito prod all give different sizes.
- The tool you use to measure page size changes the result.
- The metric you use to define page size matters.

## Experiment

Most customers interpret page size by looking at the Network tab, so that is what we should measure — specifically the transferred amount.

We measured 170 customer URLs (31 Enterprise and 139 Pro) using [experte.com](https://www.experte.com/).

### Results

| Metric          | All    | Pro    | Enterprise |
| --------------- | ------ | ------ | ---------- |
| Count           | 170.00 | 139.00 | 31.00      |
| Mean (MB)       | 2.21   | 2.15   | 2.52       |
| 10th percentile | 1.34   | 1.33   | 1.38       |
| 25th percentile | 1.38   | 1.37   | 1.53       |
| 50th percentile | 1.56   | 1.52   | 1.68       |
| 75th percentile | 2.26   | 2.16   | 2.78       |
| 90th percentile | 3.69   | 3.61   | 3.67       |
| 97th percentile | 8.79   | 8.56   | 7.43       |

**Misc details:**

- We chose experte because its numbers were nearly identical to an incognito Network tab. Other tools, such as local headless browsers, underreported page size.
- We randomly pulled pages from Mongo, then found their URLs. We filtered out preview subdomains and similar cases.
- We excluded Hobby plans because of the high density of unused pages.

## Takeaways

**Most customers have a page size just below 2 MB.** It varies a lot.

About 70% of pages are under 2 MB — not horrific, but not great. After that, page size skyrockets.

To drop page size, we should target two things:

- **A:** Get the 2 MB baseline down.
- **B:** Shrink the largest 30% of pages.

We have done a lot of A (removing shiki, crypto-js), but we need more of B.

## Popular customers have large page sizes

Many pages over 2.5 MB belong to our most popular customers — Anthropic, Lovable, Auth0, Coinbase, Pinecone, and even Mintlify. When pages get that large, higher churn would not be surprising.

## Next steps

- Cut down the size of most pages and the top 15%.
- Define clear goals for these numbers.
- Consider a scheduled job that measures every page weekly.
