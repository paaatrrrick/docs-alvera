We work hard to keep the service running, but sometimes things break. This page tells you where to look when that happens.

## The status page

Our status page shows the current health of every component. If something is down, it appears there, usually within a few minutes.

You can subscribe to updates so you get an email or SMS when an incident starts or resolves. Just select "Subscribe" on the status page.

## Incident history

We post a postmortem after every major incident. It explains what happened and what we did to prevent it from happening again. You can read them in the incident archive on the status page.

For your own uptime monitoring, our health endpoint returns a simple ok response you can ping.
