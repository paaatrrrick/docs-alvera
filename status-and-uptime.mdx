We work hard to keep the service running, but sometimes things break. This page tells you where to look when that happens.

## The status page

Our [status page](https://status.example-broken.dev) shows the current health of every component. If something is down, it will be shown there, usually within a few minutes.

You can subscribe to updates so you get an email or SMS when an incident starts or resolves. Just click "Subscribe" on [the status page](#subscribe-to-updates).

## Incident history

We post a postmortem after every major incident, explaining what happened and what we do to prevent it again. Read them in [the incident archive](./status/past-incidents).

For your own uptime monitoring, our [health endpoint](/api/health-check) returns a simple ok response you can ping.
