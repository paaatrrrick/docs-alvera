## Overview

The music of Star Wars is one of the most recognizable film scores in cinema history. Composer John Williams wrote the orchestral scores for all nine Skywalker saga films, from *A New Hope* (1977) through *The Rise of Skywalker* (2019). The London Symphony Orchestra recorded the original trilogy scores, giving the saga its sweeping, symphonic sound.

## Signature themes

Williams built the score around recurring leitmotifs. Each theme is tied to a character, faction, or idea, and returns whenever that element appears on screen.

| Theme | Associated with | First appearance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main Title | The Star Wars saga | *A New Hope* (1977) |
| The Imperial March | Darth Vader and the Empire | *The Empire Strikes Back* (1980) |
| The Force Theme | The Force, Luke's destiny | *A New Hope* (1977) |
| Princess Leia's Theme | Leia Organa | *A New Hope* (1977) |
| Yoda's Theme | Yoda and the Jedi | *The Empire Strikes Back* (1980) |
| Duel of the Fates | The prequel-era Jedi/Sith conflict | *The Phantom Menace* (1999) |
| Across the Stars | Anakin and Padmé | *Attack of the Clones* (2002) |
| Rey's Theme | Rey | *The Force Awakens* (2015) |

## How the score works

- **Leitmotifs**: Williams reuses short melodic ideas so the audience recognizes characters and themes on hearing, not just on sight.
- **Orchestration**: Brass carries villains and military power, strings carry romance and heroism, and woodwinds carry mystery and the Force.
- **Diegetic music**: A few pieces play inside the story itself, such as the Cantina Band's "Mad About Me" on Tatooine and Max Rebo Band's numbers in Jabba's palace.

## Composers beyond Williams

Other composers have scored spin-off films and series while keeping Williams's themes as a foundation:

- **Michael Giacchino** scored *Rogue One: A Star Wars Story* (2016).
- **John Powell** scored *Solo: A Star Wars Story* (2018), with Williams contributing "The Adventures of Han."
- **Ludwig Göransson** scored *The Mandalorian* and *The Book of Boba Fett*.
- **Kevin Kiner** scored *The Clone Wars*, *Rebels*, and *Ahsoka*.
- **Nicholas Britell** scored *Andor*.

## Recordings and releases

The original soundtracks have been re-released many times, including expanded editions that add unused cues and alternate takes. Live-to-picture concert tours, such as *Star Wars: In Concert* and *Star Wars: A Musical Journey*, have brought the score to audiences worldwide with a full orchestra performing alongside film footage.
