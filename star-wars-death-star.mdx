# Death Star

The Death Star is a moon-sized battle station built by the Galactic Empire. Its superlaser can destroy an entire planet with a single blast.

## Construction

The station's design began during the Clone Wars, based on plans developed by the Geonosians. The Empire completed it in secret under the oversight of Director Orson Krennic and Grand Moff Tarkin.

## The superlaser

The superlaser draws its power from massive kyber crystals. Several beams converge into one, and the combined blast can shatter a planet.

## Destruction of Alderaan

Grand Moff Tarkin ordered the Death Star to fire on Alderaan as a demonstration of the Empire's power. The planet was destroyed, along with billions of its inhabitants.

## Battle of Yavin

Rebel spies stole the station's plans, which revealed a small thermal exhaust port. Luke Skywalker fired proton torpedoes into the port and destroyed the first Death Star.

## Second Death Star

The Empire began a larger second Death Star above the forest moon of Endor. Rebel forces destroyed it during the Battle of Endor before construction was complete.
