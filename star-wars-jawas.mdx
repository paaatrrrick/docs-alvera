# Jawas

Jawas are a short, hooded species native to the desert world of Tatooine. They are known for scavenging scrap, salvaging droids, and trading their finds across the Dune Sea.

## Appearance

Jawas stand about one meter tall and wear rough brown robes with deep hoods. Only their glowing yellow eyes are visible beneath the hood, and they carry a distinctive odor.

## Sandcrawlers

Jawa clans travel in sandcrawlers, massive treaded vehicles left over from early mining operations. Each sandcrawler serves as a mobile home, workshop, and storefront.

## Trade and salvage

Jawas collect broken droids, machinery, and scrap from the desert. They repair what they can and sell it to moisture farmers and settlers. Buyers often find that a freshly sold droid needs more repair than promised.

## Language

Jawas speak Jawaese, a fast and high-pitched language. They also use a trade dialect that relies partly on scent to convey mood and meaning.

## Notable appearances

In *A New Hope*, a Jawa clan captures R2-D2 and C-3PO and sells them to Owen Lars. This sale sets Luke Skywalker on the path to joining the Rebellion.
