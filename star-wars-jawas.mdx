# Jawas

Jawas are a short, hooded species native to the desert world of Tatooine. They are known for scavenging scrap and droids from the dunes and reselling them to moisture farmers and settlers.

## Appearance

Jawas wear rough brown robes with hoods that hide their faces. Only their glowing yellow eyes are visible in the shadows beneath the hood.

## Sandcrawlers

Jawa clans travel the Dune Sea aboard sandcrawlers. These massive, treaded vehicles serve as mobile homes, workshops, and storefronts. Each sandcrawler carries salvaged droids and parts that the clan repairs and sells.

## Trade and language

Jawas speak Jawaese, a rapid language that mixes spoken words with scent to convey meaning. They are shrewd traders and often haggle hard over the price of a droid.

## Notable appearances

In *A New Hope*, a Jawa clan captures R2-D2 and C-3PO and sells them to Owen Lars. That sale brings the droids to Luke Skywalker and sets the saga in motion.
