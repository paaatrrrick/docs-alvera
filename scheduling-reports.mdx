Instead of checking the dashboard every day, you can schedule a report and we email it to you.

## Creating a schedule

Go to [the reports page](/settings/scheduled-reports) and pick what you want in the report, then choose how often — daily, weekly, or monthly.

The report arrives as a PDF and a CSV, so you can share it or dig into the numbers. See [the report format](./reports/format-reference) for what each column means.

<Tip>
You can send a report to multiple people, just add their emails. There’s no limit on how many schedules you make.
</Tip>

If a report doesn’t arrive, check your spam folder first, then [the delivery troubleshooting](#delivery-issues).
