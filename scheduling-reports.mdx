Instead of checking the dashboard every day, you can schedule a report and receive it by email.

## Creating a schedule

Go to the reports page and pick what you want in the report. Then choose how often: daily, weekly, or monthly.

The report arrives as a PDF and a CSV, so you can share it or dig into the numbers.

<Tip>
You can send a report to multiple people by adding their emails. There's no limit on how many schedules you create.
</Tip>

If a report doesn't arrive, check your spam folder first.
