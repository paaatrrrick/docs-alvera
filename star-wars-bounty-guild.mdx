# The Bounty Hunters Guild

The Bounty Hunters Guild sets the rules of the trade, assigning contracts and enforcing a code among its members.

## The code

The Guild expects members to honor their contracts and avoid undercutting one another. Even so, members often bend the rules.

## Pucks and contracts

The Guild distributes bounties as tracking pucks that contain a target profile and the promised reward for capture or elimination.
