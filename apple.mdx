## Overview

The apple (*Malus domestica*) is a fruit from a deciduous tree in the rose family. Apple trees originated in Central Asia, where their wild ancestor, *Malus sieversii*, still grows today.

## Popular varieties

| Variety      | Flavor          | Best for             |
| ------------ | --------------- | -------------------- |
| Granny Smith | Tart, crisp     | Baking, snacking     |
| Honeycrisp   | Sweet, juicy    | Snacking             |
| Fuji         | Very sweet      | Snacking, salads     |
| Gala         | Mild, sweet     | Snacking, sauces     |
| Braeburn     | Sweet-tart      | Baking, cider        |

## Nutrition

A medium apple contains about 95 calories and 4 grams of dietary fiber. Apples also provide vitamin C and antioxidants such as quercetin.

## Culinary uses

- **Baking**: pies, crumbles, and tarts
- **Beverages**: juice and cider
- **Preserves**: applesauce and apple butter
- **Raw**: sliced in salads or eaten whole
