## Overview

The Clone Wars were a pan-galactic conflict fought between the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems (CIS). The war began at the First Battle of Geonosis in 22 BBY and ended three years later with the issuing of Order 66 and the transformation of the Republic into the Galactic Empire.

## The two sides

- **Galactic Republic**: Led by Supreme Chancellor Palpatine and defended by the Jedi Order commanding the newly created Grand Army of the Republic.
- **Confederacy of Independent Systems**: A separatist coalition led by Count Dooku and backed by the Trade Federation, the Techno Union, and the Commerce Guild. Its forces were built around Battle Droid armies.

## Notable battles

- **First Battle of Geonosis (22 BBY)**: The opening engagement that revealed the clone army and triggered the war.
- **Battle of Christophsis**: Anakin Skywalker was first paired with his new Padawan, Ahsoka Tano.
- **Battle of Umbara**: A brutal ground campaign under the temporary command of Jedi General Pong Krell.
- **Battle of Ryloth**: Republic forces liberated the Twi'lek homeworld from Separatist occupation.
- **Siege of Mandalore**: Ahsoka Tano and Captain Rex led the 332nd Company against Maul's forces.
- **Battle of Coruscant**: The climactic Separatist assault on the Republic capital and the rescue of Chancellor Palpatine.

## The clone troopers

The Grand Army of the Republic was made up of clone troopers grown on Kamino from the genetic template of bounty hunter Jango Fett. Each clone had accelerated growth and standardized combat training. Behavioral modification biochips implanted in every clone would later carry out Order 66.

## Legacy

The war was engineered from both sides by Darth Sidious as a pretext to consolidate power. Its conclusion delivered emergency authority to Palpatine, ended the Jedi Order, and produced the Galactic Empire that would rule the galaxy for the next two decades.
