## Overview

The Clone Wars were a three-year, pan-galactic conflict fought between the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems (CIS). Waged from 22 BBY to 19 BBY, the war reshaped the galaxy and set the stage for the rise of the Galactic Empire.

## Origins

The conflict was engineered by Darth Sidious as part of a long plan to destroy the Jedi Order and consolidate power. Key precipitating events include:

- The secret commissioning of a clone army on Kamino.
- Count Dooku's formation of the Separatist Alliance.
- The Battle of Geonosis, widely considered the war's opening engagement.

## Sides of the conflict

| Faction | Leader | Military |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Galactic Republic | Supreme Chancellor Palpatine | Grand Army of the Republic (clone troopers) |
| Confederacy of Independent Systems | Count Dooku | Droid armies of the Separatist Alliance |

## Notable figures

- **Obi-Wan Kenobi** — Jedi General known for daring campaigns and diplomacy.
- **Anakin Skywalker** — "The Hero With No Fear," whose exploits made him a legend.
- **Ahsoka Tano** — Anakin's Padawan and a rising commander.
- **General Grievous** — Cyborg Supreme Commander of the Separatist droid armies.
- **Captain Rex** — Clone officer of the 501st Legion.

## Key battles

- **Battle of Geonosis** — Opening engagement that revealed the clone army.
- **Battle of Christophsis** — Early Republic victory led by Anakin and Obi-Wan.
- **Siege of Ryloth** — Liberation of the Twi'lek homeworld.
- **Battle of Umbara** — Grueling ground campaign on a shadowy world.
- **Siege of Mandalore** — Final major operation, ending as Order 66 was issued.

## Legacy

The war ended with the execution of Order 66, the near-extermination of the Jedi Order, and the transformation of the Republic into the Galactic Empire. Its veterans, tactics, and unresolved tensions echoed through the decades that followed, shaping the Rebellion and the wider saga.
