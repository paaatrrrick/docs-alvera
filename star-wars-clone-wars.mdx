# The Clone Wars

The Clone Wars was a three-year conflict fought between the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems. The Republic deployed a clone army bred on Kamino, while the Separatists relied on vast droid armies.

## Origins

The war began with the Battle of Geonosis, where Jedi and clone troopers clashed with Separatist droid forces for the first time. Darth Sidious secretly orchestrated the conflict and controlled both sides.

## Key campaigns

Major theaters of the war included the Outer Rim Sieges, the defense of Kamino, and the Battle of Coruscant. Jedi generals led clone forces across hundreds of worlds.

## Aftermath

The war ended with Order 66 and the fall of the Jedi Order. Chancellor Palpatine used the conflict to consolidate power and declare himself Emperor, transforming the Republic into the Galactic Empire.
