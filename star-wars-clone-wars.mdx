# Clone Wars

The Clone Wars were a pan-galactic conflict fought between the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems. The war lasted roughly three years and reshaped the galaxy, paving the way for the rise of the Empire.

## Origins

Growing dissatisfaction with the Republic led thousands of star systems to secede under the leadership of Count Dooku. The discovery of a clone army on Kamino gave the Republic the military force it needed to respond.

## Key battles

- **Battle of Geonosis** — the opening engagement, where Jedi and clone troopers first fought together.
- **Battle of Christophsis** — an early Republic victory led by Anakin Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi.
- **Battle of Umbara** — a grueling ground campaign that tested the loyalty of clone troopers.
- **Siege of Mandalore** — one of the final campaigns, led by Ahsoka Tano against Maul.

## The clone army

Cloned from the bounty hunter Jango Fett, the clone troopers were bred on Kamino for combat. Each was implanted with an inhibitor chip that would later be used to execute Order 66.

## Legacy

The war was engineered by Darth Sidious to consolidate power. Its end brought the fall of the Jedi Order and the birth of the Galactic Empire.
