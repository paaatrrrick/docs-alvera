Octopuses are some of the most unusual animals in the ocean. This page collects a few facts about them as sample content.

## Three hearts and blue blood

An octopus has three hearts. Two pump blood through the gills, and the third pumps it to the rest of the body.

Their blood is blue because it uses a copper-based protein called hemocyanin to carry oxygen.

## Arms that think

Most of an octopus's neurons sit in its arms, not its central brain. Each arm can explore and react to its surroundings on its own.

## Masters of disguise

Octopuses change color and skin texture in a fraction of a second. They use special skin cells called chromatophores to blend into rocks, coral and sand.
