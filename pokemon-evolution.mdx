title: "Pokémon evolution"
description: "How Pokémon evolve, the common evolution methods, and well-known evolution lines"
---

Evolution is the process by which a Pokémon changes into a different, usually stronger species. An evolved Pokémon typically gains higher base stats, new moves, and sometimes a new type.

## Evolution methods

| Method       | How it works                                                   | Example                          |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Level up     | The Pokémon reaches a specific level                           | Charmander evolves at level 16   |
| Evolution stone | The Trainer uses a stone item on the Pokémon                | Pikachu evolves with a Thunder Stone |
| Trade        | The Pokémon evolves after being traded to another Trainer      | Kadabra evolves into Alakazam    |
| Friendship   | The Pokémon levels up while it has high friendship             | Golbat evolves into Crobat       |
| Location     | The Pokémon levels up in a specific area                       | Eevee evolves into Leafeon near a Moss Rock |
| Time of day  | The Pokémon levels up during day or night                      | Eevee evolves into Umbreon at night |

## Branching evolutions

Some Pokémon can evolve into more than one species. The method you use determines the result. Eevee has eight evolutions: Vaporeon, Jolteon, Flareon, Espeon, Umbreon, Leafeon, Glaceon, and Sylveon.

## Mega Evolution

Mega Evolution debuted in Pokémon X and Y. It temporarily transforms a fully evolved Pokémon during battle. The Pokémon must hold its matching Mega Stone, and the Trainer can use Mega Evolution once per battle.

## Stopping evolution

You can cancel an evolution by pressing the B button while the animation plays. Giving a Pokémon an Everstone prevents it from evolving entirely.

## Well-known evolution lines

- **Bulbasaur → Ivysaur → Venusaur**: the Grass-type starter line from Kanto
- **Charmander → Charmeleon → Charizard**: the Fire-type starter line from Kanto
- **Squirtle → Wartortle → Blastoise**: the Water-type starter line from Kanto
- **Pichu → Pikachu → Raichu**: the Electric-type line featuring the franchise mascot
- **Magikarp → Gyarados**: a weak Water-type that evolves into a powerful Water/Flying-type
