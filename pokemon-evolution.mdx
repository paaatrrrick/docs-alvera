## Overview

Evolution is the process by which a Pokémon changes into a different, usually stronger species. An evolved Pokémon often gains higher base stats, new moves, and sometimes a new type. For a general introduction to the franchise, see [Pokémon](/pokemon).

## Evolution methods

Each species evolves under specific conditions. The most common methods are listed below.

| Method     | How it works                                             | Example                          |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Level up   | The Pokémon reaches a set level                          | Charmander to Charmeleon at 16   |
| Stone      | You use an evolution stone on the Pokémon                | Pikachu to Raichu with Thunder Stone |
| Trade      | You trade the Pokémon to another player                  | Kadabra to Alakazam              |
| Held item  | The Pokémon holds an item while being traded or leveling | Onix to Steelix with Metal Coat  |
| Friendship | The Pokémon levels up with high friendship               | Golbat to Crobat                 |
| Location   | The Pokémon levels up in a specific area                 | Eevee to Leafeon near a Moss Rock |
| Time of day | The Pokémon levels up during the day or night           | Eevee to Umbreon at night with high friendship |

## Evolution stages

Most evolution families have one, two, or three stages.

- **Single stage**: The Pokémon does not evolve, such as Tauros or Lapras.
- **Two stages**: The Pokémon evolves once, such as Pikachu into Raichu.
- **Three stages**: The Pokémon evolves twice, such as Bulbasaur into Ivysaur into Venusaur.

Some families also include a baby Pokémon. Baby Pokémon hatch from Eggs and evolve into the base form, such as Pichu into Pikachu.

## Branching evolutions

Some Pokémon can evolve into more than one species. Eevee is the best-known example, with eight possible evolutions:

| Evolution | Type     |
| --------- | -------- |
| Vaporeon  | Water    |
| Jolteon   | Electric |
| Flareon   | Fire     |
| Espeon    | Psychic  |
| Umbreon   | Dark     |
| Leafeon   | Grass    |
| Glaceon   | Ice      |
| Sylveon   | Fairy    |

## Stopping evolution

You can cancel an evolution by pressing the B button during the evolution animation. You can also give the Pokémon an Everstone to hold, which prevents it from evolving.

## Special mechanics

Later generations introduced temporary battle mechanics that work alongside regular evolution.

- **Mega Evolution** (Gen VI): Certain Pokémon holding a Mega Stone transform for the rest of a battle.
- **Z-Moves** (Gen VII): Pokémon holding a Z-Crystal can use a powerful move once per battle.
- **Dynamax and Gigantamax** (Gen VIII): Pokémon grow to giant size for three turns.
- **Terastallization** (Gen IX): Pokémon change their type to their Tera Type for the rest of a battle.
