Evolution is the process by which a Pokémon changes into a different, usually stronger species. An evolved Pokémon typically gains higher base stats and may gain a new type. For an introduction to the franchise, see [Pokémon](/pokemon).

## Evolution methods

| Method     | How it works                                         | Example                         |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Level up   | The Pokémon reaches a specific level                 | Charmander evolves at level 16  |
| Stone      | You use an evolution stone on the Pokémon            | Pikachu evolves with a Thunder Stone |
| Trade      | You trade the Pokémon to another player              | Kadabra evolves into Alakazam   |
| Friendship | The Pokémon reaches high friendship, then levels up  | Golbat evolves into Crobat      |

## Notable evolution lines

- **Bulbasaur → Ivysaur → Venusaur**: The Grass-type Kanto starter line.
- **Charmander → Charmeleon → Charizard**: Charizard gains the Flying type on its final evolution.
- **Squirtle → Wartortle → Blastoise**: The Water-type Kanto starter line.
- **Eevee**: Branches into eight different forms, including Vaporeon, Jolteon, and Flareon.

## Stopping evolution

You can cancel an evolution by pressing the B button while the animation plays. Some Trainers do this because an unevolved Pokémon can learn certain moves at earlier levels.
