## Overview

Evolution is a process in which a Pokémon changes into a different, usually stronger species. An evolved Pokémon typically gains higher base stats, a new appearance, and sometimes a new type. For franchise basics, see the main [Pokémon](/pokemon) page.

Not every Pokémon evolves. Many Legendary and Mythical Pokémon have no evolutionary relatives.

## Evolution methods

Each species evolves under specific conditions.

| Method             | How it works                                               | Example                               |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Level up           | The Pokémon reaches a set level.                           | Charmander evolves into Charmeleon at level 16. |
| Evolution stone    | You use an item such as a Fire Stone or Thunder Stone.     | Pikachu evolves into Raichu with a Thunder Stone. |
| Trade              | You trade the Pokémon to another player.                   | Kadabra evolves into Alakazam.        |
| Trade with item    | You trade the Pokémon while it holds a specific item.      | Onix evolves into Steelix while holding a Metal Coat. |
| Friendship         | The Pokémon levels up with high friendship.                | Golbat evolves into Crobat.           |
| Time of day        | The Pokémon levels up during the day or at night.          | Eevee evolves into Espeon by day or Umbreon by night with high friendship. |
| Location           | The Pokémon levels up in a specific area.                  | Eevee evolves into Leafeon near a Moss Rock. |
| Known move         | The Pokémon levels up while it knows a specific move.      | Aipom evolves into Ambipom after learning Double Hit. |

## Evolution stages

Most evolutionary lines have one, two, or three stages.

- **Basic**: The first form, such as Bulbasaur.
- **Stage 1**: The first evolution, such as Ivysaur.
- **Stage 2**: The final evolution, such as Venusaur.

Generation II introduced baby Pokémon, such as Pichu and Igglybuff. These are pre-evolutions of existing species, and most evolve through high friendship.

## Branching evolutions

Some Pokémon can evolve into more than one species, depending on the method you use.

- **Eevee**: Evolves into eight different Eeveelutions: Vaporeon, Jolteon, Flareon, Espeon, Umbreon, Leafeon, Glaceon, and Sylveon.
- **Gloom**: Evolves into Vileplume with a Leaf Stone or Bellossom with a Sun Stone.
- **Poliwhirl**: Evolves into Poliwrath with a Water Stone or Politoed when traded holding a King's Rock.
- **Tyrogue**: Evolves into Hitmonlee, Hitmonchan, or Hitmontop based on its Attack and Defense stats.

## Stopping evolution

You can cancel an evolution by pressing the B button during the evolution animation. You can also give a Pokémon an Everstone to hold, which prevents it from evolving.

## Battle transformations

Later generations added temporary transformations that only last for a single battle.

| Mechanic        | Introduced  | Description                                                                 |
| --------------- | ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mega Evolution  | Gen VI      | A Pokémon holding its Mega Stone transforms into a more powerful Mega form. |
| Primal Reversion| Gen VI      | Groudon and Kyogre revert to their ancient Primal forms.                    |
| Gigantamax      | Gen VIII    | Certain Pokémon grow huge and gain a unique appearance and G-Max Move.      |
| Terastallization| Gen IX      | A Pokémon changes its type to its Tera Type for the rest of the battle.     |
