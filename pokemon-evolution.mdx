## Overview

Evolution is the process by which a Pokémon changes into a different, usually stronger species. An evolved Pokémon typically gains higher stats, access to new moves, and sometimes a new type. For a broader introduction to the franchise, see [Pokémon](/pokemon).

## Evolution methods

Each species evolves under specific conditions.

| Method          | How it works                                                  | Example                          |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Level-up        | The Pokémon reaches a set level                               | Charmander evolves at level 16   |
| Evolution stone | The Trainer uses a stone item on the Pokémon                  | Pikachu evolves with a Thunder Stone |
| Trade           | The Pokémon is traded to another Trainer                      | Kadabra evolves into Alakazam    |
| Friendship      | The Pokémon levels up with high friendship                    | Golbat evolves into Crobat       |
| Special         | The Pokémon meets a condition such as time of day or location | Eevee evolves into Umbreon at night |

## Evolution stages

Most evolutionary lines have one, two, or three stages. Many starter Pokémon follow a three-stage line, such as Bulbasaur, Ivysaur, and Venusaur. Some Pokémon, such as Tauros, do not evolve at all.

## Branching evolution

Some Pokémon can evolve into more than one species. Eevee has eight possible evolutions:

- **Vaporeon** (Water)
- **Jolteon** (Electric)
- **Flareon** (Fire)
- **Espeon** (Psychic)
- **Umbreon** (Dark)
- **Leafeon** (Grass)
- **Glaceon** (Ice)
- **Sylveon** (Fairy)

## Stopping evolution

A Trainer can press the B button during the evolution animation to cancel it. The Pokémon then tries to evolve again the next time it meets the condition. An Everstone held by a Pokémon prevents evolution entirely.

## Temporary transformations

Some mechanics change a Pokémon only for the duration of a battle:

- **Mega Evolution**: Introduced in Gen VI, it transforms certain Pokémon using a Mega Stone.
- **Dynamax and Gigantamax**: Introduced in Gen VIII, these make a Pokémon giant for three turns.
- **Terastallization**: Introduced in Gen IX, it changes a Pokémon's type to its Tera Type.
