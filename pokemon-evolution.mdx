Evolution is the process by which a Pokémon transforms into a different, usually stronger, species. An evolved Pokémon typically gains higher stats, a new appearance, and sometimes a new type.

## Evolution methods

Pokémon evolve in several ways:

- **Level up:** The Pokémon evolves when it reaches a specific level. Charmander evolves into Charmeleon at level 16.
- **Evolution stones:** Using an item such as a Fire Stone or Thunder Stone triggers evolution. Pikachu evolves into Raichu with a Thunder Stone.
- **Trading:** Some Pokémon evolve when traded to another player. Kadabra evolves into Alakazam this way.
- **Friendship:** A Pokémon with high friendship evolves on level up. Eevee evolves into Espeon or Umbreon depending on the time of day.
- **Special conditions:** Some species require a held item, a known move, or a specific location.

## Branching evolutions

Some Pokémon can evolve into more than one species. Eevee has eight possible evolutions, known as the Eeveelutions:

| Evolution | Type |
| --- | --- |
| Vaporeon | Water |
| Jolteon | Electric |
| Flareon | Fire |
| Espeon | Psychic |
| Umbreon | Dark |
| Leafeon | Grass |
| Glaceon | Ice |
| Sylveon | Fairy |

## Stopping evolution

You can cancel an evolution by pressing the B button while the evolution animation plays. Holding an Everstone also prevents a Pokémon from evolving.

## Mega Evolution

Introduced in Pokémon X and Y, Mega Evolution is a temporary transformation that lasts for one battle. The Pokémon must hold its matching Mega Stone, and the Trainer must carry a Key Stone.
