## Overview

Evolution is the process by which a Pokémon transforms into a different, usually stronger species. Evolved Pokémon typically gain higher base stats and access to new moves. For a broader introduction to the franchise, see [Pokémon](/pokemon).

## Evolution methods

| Method | How it works | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level up | The Pokémon reaches a specific level. | Charmander evolves into Charmeleon at level 16. |
| Evolution stone | You use an item such as a Fire Stone or Water Stone on the Pokémon. | Pikachu evolves into Raichu with a Thunder Stone. |
| Trade | You trade the Pokémon to another player. | Kadabra evolves into Alakazam when traded. |
| Friendship | The Pokémon levels up while it has high friendship with its Trainer. | Golbat evolves into Crobat. |
| Special conditions | The Pokémon levels up at a certain time of day, location, or while holding an item. | Eevee evolves into Espeon during the day with high friendship. |

## Evolution stages

Most evolution families have one to three stages:

- **Single stage**: The Pokémon does not evolve, such as Tauros.
- **Two stages**: The Pokémon evolves once, such as Magikarp into Gyarados.
- **Three stages**: The Pokémon evolves twice, such as Bulbasaur into Ivysaur into Venusaur.

## Branching evolutions

Some Pokémon can evolve into more than one species depending on how you evolve them. Eevee is the best-known example, with eight possible evolutions:

- Vaporeon, Jolteon, and Flareon (evolution stones)
- Espeon and Umbreon (friendship and time of day)
- Leafeon and Glaceon (location or stone, depending on the game)
- Sylveon (affection or friendship with a Fairy-type move)

## Stopping evolution

You can cancel an evolution by pressing the B button during the evolution animation. An Everstone held by a Pokémon prevents it from evolving entirely.
