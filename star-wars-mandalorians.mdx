# Mandalorians

Mandalorians are a warrior people originally from the planet Mandalore. Their identity is tied less to species than to a shared creed, culture, and clan structure that endures across generations.

## The Way

Traditionalist Mandalorians follow a code known as the Way, which governs conduct, combat, and the raising of foundlings. Followers rarely remove their helmets in the presence of others.

## Beskar armor

Beskar is a Mandalorian iron capable of deflecting blaster bolts and turning aside lightsaber strikes. Full suits of beskar are forged by an armorer and passed down or reforged as clans change.

## Clans and houses

Mandalorian society is organized into clans, which in turn belong to larger houses. Notable examples include Clan Vizsla, House Kryze, and Clan Mudhorn.

## The Darksaber

The Darksaber is a black-bladed lightsaber created by Tarre Vizsla, the first Mandalorian Jedi. Ownership of the blade is traditionally tied to rule over Mandalore and can only be transferred by winning it in combat.
