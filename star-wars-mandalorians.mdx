## Overview

The Mandalorians are a warrior people from the planet Mandalore whose culture is defined by honor, clan loyalty, and mastery of combat. Though scattered by centuries of war, purges, and exile, their traditions have endured — carried forward by those who still walk the Way.

## The Resol'nare

The Resol'nare, or Six Actions, is the creed that binds every Mandalorian:

- Wear the armor.
- Speak the language, Mando'a.
- Defend yourself and your family.
- Raise your children as Mandalorians.
- Contribute to the welfare of your clan.
- Rally to the Mand'alor when called.

## Beskar armor

Beskar is a nearly indestructible iron mined from Mandalore. Forged into armor by trained armorers, it protects its wearer from blaster fire and even glancing lightsaber strikes. A Mandalorian's beskar is passed through generations, reforged as clans grow and change.

- **Helmet** — sacred to those who follow the Way; never removed before others.
- **Cuirass** — the chest plate that carries a clan's sigil.
- **Vambraces** — wrist gauntlets housing whipcord throwers, flamethrowers, and darts.
- **Jetpack** — grants the mobility that made Mandalorian warriors legendary.

## The Darksaber

The Darksaber is a unique black-bladed lightsaber crafted by Tarre Vizsla, the first Mandalorian Jedi. It became a symbol of leadership over Mandalore, passed to whoever could claim it through combat or rightful inheritance. Wielders include Pre Vizsla, Maul, Sabine Wren, Bo-Katan Kryze, and Din Djarin.

## Legacy

Even after the Great Purge scattered their people, the Mandalorians remained a force in the galaxy. Through the Covert, the Nite Owls, and the many clans that survived in exile, the Way endures — and Mandalore rises again with each new generation who takes up the creed.
