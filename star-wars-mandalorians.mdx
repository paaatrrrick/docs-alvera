# Mandalorians

Mandalorians are a warrior culture drawn from many species, united by a shared code and by iconic beskar armor. They hail from the planet Mandalore and its moon, Concordia.

## Beskar armor

Beskar is a dense metal forged into plate armor that can turn aside blaster bolts and resist lightsaber strikes. Each set of armor is passed down or reforged within a clan.

## The Way

"This is the Way" is the creed followed by traditionalist Mandalorians. It governs how a warrior wears the helmet, adopts foundlings, and honors clan bonds.

## Notable Mandalorians

| Mandalorian | Known for |
| --- | --- |
| Jango Fett | Bounty hunter and genetic template for the Grand Army of the Republic |
| Boba Fett | Jango's unaltered clone and one of the galaxy's most feared bounty hunters |
| Din Djarin | Member of the Children of the Watch who protected the foundling Grogu |
| Bo-Katan Kryze | Heir to House Kryze and later wielder of the Darksaber |

## The Darksaber

The Darksaber is a unique black-bladed lightsaber forged by Tarre Vizsla, the first Mandalorian Jedi. Rule of it traditionally passes to whoever wins it in single combat.
