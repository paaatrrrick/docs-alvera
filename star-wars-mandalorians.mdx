# Mandalorians

Mandalorians are members of a warrior culture that originated on the planet Mandalore. Mandalorian identity is defined by a shared creed rather than by species.

## Beskar armor

Mandalorians wear armor forged from beskar, a rare metal that can withstand blaster fire and even lightsaber strikes. Many Mandalorians pass their armor down through generations.

## The creed

The Mandalorian creed, the Resol'nare, centers on six tenets:

- Wear the armor
- Speak the language, Mando'a
- Defend yourself and your family
- Raise your children as Mandalorians
- Contribute to your clan's welfare
- Rally to the Mand'alor when called

Some sects, such as the Children of the Watch, add a strict rule never to remove one's helmet in front of others.

## Lightsabers

Lightsabers are energy blades powered by kyber crystals and traditionally wielded by Jedi and Sith. Mandalorians fought Jedi for centuries, so their beskar armor and tactics evolved to counter lightsaber combat. Few Mandalorians carry lightsabers themselves, with the Darksaber as the most famous exception.

## The Darksaber

The Darksaber is a unique black-bladed lightsaber created by Tarre Vizsla, the first Mandalorian inducted into the Jedi Order. Whoever wins the Darksaber in combat has a claim to lead Mandalore.
