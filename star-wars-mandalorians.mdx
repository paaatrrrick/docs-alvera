# Mandalorians

Mandalorians are a clan-based culture of warriors from the planet Mandalore. They are known for their beskar armor, combat skill, and strong sense of honor.

## Beskar armor

Mandalorian armor is forged from beskar, a rare metal that can resist blaster fire and even lightsaber strikes. Families and clans often pass armor down through generations.

## The Creed

Many Mandalorians follow a code known as the Creed. Some sects, such as the Children of the Watch, never remove their helmets in front of others.

## Clans and foundlings

Mandalorian identity is based on belief and practice rather than species. Clans often adopt orphaned children, called foundlings, and raise them in Mandalorian ways.

## The Darksaber

The Darksaber is an ancient black-bladed lightsaber. Whoever wins it in combat can claim leadership of Mandalore.

## Lightsabers

Lightsabers are energy blades powered by kyber crystals and are most closely tied to the Jedi and the Sith. Mandalorians rarely wield them. Instead, they train to fight lightsaber users and rely on beskar armor to withstand the blades.
