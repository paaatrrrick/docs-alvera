## Overview

The Mandalorians are a clan-based warrior culture from the planet Mandalore. Renowned for their martial traditions, distinctive beskar armor, and rigid code of honor, they have shaped galactic history through centuries of conflict with the Jedi Order and involvement in major galactic wars.

## Culture and creed

Mandalorian identity is defined by adherence to the *Resol'nare*, the Six Actions that every Mandalorian is expected to uphold:

- Wear armor.
- Speak the Mando'a language.
- Defend yourself and your family.
- Raise your children as Mandalorians.
- Contribute to the clan's welfare.
- Answer the summons of the *Mand'alor*, the sole leader of the Mandalorian people.

Clans are grouped into larger houses, such as House Vizsla and House Kryze, which have historically vied for political and military dominance.

## Beskar armor

Beskar, or Mandalorian iron, is a rare ore mined almost exclusively on Mandalore and its moon Concordia. Its properties make it one of the most sought-after materials in the galaxy:

- Resistant to lightsaber strikes and blaster fire.
- Forged into full-body armor, helmets, and weapons.
- Passed down between generations as a symbol of clan identity.

The T-visor helmet is the most recognizable element of Mandalorian armor and is worn as a mark of cultural pride.

## The Darksaber

The Darksaber is a unique black-bladed lightsaber created by Tarre Vizsla, the first Mandalorian inducted into the Jedi Order. It has served as a symbol of leadership over Mandalore, and by tradition can only be claimed through combat. Notable wielders include:

- Tarre Vizsla
- Pre Vizsla
- Sabine Wren
- Bo-Katan Kryze
- Din Djarin

## Notable Mandalorians

- **Jango Fett** — Bounty hunter and genetic template for the Grand Army of the Republic's clone troopers.
- **Boba Fett** — Unaltered clone of Jango Fett; became one of the galaxy's most feared bounty hunters and later Daimyo of Tatooine.
- **Din Djarin** — Foundling raised by the Children of the Watch; wielder of the Darksaber and protector of Grogu.
- **Bo-Katan Kryze** — Leader of the Nite Owls and a key figure in the effort to reunify Mandalore.
- **Sabine Wren** — Rebel fighter, artist, and former wielder of the Darksaber.

## Historical conflicts

Mandalorian history is marked by centuries of warfare:

- **Mandalorian–Jedi Wars** — Ancient conflicts that shaped the enmity between Mandalorian warriors and the Jedi Order.
- **Mandalorian Civil War** — Fought between the pacifist New Mandalorians and traditionalist factions like Death Watch.
- **The Siege of Mandalore** — A late Clone Wars campaign led by Bo-Katan Kryze and Ahsoka Tano against Maul's forces.
- **The Great Purge of Mandalore** — An Imperial campaign that devastated the planet and scattered its people.
