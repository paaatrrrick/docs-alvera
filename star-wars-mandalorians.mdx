## Overview

Mandalorians are a warrior culture from the planet Mandalore and its moon Concordia. Their identity centers on a shared creed, clan loyalty, and a martial tradition that spans thousands of years. Mandalorians are not a single species; anyone who follows the Way can be adopted into a clan.

## The Way

*The Way of the Mand'alor* is a code passed down through generations. Traditionalists, sometimes called the Children of the Watch, live by strict tenets:

- Never remove your helmet in the presence of another living being.
- Care for foundlings and raise them as your own.
- Answer the call of your clan and your Mand'alor.

Reformist Mandalorians on Mandalore under Duchess Satine Kryze pursued a pacifist path during the Clone Wars era, breaking from these traditions.

## Beskar armor

Beskar is a Mandalorian iron mined from the surface of Mandalore. It resists lightsaber strikes and blaster fire, which makes full beskar armor a symbol of both protection and status.

| Piece | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Helmet | Identity and heads-up display |
| Cuirass | Torso protection with signet plating |
| Vambraces | Mount flamethrowers, grappling lines, and whistling birds |
| Jetpack | Short-range flight and combat mobility |

## The Darksaber

The Darksaber is a unique black-bladed lightsaber forged by Tarre Vizsla, the first Mandalorian inducted into the Jedi Order. Ownership traditionally passes by combat, and holding it confers the right to rule Mandalore.

## Mandalore through the eras

- **High Republic and earlier**: Mandalorian crusaders clash with the Jedi across the galaxy.
- **Clone Wars**: Death Watch, Maul, and Bo-Katan fight for control of Mandalore.
- **Imperial era**: The Empire executes the Night of a Thousand Tears, devastating Mandalore.
- **New Republic era**: Din Djarin and Bo-Katan lead survivors to reclaim and resettle their homeworld.
