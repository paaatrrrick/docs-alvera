# Mandalorians

Mandalorians are a clan-based warrior culture that originated on the planet Mandalore. Membership is defined by a shared creed rather than species, so people from many worlds can become Mandalorian.

## Beskar armor

Mandalorian armor is forged from beskar, a rare iron that can withstand blaster fire and even resist lightsaber strikes. Armor is often passed down through families and customized with clan sigils.

## Lightsabers

A lightsaber is a plasma blade weapon traditionally carried by Jedi and Sith. Most lightsabers are powered by a kyber crystal. Mandalorians rarely wield them, but beskar armor lets them stand against Force users in combat. The Darksaber is a notable exception. This unique black-bladed lightsaber was forged by Tarre Vizsla, the first Mandalorian Jedi, and became a symbol of the right to rule Mandalore.

## The Creed

Many Mandalorians follow a code that values loyalty, honor, and the protection of foundlings. Some sects never remove their helmets in front of others.

## Notable Mandalorians

- **Din Djarin**: A bounty hunter who protects the foundling Grogu.
- **Bo-Katan Kryze**: A leader who fights to reclaim Mandalore.
- **Jango Fett**: The bounty hunter whose DNA was used to create the clone army.
