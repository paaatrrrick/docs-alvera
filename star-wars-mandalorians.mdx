# Mandalorians

Mandalorians are a culture of warriors from the planet Mandalore. Membership is defined by a shared creed rather than species, so anyone can become a Mandalorian by adopting its ways.

## The creed

Mandalorians follow a code of honor that values loyalty, family, and combat skill. Some strict sects, such as the Children of the Watch, never remove their helmets in front of others.

## Beskar armor

Mandalorian armor is forged from beskar, a rare metal that can withstand blaster fire and even resist lightsaber strikes. Armor is often passed down through generations and treated as part of a warrior's identity.

## Clans

Mandalorian society is organized into clans and houses, such as Clan Kryze and House Vizsla. Clans compete for influence, and leadership of Mandalore has shifted between them over time.

## The Darksaber

The Darksaber is a unique black-bladed lightsaber created by Tarre Vizsla, the first Mandalorian Jedi. Whoever wins it in combat can claim the right to rule Mandalore.

## Notable Mandalorians

- **Din Djarin**: A bounty hunter who protects the foundling Grogu.
- **Bo-Katan Kryze**: A leader who fights to reclaim Mandalore.
- **Jango Fett**: The bounty hunter whose DNA was used to create the clone army.
- **Sabine Wren**: A warrior and artist who joins the rebel crew of the Ghost.
