title: "Mandalorians"
description: "The warrior culture of Mandalore and its traditions"
---

Mandalorians are a culture of warriors from the planet Mandalore. Membership comes from following the Creed, not from species or birthplace.

## The Creed

The Mandalorian Creed binds its followers to a shared code. Some sects, such as the Children of the Watch, forbid removing their helmets in front of others.

## Beskar armor

Mandalorian armor is forged from beskar, a metal that can withstand blaster fire and even lightsaber strikes. Armor is often passed down through families and clans.

## Clans and leadership

Mandalorians organize into clans, each led by its own leader. Whoever wields the Darksaber holds a traditional claim to rule all of Mandalore.

## Notable Mandalorians

- **Din Djarin**: a bounty hunter who protects the foundling Grogu
- **Bo-Katan Kryze**: a leader of Clan Kryze and former Death Watch member
- **Jango Fett**: the bounty hunter whose genetic template created the clone army
- **Sabine Wren**: an artist, explosives expert, and rebel
