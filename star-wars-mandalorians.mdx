# Mandalorians

Mandalorians are a warrior culture from the planet Mandalore. Their identity comes from shared customs and a code of honor, not from species. Anyone can become a Mandalorian by adopting the creed.

## Beskar armor

Mandalorian armor is forged from beskar, a rare metal that can withstand blaster fire and even resist a lightsaber blade. Each set of armor passes down through families and clans.

## The creed

Mandalorians follow a creed often summed up as "This is the Way." Some sects, such as the Children of the Watch, never remove their helmets in front of others.

## Clans and leadership

Mandalorians organize into clans. The Darksaber, an ancient black-bladed lightsaber, is a symbol of the right to rule Mandalore.
