# Mandalorians

Mandalorians are a warrior culture from the planet Mandalore. They are united by a shared creed rather than a single species.

## The armor

Mandalorian armor, known as beskar'gam, is forged from beskar. The metal can withstand blaster fire and even lightsaber strikes.

## The creed

Followers of the creed live by a strict code of honor. Many refuse to remove their helmets in front of others.

## Notable Mandalorians

- **Jango Fett**: A bounty hunter whose genetic template created the clone army.
- **Boba Fett**: Jango's unaltered clone, feared across the galaxy as a bounty hunter.
- **Bo-Katan Kryze**: A leader of the Nite Owls who fought to reclaim Mandalore.
- **Din Djarin**: A foundling raised by the Children of the Watch.

## The Darksaber

The Darksaber is an ancient black-bladed lightsaber created by Tarre Vizsla, the first Mandalorian Jedi. By tradition, its wielder holds claim to rule Mandalore.
