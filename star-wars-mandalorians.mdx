## Overview

Mandalorians are a warrior people from the planet Mandalore and its moon Concordia. They are known less for a shared species than for a shared creed, and members of many species can join a clan by swearing the Resol'nare, the six tenets of Mandalorian life.

## Culture and creed

The Resol'nare binds Mandalorians to:

- Wear the armor.
- Speak the Mando'a language.
- Defend yourself and your family.
- Raise your children as Mandalorians.
- Contribute to the clan's welfare.
- Answer the call of the Mand'alor when summoned.

Clans are led by an *alor*, and the leader of all clans is the *Mand'alor*, the sole wielder of the Darksaber by tradition.

## Beskar armor

Mandalorian armor is forged from *beskar*, an iron ore mined on Mandalore. Beskar can turn aside blaster bolts and resist lightsaber strikes, which makes a full set both a weapon system and a mark of identity.

Common armor components include:

- **Helmet**: Sealed with a T-visor, often never removed in front of outsiders under the Way of the Mandalore.
- **Cuirass**: Chest and back plates that carry clan sigils.
- **Vambraces**: Forearm gauntlets that mount grappling lines, flamethrowers, and dart launchers.
- **Jetpack**: Enables short-range flight and combat repositioning.

## The Darksaber

The Darksaber is a unique black-bladed lightsaber created by Tarre Vizsla, the first Mandalorian Jedi. It is the traditional symbol of leadership over Mandalore, and by custom it must be won in combat to be legitimately claimed.

## Mandalore through the eras

- **Old Republic era**: Mandalorian Crusades and Neo-Crusader wars against the Jedi.
- **Clone Wars**: A pacifist government under Duchess Satine Kryze is overthrown by Death Watch and later by Darth Maul.
- **Imperial era**: The Empire carries out the Night of a Thousand Tears, glassing much of Mandalore's surface.
- **New Republic era**: Bo-Katan Kryze leads a campaign to reclaim and resettle Mandalore, as depicted in *The Mandalorian* season 3.
