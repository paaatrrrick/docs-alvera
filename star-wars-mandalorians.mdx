## Overview

Mandalorians are a warrior culture from the planet Mandalore. They are known for their beskar armor, strict code of honor, and long history of conflict with the Jedi Order.

## The Creed

Adherents of the Way of the Mandalore, known as the Children of the Watch, follow a creed centered on three tenets:

- Never remove your helmet in the presence of others.
- Protect and raise foundlings as your own.
- Live and die by "This is the Way."

Not every Mandalorian follows this strict interpretation. Reformist groups, such as those led by Bo-Katan Kryze, wear their helmets by choice rather than by oath.

## Beskar armor

Beskar is a Mandalorian iron capable of deflecting lightsaber strikes and blaster bolts. Full sets include:

| Piece | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Helmet | Sealed life support and heads-up display |
| Cuirass | Torso protection with weapon mounts |
| Vambraces | Wrist-mounted tools, including whipcord throwers and flamethrowers |
| Jetpack | Short-range flight and evasion |

Beskar is often passed down through clans, and reforging it is treated as a sacred act.

## Notable Mandalorians

- **Din Djarin**: A bounty hunter and adoptive father of Grogu, and the central figure of *The Mandalorian*.
- **Bo-Katan Kryze**: Heir to House Kryze and wielder of the Darksaber during the reclamation of Mandalore.
- **Sabine Wren**: An artist, weapons expert, and member of the Ghost crew in *Star Wars Rebels*.
- **Boba Fett**: A clone of Jango Fett who took up his father's armor and legacy.
- **Pre Vizsla**: Leader of Death Watch and a former wielder of the Darksaber.

## The Darksaber

The Darksaber is a unique black-bladed lightsaber forged by Tarre Vizsla, the first Mandalorian Jedi. It serves as a symbol of leadership over Mandalore, and tradition holds that it must be won in combat to be legitimately claimed.
