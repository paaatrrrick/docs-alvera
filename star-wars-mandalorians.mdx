# Mandalorians

Mandalorians form a proud warrior culture drawn from many species, united by tradition, honor, and the code known as the Resol'nare.

## Beskar armor

Mandalorian smiths forge their armor from beskar, a rare iron that resists lightsaber strikes and blaster bolts.

## The Creed

Followers of the Way of the Mandalore live by strict tenets, including never removing their helmets in the presence of others.

## Mandalore

Centuries of war have shaped the homeworld of Mandalore, uniting and dividing its clans across generations of conflict.
