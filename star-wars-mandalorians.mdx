# Mandalorians

Mandalorians are a fierce warrior people from the planet Mandalore, defined more by shared creed than by species.

## The Way

Followers of the traditional creed live by a strict code that governs honor, combat, and family. Foundlings raised in the Way are considered kin.

## Beskar armor

Mandalorian armor is forged from beskar, a nearly indestructible iron mined on Mandalore. A full set typically includes a helmet, cuirass, vambraces, and a jetpack.

## Notable Mandalorians

- Jango Fett — bounty hunter and template for the clone army.
- Boba Fett — Jango's clone son and legendary bounty hunter.
- Din Djarin — the Mandalorian who protected Grogu.
- Bo-Katan Kryze — wielder of the Darksaber and heir to Mandalore.
