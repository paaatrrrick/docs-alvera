# Mandalorians

Mandalorians are a warrior people from the planet Mandalore. Their culture is defined by a shared code, clan loyalties, and iconic armor forged from beskar.

## Beskar armor

Beskar is a rare Mandalorian iron capable of deflecting blaster bolts and resisting lightsaber strikes. Armorers reforge it across generations, passing pieces down within a clan.

## The Way

Adherents of the Way follow the Resol'nare, the six core tenets that bind Mandalorian life. Some sects, such as the Children of the Watch, keep the helmet on at all times in the presence of others.

## Clans and creed

Mandalorian society is organized into clans led by a chief. A foundling taken in by a clan is raised as one of their own, sealing the bond with the phrase "This is the Way."
