# Mandalorians

Mandalorians are a proud warrior culture drawn from many species, united by tradition, honor, and the code known as the Resol'nare.

## Beskar armor

Mandalorian armor is forged from beskar, a rare iron capable of resisting lightsaber strikes and blaster bolts.

## The Creed

Followers of the Way of the Mandalore live by strict tenets, including never removing their helmets in the presence of others.

## Mandalore

The homeworld of Mandalore has been shaped by centuries of war, uniting and dividing its clans through generations of conflict.
