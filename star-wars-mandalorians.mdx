# Mandalorians

Mandalorians are a culture of warriors bound by a shared creed rather than by species. Members come from many backgrounds and are united by tradition, language, and their iconic armor.

## Beskar armor

Mandalorian armor is often forged from beskar, a rare iron mined on Mandalore. Beskar can withstand blaster fire and even resist lightsaber strikes.

## The creed

Mandalorians follow a code known as the Resol'nare, or Six Actions. It includes wearing armor, speaking the Mando'a language, defending oneself and family, raising children as Mandalorians, contributing to the clan, and rallying to the leader of Mandalore when called.

## Clans and leadership

Mandalorian society is organized into clans and houses. The leader of all Mandalorians is traditionally called the Mand'alor, and some rulers have claimed the title by wielding the Darksaber.

## Lightsabers

Lightsabers are plasma blades traditionally wielded by Jedi and Sith. Mandalorians have long fought against lightsaber users, which is one reason they prize beskar armor. The Darksaber is a unique black-bladed lightsaber created by Tarre Vizsla, the first Mandalorian inducted into the Jedi Order. It became a symbol of leadership over Mandalore.

## Notable Mandalorians

- **Din Djarin**: A bounty hunter who protected the foundling Grogu
- **Bo-Katan Kryze**: A noble of House Kryze who fought to reclaim Mandalore
- **Jango Fett**: A bounty hunter and the template for the clone army
