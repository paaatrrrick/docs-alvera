# Mandalorians

Mandalorians are a warrior culture from the planet Mandalore, bound together by a shared creed rather than a single species.

## The creed

Mandalorians live by a strict code known as the Way of the Mandalore. Followers pledge to protect foundlings, care for their clan, and never remove their helmet in front of others.

## Beskar armor

Mandalorian armor is forged from beskar, a rare iron that can withstand blaster fire and even lightsaber strikes. Armor is passed down through generations of a clan.

## Notable Mandalorians

- **Din Djarin**: A bounty hunter and foundling who became guardian of Grogu.
- **Bo-Katan Kryze**: Heir of House Kryze who fought to reunite the clans of Mandalore.
- **Boba Fett**: Wearer of Mandalorian armor inherited from his father, Jango Fett.
