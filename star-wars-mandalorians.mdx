# Mandalorians

Mandalorians are a warrior people originating from the planet Mandalore. Bound less by species than by creed, their culture spans clans, houses, and generations of conflict.

## The Way

Traditionalist Mandalorians follow *the Way of the Mand'alor*, a strict code that governs combat, honor, and identity. Adherents of the Children of the Watch never remove their helmets in the presence of others.

## Beskar armor

Mandalorian armor is forged from *beskar*, a rare iron mined on Mandalore and its moon Concordia. Beskar can withstand blaster fire and even deflect lightsaber strikes, making it among the most prized materials in the galaxy.

## The Darksaber

The Darksaber is a unique black-bladed lightsaber created by Tarre Vizsla, the first Mandalorian Jedi. It is a symbol of leadership over Mandalore and can only be rightfully claimed by defeating its current wielder in combat.

## Mandalore

Once a lush world, Mandalore was devastated by the Great Purge under the Galactic Empire. Efforts to restore the planet and reunite its scattered clans are a recurring thread across the *Star Wars* saga.
