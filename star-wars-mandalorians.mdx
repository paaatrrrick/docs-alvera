Mandalorians are a culture of warriors bound by shared customs rather than a single species. They originate from the planet Mandalore in the Outer Rim.

## Beskar armor

Mandalorian armor is forged from beskar, a rare metal that resists blaster fire and can even withstand a lightsaber strike. Armor is often passed down through families as an heirloom.

## The Resol'nare

The Resol'nare, or Six Actions, are the core tenets of Mandalorian life:

- Wear the armor.
- Speak the language, Mando'a.
- Defend yourself and your family.
- Raise your children as Mandalorians.
- Contribute to the clan's welfare.
- Rally to the Mand'alor when called.

## Clans and leadership

Mandalorians organize into clans and houses. The Mand'alor is the leader of all Mandalorians, a title historically tied to wielding the Darksaber.
