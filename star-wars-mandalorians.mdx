# Mandalorians

Mandalorians are a warrior people from the planet Mandalore, known for their beskar armor, strict code of honor, and long history of conflict with the [Jedi Order](/star-wars-jedi-order).

## Beskar armor

Beskar is a rare iron mined on Mandalore that can turn aside blaster bolts and even lightsaber strikes. A Mandalorian passes their armor down through generations and reforges it for each new bearer.

## The Way

"This is the Way" is the guiding creed of the Children of the Watch and other traditionalist Mandalorian sects. The creed governs how a Mandalorian fights, raises foundlings, and wears their helmet.

## Notable Mandalorians

- **Jango Fett** — bounty hunter and template for the Republic's clone army.
- **Boba Fett** — Jango's unaltered clone, later Daimyo of Tatooine.
- **Din Djarin** — the Mandalorian who protected Grogu and briefly wielded the Darksaber.
- **Bo-Katan Kryze** — leader of the Nite Owls and eventual Mand'alor.

## The Darksaber

The Darksaber is a unique black-bladed lightsaber created by Tarre Vizsla, the first Mandalorian Jedi. Whoever wins it in combat is recognized as the rightful ruler of Mandalore.
