---
title: "Mandalorians"
description: "The warrior culture of Mandalore and its traditions"
---

Mandalorians are a culture of warriors bound by a shared creed rather than a single species. Their home system is Mandalore, in the Outer Rim.

## Beskar armor

Mandalorian armor is forged from beskar, a rare metal that can resist blaster fire and even lightsaber strikes. Many Mandalorians pass their armor down through generations.

## The creed

Mandalorian life centers on a code of honor. Some sects, such as the Children of the Watch, require members to never remove their helmets in front of others.

## Notable Mandalorians

- **Din Djarin**: A bounty hunter who protects Grogu.
- **Bo-Katan Kryze**: A leader who fights to reclaim Mandalore.
- **Jango Fett**: The bounty hunter whose DNA formed the template for the clone army.

## Weapons and gear

Mandalorians often carry jetpacks, wrist-mounted gauntlets, and blasters. The Darksaber, a unique black-bladed lightsaber, is a symbol of leadership on Mandalore.
