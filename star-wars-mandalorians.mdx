# Mandalorians

Mandalorians are a culture of warriors from the planet Mandalore. Membership is defined by creed rather than species, so anyone can become a Mandalorian by adopting the way of life.

## Beskar armor

Mandalorian armor is forged from beskar, a rare iron that can withstand blaster fire and even lightsaber strikes. Armor is often passed down through families as a mark of heritage.

## Lightsabers

Lightsabers are plasma blades traditionally wielded by Jedi and Sith. Mandalorians have a long history of fighting Force users, which is why beskar armor is prized for resisting lightsaber strikes.

The Darksaber is a unique black-bladed lightsaber created by Tarre Vizsla, the first Mandalorian Jedi. Whoever wins it in combat can claim leadership of Mandalore.

## The Resol'nare

The Resol'nare, or Six Actions, are the core tenets of Mandalorian life:

- Wearing armor
- Speaking the Mando'a language
- Defending oneself and one's family
- Raising children as Mandalorians
- Contributing to the clan's welfare
- Rallying to the Mand'alor when called

## Notable Mandalorians

- **Din Djarin**: A bounty hunter who protected the foundling Grogu.
- **Bo-Katan Kryze**: A leader who fought to reclaim Mandalore.
- **Jango Fett**: The template for the clone army of the Republic.
