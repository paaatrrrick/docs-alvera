## Overview

Mandalorians are a warrior culture from the planet Mandalore and its neighboring worlds in the Outer Rim. They are best known for their distinctive beskar armor, strict code of honor, and long history of conflict with the Jedi Order.

## Culture and creed

Mandalorian identity is defined by shared traditions rather than species — humans, Togruta, and other beings can all be Mandalorian if they follow the way.

- **The Resol'nare**: The six actions every Mandalorian is expected to uphold, including wearing armor and defending the clan.
- **The Way of the Mand'alor**: A stricter creed followed by groups such as the Children of the Watch, requiring members to keep their helmets on in the presence of others.
- **Clan and house**: Mandalorians organize into clans, which are grouped into larger houses led by a Mand'alor.

## Beskar armor

Beskar is a nearly indestructible iron mined on Mandalore. Smiths forge it into full-body armor that can deflect blaster bolts and resist lightsaber strikes.

| Element | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Helmet | Identity, HUD, and life support |
| Cuirass | Torso protection and clan sigils |
| Vambraces | House flamethrowers, whipcord, and grappling tools |
| Jetpack | Short-range flight and evasion |

## Notable Mandalorians

- **Jango Fett**: Bounty hunter and genetic template for the Republic's clone army.
- **Boba Fett**: Jango's clone son, later Daimyo of Tatooine.
- **Bo-Katan Kryze**: Leader of the Nite Owls and claimant to the title of Mand'alor.
- **Din Djarin**: Bounty hunter and protector of Grogu, star of *The Mandalorian*.
- **Sabine Wren**: Artist, demolitions expert, and member of the *Ghost* crew.

## Relationship to the wider galaxy

Mandalorians have clashed with the Jedi for millennia and were nearly wiped out during the Great Purge carried out by the Galactic Empire. Efforts to reclaim Mandalore and reunify the clans continue in the New Republic era.
