## Overview

The Mandalorians are a warrior culture from the planet Mandalore. They are known for their beskar armor, strict code of honor, and centuries of conflict with the Jedi Order. Mandalorians appear across films and series, most prominently in *The Clone Wars*, *Rebels*, *The Mandalorian*, and *The Book of Boba Fett*.

## Culture and creed

Mandalorian identity is built around a shared creed rather than a single species or planet.

- **The Way** — A traditional code followed by covert Mandalorians such as Din Djarin. Followers never remove their helmets in the presence of others.
- **Foundlings** — Children taken in by a clan and raised as Mandalorians, regardless of species or origin.
- **Clans** — Extended family groups that swear loyalty to one another and to a leader.
- **Mand'oa** — The Mandalorian language, used in oaths, songs, and clan mottos.

## Beskar armor

Beskar is a rare iron mined on Mandalore that can withstand blaster bolts and lightsaber strikes.

- Forged into helmets, chest plates, gauntlets, and vambraces.
- Passed down through clans and reforged when an owner dies or a foundling is inducted.
- Plundered by the Galactic Empire during the Great Purge and later recovered by surviving Mandalorians.

## Weapons and gear

Mandalorians pair beskar armor with a range of specialized weapons.

- **Jetpacks** — Provide short-range flight and fast repositioning in combat.
- **Whistling birds** — Wrist-mounted homing projectiles that lock onto multiple targets.
- **Flamethrowers and grappling lines** — Standard gauntlet attachments.
- **Amban rifle** — A long-barreled disruptor rifle favored by Din Djarin.
- **Darksaber** — A unique black-bladed lightsaber created by Tarre Vizsla, the first Mandalorian Jedi. Ruling Mandalore traditionally requires winning the Darksaber in combat.

## Notable Mandalorians

### Jango Fett

A bounty hunter who served as the genetic template for the Republic's clone army. Jango wore silver-and-blue Mandalorian armor and fought Jedi Master Mace Windu on Geonosis.

### Boba Fett

Jango's unaltered clone son, raised as his heir. Boba becomes one of the galaxy's most feared bounty hunters and later takes control of Jabba the Hutt's former territory on Tatooine.

### Din Djarin

The bounty hunter known as "the Mandalorian." Din protects the Force-sensitive foundling Grogu and, through a series of duels, briefly holds the Darksaber.

### Bo-Katan Kryze

A member of House Kryze and sister to Duchess Satine. Bo-Katan leads the Nite Owls, fights in the Siege of Mandalore, and later works to reunite the Mandalorian clans.

### Sabine Wren

A member of the Ghost crew in *Rebels*. Sabine is an explosives expert and artist who wields the Darksaber before passing it to Bo-Katan.

## Key events

- **Mandalorian-Jedi wars** — Ancient conflicts that shaped both cultures and led to the devastation of Mandalore's surface.
- **New Mandalorian era** — A pacifist government led by Duchess Satine Kryze during the Clone Wars.
- **Siege of Mandalore** — Ahsoka Tano and Bo-Katan lead Republic and Mandalorian forces against Maul in the final days of the Clone Wars.
- **The Great Purge** — The Empire's genocidal campaign against the Mandalorian people, leaving scattered covert groups as survivors.
- **Reclamation of Mandalore** — Din Djarin and Bo-Katan lead surviving clans back to their homeworld to rebuild.
