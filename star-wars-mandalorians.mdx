# Mandalorians

Mandalorians are a warrior culture that originated on the planet Mandalore. Membership is defined by creed rather than species, so anyone who adopts the Mandalorian way can become one.

## Beskar armor

Mandalorian armor is forged from beskar, a rare metal that can resist blaster fire and even lightsaber strikes. Many Mandalorians treat their armor as a family heirloom and pass it down through generations.

## The creed

Mandalorians follow a code known as the Resol'nare, which emphasizes:

- Wearing armor
- Speaking the Mandalorian language, Mando'a
- Defending yourself and your family
- Raising your children as Mandalorians
- Contributing to the clan's welfare
- Answering the call to rally when your leader summons you

Some sects, such as the Children of the Watch, hold stricter beliefs. Members of that sect never remove their helmets in front of others.

## Clans and leadership

Mandalorian society is organized into clans and houses, such as Clan Wren and House Kryze. Leadership has traditionally belonged to whoever holds the Darksaber, an ancient black-bladed lightsaber.
