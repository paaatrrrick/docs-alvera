## Overview

Mandalorians are a clan-based warrior culture that originated on the planet Mandalore. Membership is defined by a shared creed rather than species. Anyone who adopts the Mandalorian way of life can become one.

## Beskar armor

Mandalorian armor is forged from beskar, a rare metal that can withstand blaster fire and even resist lightsaber strikes. A Mandalorian's armor is often passed down through generations and serves as a symbol of identity and heritage.

## The Creed

Some Mandalorian sects follow a strict creed. Members of the Children of the Watch, for example, never remove their helmets in front of others. The phrase "This is the Way" affirms a Mandalorian's commitment to that path.

## Clans and leadership

Mandalorian society is organized into clans and houses, such as Clan Kryze and Clan Wren. The ruler of Mandalore traditionally carries the Darksaber, a unique black-bladed lightsaber that must be won in combat to confer a legitimate claim to leadership.
