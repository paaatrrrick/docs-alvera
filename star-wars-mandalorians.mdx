# Mandalorians

Mandalorians are members of a warrior culture that originated on the planet Mandalore. Their identity comes from a shared creed, not a single species.

## Beskar armor

Mandalorians wear armor forged from beskar, a rare metal that can withstand blaster fire and even resist lightsaber strikes. Many Mandalorians pass their armor down through generations.

## The creed

Some Mandalorians follow a strict code known as "the Way." Members of this sect never remove their helmets in front of others. They greet each other with the phrase "This is the Way."

## Notable Mandalorians

- **Din Djarin**: A bounty hunter who protects the foundling Grogu.
- **Bo-Katan Kryze**: A leader who fights to reclaim Mandalore.
- **Jango Fett**: A bounty hunter whose DNA became the template for the clone army.

## The Darksaber

The Darksaber is a unique black-bladed lightsaber. Mandalorians regard whoever wins it in combat as the rightful ruler of Mandalore.

## Lightsabers

A lightsaber is a plasma blade weapon powered by a kyber crystal. Jedi and Sith traditionally wield them, so Mandalorians have long trained to fight against them. Beskar armor lets Mandalorian warriors survive lightsaber strikes that would cut through ordinary metal.

## Famous quotes

- "This is the Way." The traditional Mandalorian affirmation of the creed.
- "I have spoken." A phrase Kuiil uses to end a discussion, later echoed by Din Djarin.
- "Weapons are part of my religion." Din Djarin explains why he won't give up his arms.
- "Wherever I go, he goes." Din Djarin describes his bond with Grogu.
