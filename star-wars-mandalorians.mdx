# Mandalorians

Mandalorians are a clan-based warrior culture from the planet Mandalore. They are known for their distinctive armor, strict codes of honor, and skill in combat.

## Beskar armor

Mandalorian armor is forged from beskar, a rare metal that can withstand blaster fire and even resist lightsaber strikes. Families often pass Mandalorian armor down through generations.

## The Creed

Many Mandalorians follow a creed that guides how they live, fight, and raise foundlings. Some sects, such as the Children of the Watch, never remove their helmets in front of others.

## Clans and leadership

Mandalorian society is organized into clans and houses. The Darksaber, an ancient black-bladed lightsaber, is a symbol of the right to rule Mandalore.

## Notable Mandalorians

- **Din Djarin**: A bounty hunter who protects the foundling Grogu.
- **Bo-Katan Kryze**: A leader who fights to reclaim Mandalore.
- **Jango Fett**: The bounty hunter whose DNA was used to create the clone army.
