## Overview

Mandalorians are a warrior culture originating from the planet Mandalore. They are defined less by species than by creed — anyone who follows the Resol'nare, the six tenets of Mandalorian life, can be counted among their number. They are best known for their signature beskar armor, T-visored helmets, and long history of conflict with the Jedi.

## The Resol'nare

The Resol'nare is the shared code that binds Mandalorians together:

- Wear the armor.
- Speak the language, Mando'a.
- Defend yourself and your family.
- Raise your children as Mandalorians.
- Contribute to the welfare of the clan.
- Rally to the Mand'alor when called.

## Beskar armor

Beskar is a nearly indestructible iron alloy mined from the surface of Mandalore. Forged by Mandalorian armorers, it resists blaster fire and even glancing lightsaber strikes.

## Clans and houses

Mandalorian society is organized into clans, which in turn belong to larger houses. Notable examples include:

- **Clan Wren** — Part of House Vizsla; known for the artist and warrior Sabine Wren.
- **Clan Kryze** — House Kryze produced Duchess Satine and Bo-Katan, wielders of the Darksaber.
- **Clan Vizsla** — Founders of Death Watch and original owners of the Darksaber.
- **Clan Djarin** — The adopted clan of Din Djarin and the foundling Grogu.
- **Clan Saxon** — Aligned with the Empire during the reign of Gar Saxon.

## The Darksaber

The Darksaber is a unique black-bladed lightsaber forged by Tarre Vizsla, the first Mandalorian Jedi. It became a symbol of leadership over Mandalore, passed by combat from one wielder to the next.

<Note>
By tradition, the Darksaber must be won in battle. Giving it freely weakens the claim of the ruler who inherits it.
</Note>

## Notable Mandalorians

- **Jango Fett** — Bounty hunter and genetic template for the Grand Army of the Republic.
- **Boba Fett** — Jango's unaltered clone; later Daimyo of Tatooine.
- **Din Djarin** — Member of the Children of the Watch and guardian of Grogu.
- **Bo-Katan Kryze** — Leader of the Nite Owls and wielder of the Darksaber.
- **Sabine Wren** — Artist, warrior, and hero of the Rebellion.
- **Pre Vizsla** — Governor of Concordia and leader of Death Watch.
