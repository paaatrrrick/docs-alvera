## Overview

The Alliance to Restore the Republic, commonly known as the Rebel Alliance or simply the Rebellion, was a coalition of freedom fighters, defected Imperial personnel, and sympathetic senators who united to overthrow the Galactic Empire and restore the Galactic Republic.

## Origins

The Alliance was formally founded when Mon Mothma, Bail Organa, and Garm Bel Iblis signed the Corellian Treaty, unifying scattered resistance cells into a single organization. Its roots trace back to the earliest opposition to Emperor Palpatine's rule during the final days of the Republic.

## Key leaders

| Leader | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Mon Mothma | Chief of State and political architect of the Alliance |
| Bail Organa | Senator of Alderaan and founding member |
| Princess Leia Organa | Diplomat, senator, and field leader |
| General Jan Dodonna | Military strategist behind the Battle of Yavin |
| Admiral Ackbar | Commander of the Alliance fleet |
| General Airen Cracken | Head of Alliance Intelligence |

## Structure

The Rebellion was organized into several coordinated branches:

- **Alliance High Command** — Political and strategic leadership.
- **Alliance Fleet** — Starfighter squadrons and capital ships operating from mobile bases.
- **Alliance Special Forces (SpecForces)** — Ground commandos, pathfinders, and infiltrators.
- **Alliance Intelligence** — Espionage, sabotage, and counter-intelligence.

## Major victories

- **Scarif** — Rebel commandos stole the Death Star plans, enabling the Alliance's first major strike.
- **Yavin** — Luke Skywalker destroyed the first Death Star, saving the Alliance's hidden base.
- **Endor** — The Rebellion destroyed the second Death Star and killed Emperor Palpatine, effectively ending Imperial dominance.

## Iconic starships

- **Millennium Falcon** — Han Solo's modified YT-1300 light freighter.
- **X-wing starfighter** — The versatile T-65 fighter flown by Rogue and Red Squadrons.
- **A-wing** and **Y-wing** — Complementary fast interceptors and heavy bombers.
- **Home One** — Admiral Ackbar's MC80 Star Cruiser and mobile command flagship.

## Legacy

After the Battle of Endor, the Alliance reorganized as the New Republic. Decades later, its ideals were carried forward by the Resistance, led by General Leia Organa, in the struggle against the First Order.
