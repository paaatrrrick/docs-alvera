## Overview

The Alliance to Restore the Republic, commonly known as the Rebel Alliance, was a coalition of resistance cells that opposed the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War. Formed in secret from disillusioned senators, defectors, and outlaw fighters, the Alliance eventually defeated the Empire at the Battle of Endor and paved the way for the New Republic.

## Founding

The Alliance was formalized through the Corellian Treaty, which united three principal rebel networks:

- Mon Mothma's political dissenters
- Bail Organa's Alderaanian resistance
- Garm Bel Iblis's independent cells

## Key operations

| Operation | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Battle of Scarif | Death Star plans stolen |
| Battle of Yavin | First Death Star destroyed |
| Battle of Hoth | Rebel base evacuated after Imperial assault |
| Battle of Endor | Emperor Palpatine killed, second Death Star destroyed |

## Leadership

- **Mon Mothma**: Chancellor and civilian leader of the Alliance.
- **Admiral Ackbar**: Supreme commander of Alliance naval forces.
- **General Jan Dodonna**: Strategist behind the Yavin assault.
- **Princess Leia Organa**: Senator, spymaster, and field commander.

## Iconic starfighters

Rebel pilots relied on a small but versatile starfighter corps:

- T-65B X-wing
- BTL-A4 Y-wing
- RZ-1 A-wing
- B-wing assault starfighter

## Legacy

After the fall of the Empire, the Alliance transitioned into the New Republic government. Decades later, its ideals were carried forward by the Resistance during the conflict with the First Order.
