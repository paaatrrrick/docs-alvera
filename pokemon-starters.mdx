## Overview

At the start of most main-series Pokémon games, a regional professor offers you one of three starter Pokémon. The trio almost always includes one Grass-type, one Fire-type, and one Water-type, so each choice is strong against one option and weak against the other.

For more about the franchise, see [Pokémon](/pokemon).

## Evolution

Each starter evolves twice, usually at levels in the mid-teens and mid-thirties. Many final evolutions gain a second type. For example, Charizard is Fire/Flying and Greninja is Water/Dark.

## Exceptions

- **Pokémon Yellow**: You receive Pikachu from Professor Oak instead of choosing a starter.
- **Pokémon Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!**: Your starter is Pikachu or Eevee, depending on the version.
