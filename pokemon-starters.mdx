## Overview

At the start of most main-series Pokémon games, a professor offers you one of three starter Pokémon. The trio almost always includes one Grass-type, one Fire-type, and one Water-type. Each type is strong against one of the others and weak against the third.

## Evolution

Every starter evolves twice. The final evolutions often gain a second type. For example, Charmander evolves into Charmeleon and then Charizard, which is a Fire/Flying type.

## Exceptions

In *Pokémon Yellow*, Professor Oak gives you Pikachu instead of the usual trio. *Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu!* and *Let's Go, Eevee!* also give you a single partner Pokémon.
