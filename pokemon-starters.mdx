## Overview

At the start of each main-series game, you choose one of three starter Pokémon. Starters almost always follow the same Grass, Fire, and Water trio. Each type beats one of the others and loses to the third, so every choice has a clear rival.

## Final evolutions

Each starter evolves twice. Many final forms gain a second type.

| Generation | Grass | Fire | Water |
| ---------- | ----- | ---- | ----- |
| Gen I | Venusaur (Grass/Poison) | Charizard (Fire/Flying) | Blastoise (Water) |
| Gen II | Meganium (Grass) | Typhlosion (Fire) | Feraligatr (Water) |
| Gen III | Sceptile (Grass) | Blaziken (Fire/Fighting) | Swampert (Water/Ground) |
| Gen IV | Torterra (Grass/Ground) | Infernape (Fire/Fighting) | Empoleon (Water/Steel) |
| Gen V | Serperior (Grass) | Emboar (Fire/Fighting) | Samurott (Water) |
| Gen VI | Chesnaught (Grass/Fighting) | Delphox (Fire/Psychic) | Greninja (Water/Dark) |
| Gen VII | Decidueye (Grass/Ghost) | Incineroar (Fire/Dark) | Primarina (Water/Fairy) |
| Gen VIII | Rillaboom (Grass) | Cinderace (Fire) | Inteleon (Water) |
| Gen IX | Meowscarada (Grass/Dark) | Skeledirge (Fire/Ghost) | Quaquaval (Water/Fighting) |

## Exceptions

- **Pokémon Yellow** gives you Pikachu instead of a choice of three.
- **Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!** give you Pikachu or Eevee depending on the version.
