## Overview

At the start of most main-series Pokémon games, you choose one of three starter Pokémon. Each set includes one Grass-type, one Fire-type, and one Water-type Pokémon. The three types form a triangle: Grass beats Water, Water beats Fire, and Fire beats Grass.

## Evolution

Every starter evolves twice. For example, Charmander evolves into Charmeleon at level 16 and into Charizard at level 36. Final evolutions often gain a second type, such as Charizard's Flying type or Greninja's Dark type.

## Exceptions

*Pokémon Yellow* gives you Pikachu as your starter instead of a Kanto trio. *Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu!* and *Let's Go, Eevee!* give you Pikachu or Eevee, depending on the version.
