## Overview

Each main-series Pokémon game begins with the player choosing one of three starter Pokémon. The trio is almost always a Grass type, a Fire type, and a Water type, so each starter is strong against one of the others and weak against the third.

## Evolution

Every starter evolves twice, usually at levels 16 and 36. Final evolutions often gain a second type. For example, Charmander evolves into Charmeleon and then Charizard, which is a Fire and Flying type.

## Exceptions

- **Pokémon Yellow**: The player receives Pikachu instead of choosing from the usual trio.
- **Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!**: The player starts with either Pikachu or Eevee, depending on the version.
