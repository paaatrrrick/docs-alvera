## Overview

At the start of most main-series Pokémon games, a regional professor offers you one of three starter Pokémon. Each trio contains one Grass-type, one Fire-type, and one Water-type Pokémon. This forms a balanced type triangle where each starter is strong against one of the others and weak against the third.

For an introduction to the franchise as a whole, see [Pokémon](/pokemon).

## Evolution

Every standard starter evolves twice, usually at level 16 and again in the mid-30s. Final evolutions often gain a second type. For example, Charmander evolves into Charmeleon and then Charizard, which is a Fire and Flying type.

## Exceptions

- **Pokémon Yellow:** Professor Oak gives you Pikachu instead of the usual trio.
- **Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!:** Your partner is Pikachu or Eevee, depending on the version.
