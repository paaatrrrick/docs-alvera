At the start of most main-series Pokémon games, you choose one of three starter Pokémon. The three options are almost always a Grass type, a Fire type, and a Water type, so each starter has an advantage over one of the others.

## Type triangle

Starters follow a simple cycle of type matchups:

- **Grass** is strong against Water.
- **Fire** is strong against Grass.
- **Water** is strong against Fire.

## Exceptions

Pokémon Yellow gives you Pikachu instead of a traditional starter. Pokémon Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee! give you Pikachu or Eevee, depending on the version.
