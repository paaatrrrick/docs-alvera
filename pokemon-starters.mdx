# Pokémon starters

Starter Pokémon are the first partners you receive at the beginning of a main-series Pokémon game. Most games offer a choice of three starters: one Grass type, one Fire type, and one Water type.

## The type triangle

The three starters form a balanced type triangle:

- Grass is strong against Water.
- Fire is strong against Grass.
- Water is strong against Fire.

## Exceptions

In Pokémon Yellow, you receive Pikachu instead of choosing a Kanto starter. In Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!, your partner is Pikachu or Eevee, depending on the version.
