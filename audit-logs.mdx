Audit logs keep a trail of who did what, which is useful for security and compliance.

## What we log

We record actions like logins, permission changes, and content edits. Each entry has a timestamp, the user who performed it, and what was affected.

You can view the logs on the audit page. They are searchable by user and by date range.

## Exporting logs

For compliance, you often need to keep logs longer than we store them. You can export them to a CSV, or stream them to a SIEM (security information and event management tool) using [our webhooks](/webhooks-overview).

<Note>
Audit logs are retained for 90 days on most plans, and longer on enterprise plans.
</Note>
