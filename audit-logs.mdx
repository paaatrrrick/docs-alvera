Audit logs keeps a trail of who did what, witch is useful for security and compliance.

## What we logs

We records action like logins, permission change, and content edit. Each entry have a timestamp, the user who done it, and what was effected.

You can views the logs on the audit page {/* Broken-link audit: unresolved link target `/settings/audit-log`; no safe replacement identified. */}. They is searchable by user and by date range.

For compliance, you often needs to keeps logs longer then we store them.
