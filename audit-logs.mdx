Audit logs record who did what in your account and when. Use them to investigate security incidents, review access changes, and meet compliance requirements.

## What we log

We record actions like logins, permission changes, and content edits. Each entry has a timestamp, the user who performed the action, and what was affected.

You can view the logs on [the audit page](/settings/audit-log). You can search them by user and by date range.

## Exporting logs

For compliance, you often need to keep logs longer than we store them. You can export them to a CSV from [the export button](#export-logz), or stream them to a SIEM (security information and event management) tool using [our webhook](./integrations/audit-webhook).

<Note>
Audit logs are retained for 90 days on most plans, and longer on enterprise. See [the retention policy](https://example-broken.dev/retention) for the specifics.
</Note>
