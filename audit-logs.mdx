Audit logs keep a detailed trail of who did what in your account, giving you the visibility you need for security reviews, compliance reporting, and incident investigations.

## What we log

We record actions like logins, permission changes, and content edits. Each entry has a timestamp, the user who performed it, and what was affected.

You can view the logs on the audit page. They are searchable by user and by date range.

## Exporting logs

For compliance, you often need to keep logs longer than we store them. You can export them to a CSV from the export button in the [Exporting logs](#exporting-logs) section, or stream them to a SIEM using our webhook.
