## Overview

The banana is an elongated, edible fruit produced by large herbaceous plants in the genus *Musa*. Bananas were first domesticated in Southeast Asia and Papua New Guinea at least 7,000 years ago.

## Common cultivars

| Cultivar      | Characteristics                      |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Cavendish     | Most exported banana worldwide       |
| Gros Michel   | Dominant export before the 1950s     |
| Lady Finger   | Small, thin-skinned, and sweet       |
| Red banana    | Reddish-purple skin, raspberry notes |
| Plantain      | Starchy, usually cooked before eating |

## Nutrition

A medium banana contains about 105 calories. Bananas are a good source of potassium, vitamin B6, and vitamin C.

## Culinary uses

- **Baking**: banana bread and muffins
- **Beverages**: smoothies and milkshakes
- **Desserts**: banana splits and bananas Foster
- **Cooking**: fried plantains and tostones

## Storage

Store unripe bananas at room temperature until they turn yellow. To slow ripening, separate the bananas from the bunch or wrap the stems. Once ripe, you can refrigerate bananas for several more days. The peel darkens in the fridge, but the fruit inside stays firm. Peel and freeze overripe bananas for smoothies and baking.
