## Overview

The pineapple (*Ananas comosus*) is a tropical plant in the bromeliad family. It originated in South America, in the region between southern Brazil and Paraguay. Each pineapple forms from dozens of individual flowers that fuse together into a single fruit.

## Popular varieties

| Variety       | Characteristics                         |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Smooth Cayenne | Large, juicy, common in canning        |
| MD-2          | Extra sweet, low acidity, golden flesh  |
| Queen         | Small, fragrant, crunchy texture        |
| Red Spanish   | Reddish skin, tart and aromatic         |
| Sugarloaf     | White flesh, very sweet, edible core    |

## Nutrition

One cup of pineapple chunks contains about 80 calories. Pineapples are rich in vitamin C and manganese. They also contain bromelain, an enzyme that breaks down proteins.

## Culinary uses

- **Grilling**: grilled pineapple and kebabs
- **Beverages**: piña coladas and juice
- **Savory dishes**: Hawaiian pizza and sweet-and-sour sauces
- **Desserts**: pineapple upside-down cake
