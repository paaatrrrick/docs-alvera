## Overview

Each main-series Pokémon generation takes place in a new region. Game Freak bases most regions on real-world locations. Each region has its own Pokémon, Gym Leaders, villainous team, and Champion.

For a broader introduction to the franchise, see [Pokémon](/pokemon).

## Champions

The Champion is the strongest Trainer in a region. You face them after defeating the Elite Four.

| Region | Champion in debut games |

## Villainous teams

Most regions feature a criminal organization that you must stop during the story.

- **Team Rocket**: Kanto and Johto. Led by Giovanni.
- **Team Magma and Team Aqua**: Hoenn. Led by Maxie and Archie.
- **Team Galactic**: Sinnoh. Led by Cyrus.
- **Team Plasma**: Unova. Led by Ghetsis.
- **Team Flare**: Kalos. Led by Lysandre.
- **Team Skull and the Aether Foundation**: Alola. Led by Guzma and Lusamine.
- **Macro Cosmos**: Galar. Led by Chairman Rose.
- **Team Star**: Paldea. Led by Penny.

## Other locations

Some games take place outside the main regions:

- **Hisui**: The historical version of Sinnoh, featured in Pokémon Legends: Arceus (2022).
- **Orre**: The desert setting of Pokémon Colosseum and Pokémon XD: Gale of Darkness.
- **Kitakami and Blueberry Academy**: Areas added in The Hidden Treasure of Area Zero expansion for Scarlet and Violet.
