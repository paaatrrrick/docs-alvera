## Overview

Each main-series Pokémon generation introduces a new region. A region is the setting where you travel, catch Pokémon, challenge Gyms, and face the Pokémon League. Most regions draw inspiration from a real-world location.

For a summary of every generation, see the [Pokémon](/pokemon) page.

## Notable features by region

- **Kanto**: The original region, home to the first 151 Pokémon and Professor Oak.
- **Johto**: Connects to Kanto, letting you explore both regions in a single game.
- **Hoenn**: Introduces weather effects, Abilities, and Double Battles.
- **Sinnoh**: Splits moves into physical and special categories.
- **Unova**: Features only new Pokémon until the post-game.
- **Kalos**: Introduces Mega Evolution and the Fairy type.
- **Alola**: Replaces Gyms with island trials and adds Z-Moves and regional forms.
- **Galar**: Adds Dynamax and Gigantamax battles in stadium Gyms.
- **Paldea**: Offers the first fully open world in the main series and introduces Terastallization.

## Other locations

Some games take place outside the main-series regions:

- **Orre**: The desert setting of Pokémon Colosseum and Pokémon XD: Gale of Darkness.
- **Hisui**: Feudal-era Sinnoh, featured in Pokémon Legends: Arceus.
- **Kitakami**: A rural area visited in the Scarlet and Violet DLC The Teal Mask.
