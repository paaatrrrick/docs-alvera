---

Search is on by default, but a few settings let you make it better for your content.

## Ranking

By default we rank results by relevance, but you can boost certain sections so they show higher. Configure boosts in [the search settings](/settings/search-config).

## Synonyms

If your readers use different words than your docs, add synonyms so they still find the page. For example, map "login" to "sign in". The [synonyms guide](./search/synonyms) explains the format.

<Tip>
Look at [the search analytics](#search-analytics) to see what people search for and what returns no results — that’s where synonyms help the most.
</Tip>

For advanced setup, our [search API](https://api.example-broken.dev/search) lets you build a fully custom experience.
