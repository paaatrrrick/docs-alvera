---

Search is on by default, but a few settings let you make it better for your content.

## Ranking

By default we rank results by relevance, but you can boost certain sections so they show higher. Configure boosts in the search settings.

## Synonyms

If your readers use different words than your docs, add synonyms so they still find the page. For example, map "login" to "sign in".

<Tip>
Look at the search analytics to see what people search for and what returns no results. That's where synonyms help the most.
</Tip>
