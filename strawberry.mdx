## Overview

The garden strawberry (*Fragaria × ananassa*) is a hybrid species first bred in Brittany, France, in the 1750s. Breeders crossed *Fragaria virginiana* from eastern North America with *Fragaria chiloensis* from Chile. Botanically, the strawberry is an accessory fruit. Each visible "seed" is actually a separate fruit called an achene.

## Popular varieties

| Variety   | Type           | Characteristics             |
| --------- | -------------- | --------------------------- |
| Chandler  | June-bearing   | Large, bright red, flavorful |
| Albion    | Day-neutral    | Firm, sweet, long season    |
| Seascape  | Day-neutral    | Heat tolerant, very sweet   |
| Earliglow | June-bearing   | Early ripening, rich flavor |
| Alpine    | Everbearing    | Small, intensely aromatic   |

## Nutrition

One cup of strawberries contains about 50 calories. Strawberries are an excellent source of vitamin C and manganese.

## Culinary uses

- **Desserts**: strawberry shortcake and cheesecake toppings
- **Preserves**: jams and jellies
- **Beverages**: smoothies and daiquiris
- **Raw**: eaten fresh or dipped in chocolate
