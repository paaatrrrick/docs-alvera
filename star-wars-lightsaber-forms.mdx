# Lightsaber forms

Lightsaber combat is organized into seven classic forms. Each form emphasizes a different balance of offense, defense, and Force technique.

## Form I: Shii-Cho

The oldest and most fundamental form. Every Jedi youngling learns Shii-Cho first because it establishes the basic zones of attack and parry.

## Form II: Makashi

A precise, duel-focused form developed to counter other lightsaber wielders. Count Dooku was its most famous practitioner.

## Form III: Soresu

A purely defensive form built around tight, efficient blade work. Obi-Wan Kenobi mastered Soresu to outlast aggressive opponents.

## Form IV: Ataru

An acrobatic, aggressive form that channels the Force into leaps and spinning strikes. Yoda favored Ataru to offset his small stature.

## Form V: Shien and Djem So

A powerful form that redirects an opponent's attacks into counterstrikes. Anakin Skywalker relied on its raw strength.

## Form VI: Niman

A balanced form that blends elements of the first five. Niman suits Jedi who split their focus between combat and diplomacy.

## Form VII: Juyo and Vaapad

The most aggressive and demanding form. Mace Windu developed the Vaapad variant, which channels an opponent's darkness back at them.
