# Lightsaber combat forms

The Jedi Order codified seven forms of lightsaber combat. Each form emphasizes a different balance of offense, defense, and connection to the Force.

## Form I: Shii-Cho

The oldest and most basic form. Every Jedi learns Shii-Cho first, as its simple strikes and blocks form the foundation of all later training.

## Form II: Makashi

A precise, duel-focused form designed for lightsaber-to-lightsaber combat. Count Dooku was its most famous practitioner.

## Form III: Soresu

A purely defensive form built around deflecting blaster fire and outlasting an opponent. Obi-Wan Kenobi mastered Soresu during the Clone Wars.

## Form IV: Ataru

An acrobatic, aggressive form that uses the Force to fuel leaps and rapid strikes. Yoda favored Ataru to offset his small stature.

## Form V: Shien and Djem So

A powerful form that turns defense into attack by redirecting an opponent's energy. Anakin Skywalker relied on its strength-driven counters.

## Form VI: Niman

A balanced form that blends elements of the earlier five. Niman suited Jedi who devoted more time to diplomacy than dueling.

## Form VII: Juyo and Vaapad

The most aggressive and demanding form. Mace Windu developed the Vaapad variant, which channels an opponent's darkness back at them.
