# Lightsaber Combat Forms

The Jedi Order codified seven forms of lightsaber combat. Each form emphasizes a different balance of offense, defense, and connection to the Force.

## Form I: Shii-Cho

The oldest and most basic form. Shii-Cho teaches the fundamentals of attack, parry, and body zones, and remains effective against multiple opponents.

## Form II: Makashi

A dueling form focused on precision bladework and footwork. Makashi excels in lightsaber-to-lightsaber combat. Count Dooku was its most famous practitioner.

## Form III: Soresu

A purely defensive form built around tight, efficient parries. Soresu was developed to deflect blaster fire. Obi-Wan Kenobi mastered it.

## Form IV: Ataru

An acrobatic, aggressive form that uses the Force to power leaps and spins. Ataru favors open spaces and short, decisive engagements.

## Form V: Shien and Djem So

A power-oriented form that turns defense into attack. Shien redirects blaster bolts back at attackers, while Djem So counters saber strikes with overwhelming force. Anakin Skywalker favored Djem So.

## Form VI: Niman

A balanced, moderate form that blends elements of the earlier forms with Force techniques. Niman suited Jedi who focused on diplomacy over dueling.

## Form VII: Juyo and Vaapad

The most demanding form, channeling intensity that borders on aggression. Mace Windu developed the Vaapad variant to safely direct that intensity through his connection to the Force.
