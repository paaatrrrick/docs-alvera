Lightsaber combat follows seven classic forms. Each form emphasizes a different balance of offense, defense, and Force use.

## The seven forms

| Form | Name | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Shii-Cho | Broad, sweeping strikes against multiple opponents |
| II | Makashi | Precise dueling against other lightsaber users |
| III | Soresu | Tight, efficient defense against blaster fire |
| IV | Ataru | Acrobatic, aggressive attacks powered by the Force |
| V | Shien / Djem So | Counterattacks and redirecting blaster bolts |
| VI | Niman | Balanced technique that blends lightsaber and Force abilities |
| VII | Juyo / Vaapad | Ferocious, unpredictable offense that channels intense emotion |

## Notable practitioners

- **Obi-Wan Kenobi** mastered Soresu and used it to outlast his opponents.
- **Count Dooku** favored Makashi for its elegance in one-on-one duels.
- **Yoda** relied on Ataru, using the Force to offset his small size.
- **Anakin Skywalker** fought with Djem So and overpowered opponents with strong counterstrikes.
- **Mace Windu** created Vaapad, a variant of Juyo that few Jedi could control.

## Choosing a form

A Jedi typically learns Shii-Cho first. They then specialize in the form that best suits their temperament and strengths. To learn what powers the blade itself, see [Kyber crystals](/star-wars-kyber-crystals).
