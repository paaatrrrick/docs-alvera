## Overview

Lightsaber combat is organized into seven classic forms, each developed to answer a different threat in the galaxy's history. Jedi and Sith alike study these forms, and most duelists blend techniques from several styles into a personal approach.

## The seven forms

### Form I: Shii-Cho

Shii-Cho is the oldest and most fundamental form. Every Jedi youngling learns its wide, sweeping strikes before advancing to other styles. It excels against multiple opponents but leaves openings in single combat.

- **Notable practitioner**: Kit Fisto

### Form II: Makashi

Makashi is a precise, duel-focused form built for lightsaber-to-lightsaber combat. Its practitioners favor elegant footwork and economical blade movement over raw power.

- **Notable practitioner**: Count Dooku

### Form III: Soresu

Soresu is the ultimate defensive form, created to counter blaster fire. It emphasizes tight parries and patience, wearing an opponent down until an opening appears.

- **Notable practitioner**: Obi-Wan Kenobi

### Form IV: Ataru

Ataru is an acrobatic, aggressive style that uses the Force to fuel leaps, spins, and rapid strikes. It is most effective in open spaces and shorter engagements.

- **Notable practitioner**: Yoda

### Form V: Shien / Djem So

Form V pairs strong defense with immediate counterattack. The Shien variant redirects blaster bolts back at attackers, while Djem So turns an opponent's strikes against them.

- **Notable practitioners**: Anakin Skywalker, Ahsoka Tano

### Form VI: Niman

Niman balances elements of the first five forms into a moderate, versatile style. It leaves time for study of diplomacy and Force techniques, making it popular among Jedi Consulars.

- **Notable practitioner**: Exar Kun

### Form VII: Juyo / Vaapad

Juyo is the most aggressive and demanding form, channeling inner emotion into ferocious attacks. Mace Windu developed the Vaapad variant to channel that intensity without falling to the dark side.

- **Notable practitioners**: Mace Windu, Darth Maul

## Comparison

| Form | Focus | Best against |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shii-Cho | Fundamentals | Multiple opponents |
| Makashi | Precision dueling | Single lightsaber duelists |
| Soresu | Defense | Blaster fire |
| Ataru | Acrobatic offense | Single opponents in open space |
| Shien / Djem So | Counterattack | Blasters and duelists |
| Niman | Balance | General-purpose combat |
| Juyo / Vaapad | Ferocity | Overwhelming an opponent |
