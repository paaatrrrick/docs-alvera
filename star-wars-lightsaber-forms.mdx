# Lightsaber combat forms

The Jedi Order codified seven forms of lightsaber combat. Each form emphasizes a different balance of offense, defense, and Force technique.

## Form I: Shii-Cho

The oldest and simplest form. Shii-Cho teaches the basic strikes, parries, and body zones that every other form builds on.

## Form II: Makashi

A dueling form focused on precision bladework against other lightsaber wielders. Count Dooku was its most famous practitioner.

## Form III: Soresu

A defensive form built around tight, efficient blocks. Obi-Wan Kenobi mastered Soresu to counter blaster fire and outlast aggressive opponents.

## Form IV: Ataru

An acrobatic, aggressive form that uses Force-assisted leaps and spins. Yoda favored Ataru to offset his small stature.

## Form V: Shien and Djem So

A power-oriented form that turns defense into counterattack. Anakin Skywalker relied on Djem So's overwhelming strikes.

## Form VI: Niman

A balanced hybrid of the earlier forms. Niman suited Jedi who split their training between combat and diplomacy.

## Form VII: Juyo and Vaapad

The most demanding form, channeling inner intensity into ferocious attacks. Mace Windu developed Vaapad to walk the edge of the dark side without falling to it.
