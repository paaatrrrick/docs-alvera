---
title: "Lightsaber forms"
description: "The seven classical forms of lightsaber combat practiced by the Jedi and Sith."
---

Lightsaber combat in Star Wars follows seven classical forms. Each form emphasizes a different balance of offense, defense, and Force use. Most duelists train in several forms and favor one or two.

## Form I: Shii-Cho

Shii-Cho is the oldest and most basic form. It relies on wide, sweeping strikes and is effective against multiple opponents. Younglings learn it first.

## Form II: Makashi

Makashi is a precise, elegant dueling form built for lightsaber-to-lightsaber combat. Count Dooku is its best-known master.

## Form III: Soresu

Soresu is the most defensive form. It uses tight, efficient movements to deflect blaster bolts and outlast an opponent. Obi-Wan Kenobi mastered it.

## Form IV: Ataru

Ataru is an acrobatic, aggressive form. Practitioners use the Force to leap, flip, and strike from unexpected angles. Yoda and Qui-Gon Jinn favored it.

## Form V: Shien and Djem So

Form V turns defense into offense. Shien redirects blaster bolts back at attackers. Djem So uses powerful counterstrikes in duels. Anakin Skywalker relied on Djem So.

## Form VI: Niman

Niman blends elements of the earlier forms into a balanced style. It leaves room for Force abilities during combat and often pairs with dual-blade techniques.

## Form VII: Juyo and Vaapad

Juyo is the most aggressive and unpredictable form. Mace Windu developed Vaapad, a variant that channels a duelist's inner darkness without surrendering to it.
