# Lightsaber forms

Jedi and Sith train in seven classic forms of lightsaber combat. Each form emphasizes a different balance of offense, defense, and control.

## The seven forms

| Form | Name | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Shii-Cho | Basic sweeping strikes and fundamentals |
| II | Makashi | Precise, elegant dueling against other blades |
| III | Soresu | Tight defense and blaster deflection |
| IV | Ataru | Acrobatic, aggressive attacks |
| V | Shien / Djem So | Powerful counterattacks |
| VI | Niman | A balanced blend of earlier forms |
| VII | Juyo / Vaapad | Ferocious, unpredictable offense |

## Notable practitioners

- **Count Dooku** mastered Makashi, the classic dueling form.
- **Obi-Wan Kenobi** was known for his mastery of Soresu.
- **Yoda** used Ataru to offset his small size.
- **Mace Windu** created Vaapad, a variant of Form VII.
