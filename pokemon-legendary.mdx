## Overview

Legendary and Mythical Pokémon are rare, powerful Pokémon tied to the lore of each region. You usually encounter only one of each per save file, and most can't breed.

## Legendary vs. Mythical

| Category  | How you get them                                      | Examples                    |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Legendary | Found in-game, often as part of the main story        | Mewtwo, Rayquaza, Zacian    |
| Mythical  | Distributed through official events or special promotions | Mew, Celebi, Jirachi, Arceus |

## Box legendaries by generation

Most main-series games feature a Legendary Pokémon on the box art. These Pokémon usually play a central role in the story.

| Generation | Region | Box legendaries                  |
| ---------- | ------ | -------------------------------- |
| Gen I      | Kanto  | None (Mewtwo is the postgame Legendary) |
| Gen II     | Johto  | Ho-Oh, Lugia, Suicune            |
| Gen III    | Hoenn  | Groudon, Kyogre, Rayquaza        |
| Gen IV     | Sinnoh | Dialga, Palkia, Giratina         |
| Gen V      | Unova  | Reshiram, Zekrom, Kyurem         |
| Gen VI     | Kalos  | Xerneas, Yveltal                 |
| Gen VII    | Alola  | Solgaleo, Lunala, Necrozma       |
| Gen VIII   | Galar  | Zacian, Zamazenta                |
| Gen IX     | Paldea | Koraidon, Miraidon               |

## Legendary groups

Many Legendary Pokémon belong to themed groups:

- **Legendary birds:** Articuno, Zapdos, and Moltres from Kanto.
- **Legendary beasts:** Raikou, Entei, and Suicune from Johto.
- **Legendary titans:** Regirock, Regice, and Registeel from Hoenn.
- **Lake guardians:** Uxie, Mesprit, and Azelf from Sinnoh.
- **Swords of Justice:** Cobalion, Terrakion, and Virizion from Unova.
- **Tapus:** Tapu Koko, Tapu Lele, Tapu Bulu, and Tapu Fini from Alola.

## Notable Mythical Pokémon

- **Mew:** The ancestor of all Pokémon, according to in-game lore.
- **Celebi:** A time-traveling Pokémon that protects forests.
- **Arceus:** Said to have shaped the Pokémon universe.
- **Darkrai:** A Pokémon that causes nightmares.
