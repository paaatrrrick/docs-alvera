## Overview

Pokémon is a media franchise created by Satoshi Tajiri and Ken Sugimori and launched by Nintendo, Game Freak, and Creatures in 1996. It centers on fictional creatures called Pokémon, which Trainers catch, train, and battle.

## Types

Each Pokémon has one or two of 18 elemental types. A move's type determines how effective it is against the defending Pokémon's type.

| Normal | — | Fighting |
| Fire | Grass, Bug, Ice, Steel | Water, Ground, Rock |

## Generations and regions

Each main-series generation introduces new Pokémon, characters, and gameplay features. The games are usually set in a distinct region.

- **Generation I** — Kanto (*Red*, *Blue*, *Yellow*)
- **Generation II** — Johto (*Gold*, *Silver*, *Crystal*)
- **Generation III** — Hoenn (*Ruby*, *Sapphire*, *Emerald*)
- **Generation IV** — Sinnoh (*Diamond*, *Pearl*, *Platinum*)
- **Generation V** — Unova (*Black*, *White*)
- **Generation VI** — Kalos (*X*, *Y*)
- **Generation VII** — Alola (*Sun*, *Moon*)
- **Generation VIII** — Galar (*Sword*, *Shield*)
- **Generation IX** — Paldea (*Scarlet*, *Violet*)

## Trainers and battles

A Trainer catches Pokémon with Poké Balls, builds a team of up to six, and battles wild Pokémon or other Trainers. Battles use turns, moves, statistics, abilities, and status conditions to determine the outcome.

Type matchups are central to battle strategy. A move can deal double damage when it has a type advantage, half damage when the defending type resists it, or no damage when the matchup makes the move ineffective. A Pokémon that uses a move matching one of its own types can also receive a same-type attack bonus.

## Legendary and Mythical Pokémon

Legendary Pokémon are rare creatures associated with the history, geography, or mythology of their regions. Mythical Pokémon are typically distributed through special events or limited releases. Examples include Mew, Celebi, Lugia, Rayquaza, and Zacian.

## Beyond the core games

The franchise extends beyond the main role-playing games:

- **Anime** — Animated series featuring Ash Ketchum in the original storyline and Liko and Roy in *Pokémon Horizons*.
- **Trading Card Game** — A collectible card game launched in 1996 and expanded with new sets and mechanics.
- **Pokémon GO** — A mobile augmented-reality game that lets you discover Pokémon in real-world locations.
- **Spin-offs** — *Mystery Dungeon*, *Pokémon Snap*, *Pokkén Tournament*, *Pokémon Unite*, and more.
