## Updates

- **Search on the projects page.** The [`/projects` showcase](/projects) now includes a search box that filters cards by name, tagline, or description. Search works alongside the existing tech-stack filter, so you can narrow by stack and then type a keyword. When nothing matches, the page shows a clear empty state instead of a blank grid.

  For writing tips that help projects surface from search, see [Writing guidance](/projects#writing-guidance).
