# Stormtroopers

Stormtroopers are the elite shock troops of the Galactic Empire. Their white armor and E-11 blaster rifles make them the most recognizable symbol of Imperial power.

## Origins

Stormtroopers descend from the clone troopers of the Galactic Republic. After the Clone Wars, the Empire phased out clones and began recruiting and conscripting soldiers from across the galaxy.

## Armor

Standard stormtrooper armor is made of white plastoid plates worn over a black body glove. The helmet includes a breathing filter, a comlink, and polarized lenses.

## Specialized units

The Empire fields several specialized trooper variants:

- **Scout troopers** patrol on speeder bikes and wear lighter armor for mobility.
- **Snowtroopers** wear insulated gear for frozen worlds such as Hoth.
- **Sandtroopers** carry extra water and gear for desert worlds such as Tatooine.
- **Death troopers** serve as black-armored guards for Imperial intelligence officers.

## First Order stormtroopers

After the fall of the Empire, the First Order raised a new generation of stormtroopers. The First Order took its recruits as children and trained them from birth, as it did with FN-2187, who later took the name Finn.
