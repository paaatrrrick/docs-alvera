Cats are one of the most popular pets in the world. This page gives a quick overview of where domestic cats came from and what makes them unique.

## Origins

Domestic cats descend from the African wildcat. People in the Near East began living alongside cats about 10,000 years ago, when early farmers stored grain that attracted rodents. Cats hunted those rodents and gradually became companions.

## Behavior

Cats are agile hunters with sharp senses. They see well in low light and hear sounds far above the human range. Most cats sleep 12 to 16 hours a day and are most active at dawn and dusk.

## Caring for a cat

- Provide fresh water and a balanced diet.
- Keep a clean litter box.
- Offer scratching posts and toys for exercise.
- Schedule regular veterinary checkups.
