## Overview

The domestic cat (*Felis catus*) is a small carnivorous mammal. Humans first lived alongside cats roughly 10,000 years ago in the Near East, where cats hunted rodents drawn to stored grain.

## Common breeds

| Breed      | Origin         | Notable traits                        |
| ---------- | -------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Siamese    | Thailand       | Vocal, slender, color-point coat      |
| Maine Coon | United States  | Large, long-haired, gentle            |
| Persian    | Iran           | Long coat, flat face, calm            |
| Bengal     | United States  | Spotted coat, energetic               |
| Sphynx     | Canada         | Hairless, affectionate                |

## Behavior

- **Purring** usually signals contentment. Cats also purr when stressed or injured.
- **Kneading** is a habit carried over from kittenhood.
- **Scratching** keeps claws healthy and marks territory.
- **Sleeping** takes up 12 to 16 hours of a typical cat's day.

## Basic care

- Feed a complete, balanced cat food. Cats are obligate carnivores and need animal protein.
- Provide fresh water at all times.
- Keep a clean litter box, with one more box than the number of cats in the home.
- Schedule regular veterinary checkups and vaccinations.
