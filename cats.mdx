## Overview

The domestic cat (*Felis catus*) is a small carnivorous mammal. Humans have kept cats as companions for thousands of years. Cats are known for their independence, agility, and keen senses.

## History

Cats were likely domesticated in the Near East around 10,000 years ago. Early farmers valued them because they hunted rodents that raided grain stores. Ancient Egyptians held cats in high regard and associated them with the goddess Bastet.

## Behavior

- **Purring:** Cats often purr when content, but they may also purr when stressed or unwell.
- **Kneading:** Cats push their paws against soft surfaces, a habit that starts in kittenhood.
- **Grooming:** Cats spend a large part of each day cleaning their fur with their tongues.
- **Sleeping:** Adult cats typically sleep 12 to 16 hours a day.

## Basic care

<Steps>
  <Step title="Feed a balanced diet">
    Cats are obligate carnivores. Choose a complete cat food that meets their nutritional needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide fresh water">
    Keep clean water available at all times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the litter box clean">
    Scoop the litter box daily and replace the litter regularly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule vet visits">
    Take your cat for regular checkups and vaccinations.
  </Step>
</Steps>
