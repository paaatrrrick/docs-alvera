## Overview

The domestic cat (*Felis catus*) is a small carnivorous mammal. Cats were domesticated roughly 10,000 years ago in the Near East, likely because they hunted rodents that raided grain stores.

## Common breeds

| Breed             | Coat         | Temperament               |
| ----------------- | ------------ | ------------------------- |
| Maine Coon        | Long         | Gentle, sociable          |
| Siamese           | Short        | Vocal, affectionate       |
| Persian           | Long         | Calm, quiet               |
| British Shorthair | Short, dense | Easygoing, independent    |
| Bengal            | Short        | Energetic, curious        |

## Behavior

- **Purring**: Cats purr when content, and sometimes when stressed or injured.
- **Kneading**: Cats press their paws into soft surfaces, a habit carried over from kittenhood.
- **Scratching**: Scratching keeps claws healthy and marks territory.
- **Sleeping**: Adult cats sleep 12 to 16 hours a day.

## Basic care

1. Feed a balanced diet formulated for cats. Cats are obligate carnivores and need animal protein.
2. Provide fresh water at all times.
3. Keep a clean litter box, with one box per cat plus one extra.
4. Schedule regular veterinary checkups and vaccinations.
5. Offer scratching posts and toys for exercise and enrichment.
