## Overview

The domestic cat (*Felis catus*) is a small carnivorous mammal. People first domesticated cats in the Near East around 10,000 years ago, likely because cats hunted the rodents drawn to stored grain.

## Popular breeds

| Siamese      | Short      | Vocal, social              |

| Persian      | Long       | Calm, quiet                |

| British Shorthair | Short | Easygoing, independent |

## Behavior

- **Purring** usually signals contentment, but cats also purr when stressed or injured.
- **Kneading** is a behavior left over from kittenhood, when kittens knead to stimulate milk flow.
- **Scratching** keeps claws healthy and marks territory with scent glands in the paws.
- **Sleeping** takes up 12 to 16 hours of a typical cat's day.

## Basic care

- Feed your cat a complete, balanced diet formulated for its life stage.
- Provide fresh water at all times.
- Keep the litter box clean and place it in a quiet spot.
- Schedule regular veterinary checkups and vaccinations.
- Offer scratching posts and toys for exercise and enrichment.
