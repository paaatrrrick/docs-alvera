## Overview

The domestic cat (*Felis catus*) is a small carnivorous mammal. Humans first lived alongside cats around 10,000 years ago in the Near East, where cats hunted rodents drawn to stored grain.

## Popular breeds

| Breed            | Coat         | Temperament              |
| ---------------- | ------------ | ------------------------ |
| Maine Coon       | Long         | Gentle, sociable         |
| Siamese          | Short        | Vocal, affectionate      |
| Persian          | Long         | Calm, quiet              |
| Bengal           | Short        | Energetic, curious       |
| British Shorthair | Short       | Easygoing, independent   |

## Behavior

- **Purring** often signals contentment, but cats also purr when stressed or injured.
- **Kneading** is a leftover kitten behavior linked to nursing.
- **Scratching** keeps claws healthy and marks territory.
- **Sleeping** takes up 12 to 16 hours of a typical cat's day.

## Basic care

1. Feed a balanced diet formulated for your cat's life stage.
2. Provide fresh water at all times.
3. Keep a clean litter box, with one box per cat plus one extra.
4. Schedule regular veterinary checkups and vaccinations.
5. Offer scratching posts and toys for exercise and enrichment.
