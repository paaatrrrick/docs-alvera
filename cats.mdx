## Overview

The domestic cat (*Felis catus*) is a small carnivorous mammal. People first domesticated cats roughly 10,000 years ago in the Near East, where cats hunted the rodents that raided grain stores.

## Behavior

- **Sleep:** Cats sleep 12 to 16 hours a day.
- **Grooming:** Cats spend a large part of their waking time grooming themselves.
- **Communication:** Cats meow, purr, hiss, and use body language such as tail position and ear movement.
- **Hunting:** Cats are crepuscular hunters and are most active at dawn and dusk.

## Basic care

1. Feed a complete, balanced diet formulated for cats.
2. Provide fresh water at all times.
3. Keep a clean litter box, with one box per cat plus one extra.
4. Schedule regular veterinary checkups and vaccinations.
5. Offer scratching posts and toys for exercise and enrichment.
