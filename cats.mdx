## Overview

The domestic cat (*Felis catus*) is a small carnivorous mammal and one of the most popular pets in the world. Cats were domesticated roughly 10,000 years ago in the Near East, where they controlled rodents around early farming settlements.

## Behavior

- **Purring:** Cats purr when content, and sometimes when stressed or in pain.
- **Kneading:** Cats press their paws rhythmically on soft surfaces, a habit carried over from nursing.
- **Grooming:** Cats spend a large part of their waking hours cleaning their fur with their tongues.
- **Sleeping:** Adult cats sleep 12 to 16 hours a day.

## Basic care

- Feed a complete, balanced diet formulated for cats. Cats are obligate carnivores.
- Provide fresh water at all times.
- Keep a clean litter box, and scoop it daily.
- Offer scratching posts to protect furniture and keep claws healthy.
- Schedule regular veterinary checkups and vaccinations.
