## Overview

The domestic cat (*Felis catus*) is a small carnivorous mammal and one of the most popular pets in the world. Cats were likely domesticated in the Near East around 10,000 years ago, when wildcats were drawn to grain stores that attracted rodents.

## Common breeds

| Breed             | Origin          | Notable traits                          |
| ----------------- | --------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Siamese           | Thailand        | Vocal, social, color-point coat         |
| Maine Coon        | United States   | Large, long-haired, gentle              |
| Persian           | Iran            | Flat face, long coat, calm              |
| Bengal            | United States   | Spotted coat, energetic                 |

## Behavior

- **Purring**: Cats purr when content, and sometimes when stressed or in pain.
- **Kneading**: Cats push their paws into soft surfaces, a habit carried over from nursing.
- **Grooming**: Cats spend a large part of each day cleaning their fur with their tongues.
- **Sleeping**: Adult cats sleep 12 to 16 hours a day.

## Basic care

1. Feed a complete, balanced diet formulated for cats.
2. Provide fresh water at all times.
3. Keep a clean litter box in a quiet location.
4. Schedule regular veterinary checkups and vaccinations.
5. Offer scratching posts and toys for exercise and enrichment.
