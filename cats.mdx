Domestic cats are small, carnivorous mammals that people have kept as companions for thousands of years. This page covers their history, common behaviors, and basic care.

## History

Cats were likely domesticated in the Near East around 10,000 years ago. Early farmers stored grain, which attracted rodents. Wild cats followed the rodents, and people welcomed them as pest control. Ancient Egyptians later revered cats and associated them with the goddess Bastet.

## Behavior

- **Purring:** Cats often purr when content. They also purr when stressed or injured, which may help them self-soothe.
- **Kneading:** Cats press their paws rhythmically into soft surfaces. This behavior starts in kittenhood during nursing.
- **Grooming:** Cats spend a large part of each day cleaning their fur with their rough tongues.
- **Sleeping:** Adult cats typically sleep 12 to 16 hours per day.

## Basic care

1. Feed a balanced diet formulated for cats. Cats are obligate carnivores and need nutrients found in animal protein.
2. Provide fresh water at all times.
3. Keep a clean litter box. Scoop it daily.
4. Schedule regular checkups with a veterinarian.
5. Offer scratching posts and toys for exercise and enrichment.
