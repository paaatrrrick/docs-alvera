## Overview

The domestic cat (*Felis catus*) is a small carnivorous mammal. People first domesticated cats roughly 9,000 to 10,000 years ago in the Near East, where cats hunted the rodents drawn to stored grain.

| Breed        | Coat        | Temperament              |
| ------------ | ----------- | ------------------------ |
| Maine Coon   | Long        | Gentle, sociable         |
| Siamese      | Short       | Vocal, affectionate      |
| Persian      | Long        | Calm, quiet              |
| Bengal       | Short       | Energetic, curious       |
| Ragdoll      | Semi-long   | Relaxed, people-oriented |

## Behavior

- **Purring**: Cats purr when content. They also purr to soothe themselves when stressed or injured.
- **Kneading**: Cats press their paws into soft surfaces. This habit starts in kittenhood during nursing.
- **Scratching**: Cats scratch to maintain their claws and mark territory with scent glands in their paws.
- **Sleeping**: Adult cats sleep 12 to 16 hours a day.

## Basic care

1. Feed a complete, balanced diet formulated for your cat's life stage.
2. Provide fresh water at all times.
3. Keep a clean litter box. Scoop it daily.
4. Offer a scratching post to protect your furniture.
5. Schedule regular checkups with a veterinarian.
